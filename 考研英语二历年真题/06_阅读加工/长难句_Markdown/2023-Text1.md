# 2023 年考研英语二阅读 Text 1 · 长难句

## E2-2023-T1-P1-S4

The Royal Horticultural Society (RHS), which runs the annual show in west London, says it has introduced the ban because of the damage plastic grass does to the environment and biodiversity.

**译文：** 在伦敦西部主办这一年度花展的英国皇家园艺学会表示，之所以实施这项禁令，是因为塑料草皮会损害环境和生物多样性。

**主干：** S+V+O：The RHS says it has introduced the ban（英国皇家园艺学会表示已实施禁令）

**逻辑：** which 从句补充学会身份；says 后给出其决定；because of 说明禁令缘由，其中 damage 后省略关系代词的定语从句指出受害对象。

**结构：**

- 主句：The Royal Horticultural Society (RHS) says
-   ├ 非限制性定语从句(which)：which runs the annual show in west London
-   └ 宾语从句：it has introduced the ban
-       └ 原因状语(because of)：because of the damage
-           └ 定语从句：plastic grass does to the environment and biodiversity〔修饰 damage〕

**考点：**

- run the show 意为主办、运营花展。
- do damage to 是固定搭配，表示对……造成损害。
- biodiversity 指生物多样性。

## E2-2023-T1-P1-S9

It is trying to encourage people to sign two petitions, one calling for a ban on the sale of plastic grass and another calling for an "ecological damage" tax on such lawns.

**译文：** 该账号正鼓励人们签署两份请愿书：一份呼吁禁止销售塑料草皮，另一份呼吁对这类草坪征收“生态损害”税。

**主干：** S+V+O：It is trying to encourage people to sign two petitions（该账号正鼓励人们签署两份请愿书）

**逻辑：** 主句先给出行动目标；逗号后用 one...another...分别说明两份请愿书的诉求，两个 calling 均是现在分词后置修饰 petition。

**结构：**

- 主句：It is trying to encourage people to sign two petitions
- 同位解释1：one calling for a ban on the sale of plastic grass
- 并列同位解释2：another calling for an "ecological damage" tax on such lawns

**考点：**

- It 指上文的 Twitter account。
- call for 意为呼吁、要求。
- one...another... 对应“两者中的一份……另一份……”。

## E2-2023-T1-P1-S16

However, the use of artificial grass must comply with the legal and policy safeguards in place to protect biodiversity and ensure sustainable drainage, while measures such as the strengthened biodiversity duty should serve to encourage public authorities to consider sustainable alternatives."

**译文：** 不过，人造草的使用必须遵守现行的法律和政策保障措施，以保护生物多样性并确保可持续排水；同时，强化生物多样性职责等措施应能促使公共机构考虑可持续的替代方案。”

**主干：** S+V；S+V：The use must comply..., while measures should serve...（人造草使用必须合规，而相关措施应起到促进作用）

**逻辑：** 前半句规定使用人造草的合规底线；while 连接另一层政策引导，强调措施还应促使公共机构主动选择可持续方案。

**结构：**

- 分句1：the use of artificial grass must comply with the legal and policy safeguards
-   ├ 过去分词短语：in place〔修饰 safeguards〕
-   └ 目的状语(to)：to protect biodiversity and ensure sustainable drainage
- 分句2(while)：while measures such as the strengthened biodiversity duty should serve
-   └ 不定式：to encourage public authorities to consider sustainable alternatives

**考点：**

- safeguards in place 指已经生效或设置好的保障措施。
- serve to do 表示“起到……作用”。
- sustainable drainage 指减少积水和径流风险的可持续排水。
