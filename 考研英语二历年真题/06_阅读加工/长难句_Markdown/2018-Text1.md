# 2018 年考研英语二阅读 Text 1 · 长难句

## E2-2018-T1-P1-S4

When did it become accepted wisdom that students should be able to name the 13th president of the United States but be utterly overwhelmed by a broken bike chain?

**译文：** 学生能说出美国第13任总统的名字，却面对一根断掉的自行车链条便完全不知所措——这种看法从何时起成了公认的道理？

**主干：** 疑问主干：When did it become accepted wisdom that…?（何时……成了公认的道理？）

**逻辑：** it作形式主语或泛指情形，that从句给出所谓“公认道理”的具体内容；but并列强烈反差，构成反问。

**结构：**

- 特殊疑问句：When did it become accepted wisdom
-   └ 同位/内容从句(that)：that students should be able to name the 13th president of the United States but be utterly overwhelmed by a broken bike chain
-       ├ 并列谓语①：should be able to name…
-       └ 并列谓语②(but)：〔should〕be utterly overwhelmed by…

**考点：**

- accepted wisdom指“普遍接受的看法、公认道理”。
- but后省略与前面共享的should。
- be overwhelmed by在此表示“面对……完全不知所措”。

## E2-2018-T1-P1-S16

But the headlong push into bachelor's degrees for all - and the subtle devaluing of anything less - misses an important point: That's not the only thing the American economy needs.

**译文：** 但是，所有人一窝蜂地追求学士学位，并暗中贬低低于学士学位的任何选择，却忽略了一个要点：美国经济需要的并不只有学士学位。

**主干：** 主句：the push…and the devaluing…miss an important point（对学位的盲目追逐和对其他选择的贬低忽略了一个要点）

**逻辑：** 两个并列名词短语共同指出教育取向的偏差；冒号后的句子解释被忽略的要点，即经济需求并非只有学士学位。

**结构：**

- 并列主语①：the headlong push into bachelor's degrees for all
- 并列主语②：and the subtle devaluing of anything less
- 谓语宾语：misses an important point
- 冒号后解释point：That's not the only thing
-   └ 定语从句〔省略that，修饰 thing〕：the American economy needs

**考点：**

- headlong表示“轻率而迅猛的、不顾一切的”。
- anything less指低于学士学位的教育或资格。
- the only thing the economy needs中省略了关系代词that。

## E2-2018-T1-P1-S20

In other words, at a time when the working class has turned the country on its political head, frustrated that the opportunity that once defined America is vanishing, one obvious solution is staring us in the face.

**译文：** 换言之，在工人阶层因曾经定义美国的机会正在消失而感到挫败、进而彻底改变国家政治格局之际，一个显而易见的解决方案就摆在我们面前。

**主干：** 主句：one obvious solution is staring us in the face（一个显而易见的解决方案就摆在眼前）

**逻辑：** at a time when交代政治背景，frustrated进一步解释工人阶层改变政治格局的原因，最后主句强调解决方案其实显而易见。

**结构：**

- 衔接语：In other words
- 时间状语从句：at a time when the working class has turned the country on its political head
-   ├ 定语从句(when，修饰 a time)：when the working class has turned the country on its political head
-   └ 过去分词短语补充working class：frustrated that the opportunity…is vanishing
-       └ 定语从句(that，修饰 opportunity)：that once defined America
- 主句：one obvious solution is staring us in the face

**考点：**

- turn something on its head意为“彻底颠覆、完全改变”。
- frustrated that…是过去分词短语，补充说明working class的状态。
- stare sb in the face是习语，指“明摆在某人眼前”。
