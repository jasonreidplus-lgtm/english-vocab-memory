# 2022 年考研英语二阅读 Text 3 · 长难句

## E2-2022-T3-P1-S5

First coined in 2010 by user experience expert Harry Brignull, "dark patterns" is a catch-all term for practices that manipulate user interfaces to influence the decision-making ability of users.

**译文：** “暗黑模式”一词最早由用户体验专家哈里·布里格努尔于2010年提出，是对一类做法的统称：它们操纵用户界面，从而影响用户作出决定的能力。

**主干：** S+V+C："Dark patterns" is a catch-all term（“暗黑模式”是一个统称）

**逻辑：** 句首过去分词补充术语的提出背景；主句定义概念；that 从句说明这些 practices 做什么，不定式进一步说明操纵界面的目的。

**结构：**

- 过去分词状语：First coined in 2010 by user experience expert Harry Brignull
- 主句："dark patterns" is a catch-all term for practices
-   └ 定语从句(that)：that manipulate user interfaces
-       └ 目的状语(to)：to influence the decision-making ability of users

**考点：**

- coin 作动词表示“创造、首创（新词）”。
- catch-all term 指覆盖多种现象的概括性名称。
- decision-making ability 是复合名词，指作出决定的能力。

## E2-2022-T3-P1-S11

Businesses should engage in conversations with IT, compliance, risk, and legal teams to review their privacy policy, and include in the discussion the customer/user experience designers and coders responsible for the company's user interface, as well as the marketers and advertisers responsible for sign-ups, checkout baskets, pricing, and promotions.

**译文：** 企业应让信息技术、合规、风险和法务团队展开讨论，以审查其隐私政策；参与讨论的还应包括负责公司用户界面的客户/用户体验设计师和程序员，以及负责注册、结算购物篮、定价和促销的营销人员与广告人员。

**主干：** S+V：Businesses should engage..., and include...（企业应展开讨论，并让相关人员参与）

**逻辑：** should 同时支配 engage 与 include 两个动作；后半句宾语很长，被两个 responsible for 短语分别限定。识别两个并列谓语和两组人员即可拆开长句。

**结构：**

- 谓语1：should engage in conversations with IT, compliance, risk, and legal teams
-   └ 目的状语(to)：to review their privacy policy
- 并列谓语2(and)：include in the discussion the customer/user experience designers and coders
-   ├ 形容词短语：responsible for the company's user interface
-   └ 并列宾语(as well as)：the marketers and advertisers
-       └ 形容词短语：responsible for sign-ups, checkout baskets, pricing, and promotions

**考点：**

- compliance 在企业语境中指合规职能。
- include in the discussion... 是宾语后移结构。
- as well as 在此补充第二组参与者。

## E2-2022-T3-P1-S17

Dark patterns also can be addressed on a self-regulatory basis, but only if organizations hold themselves accountable, not just to legal requirements but also to industry best practices and standards.

**译文：** 暗黑模式也可以通过行业自律加以治理，但前提是组织不仅对法律要求负责，也对行业最佳实践和标准负责。

**主干：** S+V：Dark patterns can be addressed, but only if organizations hold themselves accountable（暗黑模式可以治理，但前提是组织自我负责）

**逻辑：** 主句提出行业自律这一途径；but only if 强化必要条件；not just...but also...把责任范围从最低法律要求扩展到更高的行业实践和标准。

**结构：**

- 主句：Dark patterns also can be addressed on a self-regulatory basis
- 限制条件(but only if)：but only if organizations hold themselves accountable
-   └ 相关对象并列：not just to legal requirements but also to industry best practices and standards

**考点：**

- on a self-regulatory basis 意为“以行业自律方式”。
- hold oneself accountable 表示主动承担责任、接受问责。
- not just A but also B 是递进并列，不应漏译 B。
