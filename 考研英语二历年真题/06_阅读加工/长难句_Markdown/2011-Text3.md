# 2011 年考研英语二阅读 Text 3 · 长难句

## E2-2011-T3-P1-S5

The phrase "less is more" was actually first popularized by a German, the architect Ludwig Mies van der Rohe, who like other people associated with the Bauhaus, a school of design, emigrated to the United States before World War II and took up posts at American architecture schools.

**译文：** “少即是多”这句话其实最先由一位德国人推广开来，他就是建筑师路德维希·密斯·凡德罗；与包豪斯设计学院的其他相关人士一样，他在二战前移居美国，并在美国建筑院校任职。

**主干：** S+V：The phrase was first popularized by a German（这句话最先由一位德国人推广）

**逻辑：** 主句被动说明名言的推广者；German 后以同位语点名密斯，再由 who 从句补充其移民和任教经历。

**结构：**

- 主句：The phrase "less is more" was actually first popularized by a German
-   └ 同位语：the architect Ludwig Mies van der Rohe
-       └ 非限制性定语从句〔who，修饰 Mies〕：who ... emigrated to the United States ... and took up posts at American architecture schools
-           ├ 比较/伴随插入语：like other people associated with the Bauhaus
-           │   ├ 过去分词短语〔修饰 people〕：associated with the Bauhaus
-           │   └ the Bauhaus 的同位语：a school of design
-           └ 并列谓语：emigrated ... and took up posts ...

**考点：**

- popularize 指“使普及、推广开来”。
- like other people... 是插入比较，不是 who 的谓语。
- take up posts 指就任职位。

## E2-2011-T3-P1-S11

The apartments in the elegant towers Mies built on Chicago's Lake Shore Drive, for example, were smaller - two-bedroom units under 1,000 square feet - than those in their older neighbors along the city's Gold Coast.

**译文：** 例如，密斯在芝加哥湖滨大道建造的优雅高楼中，公寓面积比该市黄金海岸沿线那些年代更久的邻近公寓还小，其中两居室不到1000平方英尺。

**主干：** S+V+C：The apartments were smaller than those（这些公寓比那些公寓更小）

**逻辑：** 比较主干是 apartments were smaller than those；those 替代 apartments，避免重复；两个破折号间补充说明其面积。

**结构：**

- 主句：The apartments ... were smaller ... than those in their older neighbors along the city's Gold Coast
-   ├ 地点定语：in the elegant towers
-   │   └ 定语从句〔省略 that，修饰 towers〕：Mies built on Chicago's Lake Shore Drive
-   ├ 插入语：for example
-   └ 破折号同位说明：two-bedroom units under 1,000 square feet

**考点：**

- Mies built... 是省略关系代词的定语从句，不能把 Mies 当主句主语。
- those 指 older neighboring buildings 中的公寓，而不是建筑本身。
- under 1,000 square feet 表示不足1000平方英尺。

## E2-2011-T3-P1-S17

In his Case Study House, Ralph Rapson may have mispredicted just how the mechanical revolution would impact everyday life - few American families acquired helicopters, though most eventually got clothes dryers - but his belief that self-sufficiency was both desirable and inevitable was widely shared.

**译文：** 在自己的案例研究住宅中，拉尔夫·拉普森也许误判了机械革命将如何影响日常生活——很少有美国家庭购置直升机，尽管大多数家庭最终都有了干衣机——但他关于自给自足既值得追求又不可避免的信念，当时得到了广泛认同。

**主干：** 转折主干：Ralph Rapson may have mispredicted..., but his belief was widely shared（拉普森或许误判了……，但他的信念广受认同）

**逻辑：** may have done 对过去判断作保留性批评；破折号用现实结果举证；but 转向作者真正肯定的信念内容。

**结构：**

- 分句①：Ralph Rapson may have mispredicted
-   └ 宾语从句(how)：how the mechanical revolution would impact everyday life
-   └ 破折号插入例证：few American families acquired helicopters, though most eventually got clothes dryers
- 转折分句②(but)：his belief ... was widely shared
-   └ 同位语从句(that)〔说明 belief〕：that self-sufficiency was both desirable and inevitable

**考点：**

- mispredict how... 指对……将如何发展作出错误预测。
- though 在插入例证内部形成“直升机少见—干衣机普及”的让步对照。
- both desirable and inevitable：既值得追求，又不可避免。
