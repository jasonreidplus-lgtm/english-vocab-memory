# 2025 年考研英语二阅读 Text 2 · 长难句

## E2-2025-T2-P1-S4

With more than 6.8 million on waitlists, healthcare is becoming increasingly inaccessible for those who cannot opt to pay for private treatment; and the cost of providing healthcare is increasingly squeezing our investment in other public services.

**译文：** 候诊名单上已有超过680万人，无法选择自费接受私立医疗的人越来越难获得医疗服务；与此同时，医疗成本也日益挤压我们对其他公共服务的投入。

**主干：** Healthcare is becoming inaccessible; the cost is squeezing investment（医疗服务愈发难以获得，成本正挤压其他投入）

**逻辑：** With先给出候诊规模，分号两侧再并列说明可及性下降和公共资金被挤压两种后果；who限定无法购买私立医疗的人群。

**结构：**

- with复合背景：With more than 6.8 million on waitlists
- 并列分句一：healthcare is becoming increasingly inaccessible for those
-   └ 定语从句(who)：who cannot opt to pay for private treatment
- 并列分句二：and the cost of providing healthcare is increasingly squeezing our investment in other public services

**考点：**

- waitlist 指等待接受医疗服务的名单。
- opt to do 意为“选择做某事”。
- squeeze investment 指成本压力压缩可用于其他领域的投资。

## E2-2025-T2-P1-S12

Together, we are calling for a much more open and honest conversation about the future of health in the UK, and an "urgent rethink" of the hospital-centric model we retain.

**译文：** 我们共同呼吁，就英国医疗的未来展开一场更加公开、坦诚的讨论，并"紧急重新思考"我们仍在沿用的以医院为中心的模式。

**主干：** We are calling for a conversation and an urgent rethink（我们呼吁开展讨论并紧急反思）

**逻辑：** call for后并列两个名词中心：conversation和rethink；末尾省略关系代词的从句说明需要反思的是英国仍在保留的模式。

**结构：**

- 主句：Together, we are calling for a much more open and honest conversation ... and an urgent rethink ...
-   ├ 并列宾语一：a ... conversation about the future of health in the UK
-   └ 并列宾语二：an urgent rethink of the hospital-centric model
-       └ 定语从句〔省略that，修饰 model〕：we retain

**考点：**

- call for 意为“呼吁、要求”。
- hospital-centric 指“以医院为中心的”。
- retain 在此意为“继续保留、沿用”。

## E2-2025-T2-P1-S18

What health functions should remain at the centre, and what should be given to local leaders, often responsible for services that create health, and with a much better understanding of the needs of their populations?

**译文：** 哪些医疗职能应保留在中央，哪些应交给地方领导者？这些领导者往往负责那些能够促进健康的服务，也更加了解当地居民的需求。

**主干：** What functions should remain, and what should be given to local leaders?（哪些职能应保留，哪些应交给地方领导者？）

**逻辑：** 两个what疑问分句对照中央和地方的职能分配；后面的responsible和with结构补充说明地方领导者更适合承接部分职能的理由。

**结构：**

- 并列疑问分句一：What health functions should remain at the centre
- 并列疑问分句二：and what should be given to local leaders
-   ├ 分词短语〔补充 local leaders〕：often responsible for services
-   │   └ 定语从句(that)：that create health
-   └ 并列补充：and with a much better understanding of the needs of their populations

**考点：**

- at the centre 在本文语境中指中央层级。
- services that create health 指能改善健康决定因素的公共服务。
- their populations 指各地方领导者所服务的当地居民。
