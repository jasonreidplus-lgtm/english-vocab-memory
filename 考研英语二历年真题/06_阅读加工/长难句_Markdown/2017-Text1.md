# 2017 年考研英语二阅读 Text 1 · 长难句

## E2-2017-T1-P1-S7

Planning documents pledged that the great legacy of the Games would be to lever a nation of sport lovers away from their couches.

**译文：** 规划文件承诺，奥运会留下的重要遗产将是把一个热爱体育的民族从沙发上拉起来。

**主干：** 主句：Planning documents pledged that the great legacy would be to lever a nation…away from their couches（规划文件承诺，重要遗产将是让全民离开沙发去运动）

**逻辑：** pledged后接that从句说明奥运规划的承诺；从句用不定式定义所谓“重要遗产”的具体内容。

**结构：**

- 主句：Planning documents pledged
-   └ 宾语从句(that)：that the great legacy of the Games would be to lever a nation of sport lovers away from their couches
-       ├ 主语：the great legacy of the Games
-       └ 表语不定式：to lever a nation of sport lovers away from their couches

**考点：**

- lever在此作动词，意为“撬动、促使”。
- lever A away from B可理解为“把A从B处拉开”。
- a nation of sport lovers是集合性表达，指整个热爱体育的民族。

## E2-2017-T1-P1-S18

There is as much joy over a puffed-out first-timer being clapped over the line as there is about top talent shining.

**译文：** 人们为气喘吁吁的初跑者鼓掌越过终点线所感到的喜悦，丝毫不亚于看到顶尖选手大放异彩。

**主干：** 比较结构：There is as much joy over A as there is about B（人们为A感到的喜悦与为B感到的一样多）

**逻辑：** as much…as…把初跑者完赛与高手出彩带来的喜悦等量比较，突出活动不只重视精英成绩。

**结构：**

- 主句前半：There is as much joy over a puffed-out first-timer being clapped over the line
-   └ 动名词复合结构：a puffed-out first-timer being clapped over the line
- 比较从句(as)：as there is about top talent shining
-   └ 动名词复合结构：top talent shining

**考点：**

- puffed-out意为“上气不接下气的”。
- clap sb over the line指“鼓掌为某人加油，直到其越过终点”。
- being clapped是被动动名词，first-timer是其逻辑主语。

## E2-2017-T1-P1-S22

If there is a role for government, it should really be getting involved in providing common goods - making sure there is space for playing fields and the money to pave tennis and netball courts, and encouraging the provision of all these activities in schools.

**译文：** 如果政府确有角色，那就应当切实参与公共资源的供给——确保有运动场地和铺设网球场、无挡板篮球场的资金，并鼓励学校开展所有这些活动。

**主干：** 条件句主干：If there is a role for government, it should be getting involved in providing common goods（如果政府有角色，就应参与提供公共资源）

**逻辑：** if先限定政府介入的前提与边界；破折号后的两个并列动名词说明政府应提供哪些基础条件。

**结构：**

- 条件状语从句(if)：If there is a role for government
- 主句：it should really be getting involved in providing common goods
- 破折号后具体解释providing common goods：
-   ├ making sure there is space for playing fields and the money to pave tennis and netball courts
-   └ and encouraging the provision of all these activities in schools

**考点：**

- common goods在此指公共资源或公共产品。
- make sure后接两个由and连接的存在句成分：space与money。
- the money to pave…中的不定式修饰money，说明资金用途。
