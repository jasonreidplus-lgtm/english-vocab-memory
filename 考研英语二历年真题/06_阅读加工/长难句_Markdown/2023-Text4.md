# 2023 年考研英语二阅读 Text 4 · 长难句

## E2-2023-T4-P1-S4

In adolescence, helpless and dependent children who have relied on grown-ups for just about everything become independent people who can take care of themselves and help each other.

**译文：** 进入青春期后，原本无助、依赖成人处理几乎一切事务的儿童，会变成能够照顾自己并互相帮助的独立个体。

**主干：** S+V+C：Children become independent people（儿童会变成独立的人）

**逻辑：** 两个 who 从句分别刻画变化前后的儿童：此前几乎事事依赖成人，之后能够自理并互助；主干 become 把两种状态连接起来。

**结构：**

- 时间状语：In adolescence
- 主语：helpless and dependent children
-   └ 定语从句(who)：who have relied on grown-ups for just about everything
- 谓语与表语：become independent people
-   └ 定语从句(who)：who can take care of themselves and help each other

**考点：**

- grown-ups 指成年人。
- just about everything 意为几乎所有事情。
- take care of themselves 在此指独立照顾自己。

## E2-2023-T4-P1-S11

The participants filled out questionnaires about how often they did things that were altruistic and positive, like sacrificing their own interests to help a friend, or rebellious and negative, like getting drunk or staying out late.

**译文：** 参与者填写问卷，报告自己多常做利他而积极的事情，例如牺牲自身利益帮助朋友；或做叛逆而消极的事情，例如醉酒或深夜不归。

**主干：** S+V+O：The participants filled out questionnaires（参与者填写问卷）

**逻辑：** how often 从句说明问卷内容；后面用 or 对举积极与消极行为，两边各用 like 举例，第二边省略重复的 things that were。

**结构：**

- 主句：The participants filled out questionnaires
-   └ 介词宾语从句：about how often they did things
-       ├ 定语从句1(that)：that were altruistic and positive
-       │   └ 举例：like sacrificing their own interests to help a friend
-       └ 并列省略：or [things that were] rebellious and negative
-           └ 举例：like getting drunk or staying out late

**考点：**

- fill out a questionnaire 表示填写问卷。
- altruistic 指利他的。
- sacrifice A to do B 表示为做B而牺牲A。

## E2-2023-T4-P1-S23

Reward sensitivity, like prosocial behavior and risk-taking, seems to go up in adolescence and then down again as we age.

**译文：** 奖赏敏感性与亲社会行为和冒险行为一样，似乎会在青春期上升，随后又随着年龄增长而下降。

**主干：** S+V：Reward sensitivity seems to go up and then down（奖赏敏感性似乎先上升后下降）

**逻辑：** like 插入语把奖赏敏感性与另外两种行为联系起来；go up...and then down...呈现随年龄变化的倒U形轨迹。

**结构：**

- 主语：Reward sensitivity
- 插入比较：like prosocial behavior and risk-taking
- 谓语：seems to go up in adolescence
- 并列变化(and then)：and then down again as we age
-   └ 时间从句(as)：as we age

**考点：**

- risk-taking 指冒险行为。
- down 前省略与 go up 平行的 go。
- as we age 意为随着年龄增长。
