# 2021 年考研英语二阅读 Text 2 · 长难句

## E2-2021-T2-P1-S1

With the global population predicted to hit close to 10 billion by 2050, and forecasts that agricultural production in some regions will need to nearly double to keep pace, food security is increasingly making headlines.

**译文：** 据预测，到2050年全球人口将接近100亿；另有预测称，一些地区的农业产量需要近乎翻倍才能跟上需求，因此粮食安全正越来越多地成为新闻焦点。

**主干：** 独立主格背景 + 主句：food security is increasingly making headlines（在两项人口与产量预测背景下，粮食安全日益成为焦点）

**逻辑：** with 复合结构和 forecasts 并列交代人口、产量压力，主句给出这些压力带来的公共议题变化。

**结构：**

- with 复合结构①：With the global population predicted to hit close to 10 billion by 2050
- 并列名词背景②：and forecasts
-   └ 同位语从句(that)：that agricultural production in some regions will need to nearly double to keep pace
- 主句：food security is increasingly making headlines

**考点：**

- population predicted to hit... 是“宾语+过去分词”结构。
- keep pace：跟上需求或发展速度。
- make headlines：成为新闻焦点。

## E2-2021-T2-P1-S10

To become much more self-sufficient, the UK would need to drastically reduce its consumption of animal foods, and probably also farm more intensively - meaning fewer green fields and more factory-style production.

**译文：** 要大幅提高自给程度，英国需要显著减少动物性食品消费，并可能还要采用更集约化的农业，这意味着更少的绿色田野和更多工厂化生产。

**主干：** S+V+O：the UK would need to reduce consumption and farm more intensively（英国需要减少消费并提高集约化程度）

**逻辑：** 句首说明目标，主句列出实现目标的两项代价；meaning 分词短语把“更集约”具体化为景观和生产方式变化。

**结构：**

- 目的不定式：To become much more self-sufficient
- 主句：the UK would need
-   ├ 不定式①：to drastically reduce its consumption of animal foods
-   └ 并列不定式②：(to) probably also farm more intensively
-       └ 破折号解释：meaning fewer green fields and more factory-style production

**考点：**

- self-sufficient：自给自足的。
- farm intensively：采用集约化方式耕作。
- factory-style production：工厂化生产。

## E2-2021-T2-P1-S14

Even if we converted all the suitable land to fields of fruit and veg - which would involve taking out all the nature reserves and removing thousands of people from their homes - we would achieve only a 30 per cent boost in crop production.

**译文：** 即便把所有适宜土地都改造成果蔬田——这将涉及取消全部自然保护区，并让数千人搬离家园——作物产量也只能提高30%。

**主干：** 让步条件复句：Even if we converted all suitable land..., we would achieve only a 30% boost（即便改造全部适宜土地，也只能增产30%）

**逻辑：** Even if 构造最极端假设，which 补充其巨大社会和生态代价，主句用 only 强调收益仍然有限。

**结构：**

- 让步条件从句(Even if)：we converted all the suitable land to fields of fruit and veg
-   └ 非限制性定语从句(which，指前述做法)：which would involve
-       ├ 动名词①：taking out all the nature reserves
-       └ 并列动名词②：removing thousands of people from their homes
- 主句：we would achieve only a 30 per cent boost in crop production

**考点：**

- convert land to fields：把土地改造成田地。
- take out nature reserves：取消或占用自然保护区。
- a boost in production：产量增长。
