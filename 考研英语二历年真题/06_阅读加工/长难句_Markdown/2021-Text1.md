# 2021 年考研英语二阅读 Text 1 · 长难句

## E2-2021-T1-P1-S1

"Reskilling" is something that sounds like a buzzword but is actually a requirement if we plan to have a future where a lot of would-be workers do not get left behind.

**译文：** “技能再培训”听起来像个流行术语，但如果我们希望未来许多潜在劳动者不被落下，它实际上是一项必要举措。

**主干：** S+系动词+表语：Reskilling is something but is actually a requirement（技能再培训看似某物，实则是一项必要举措）

**逻辑：** but 把表面印象和实际必要性对照；if 给出必要性的前提，where 从句具体描述所期望的未来。

**结构：**

- 主语：Reskilling
-   ├ 系表结构①：is something
-   │   └ 定语从句(that)：that sounds like a buzzword
-   └ 转折并列系表结构②：but is actually a requirement
-       └ 条件状语从句(if)：if we plan to have a future
-           └ 定语从句(where)：where a lot of would-be workers do not get left behind

**考点：**

- buzzword：流行术语、时髦说法。
- would-be workers：潜在劳动者。
- get left behind：被落下、无法跟上变化。

## E2-2021-T1-P1-S11

Efforts in Canada and elsewhere have been arguably languid at best, and have given us a situation where we frequently hear of employers begging for workers, even at times and in regions where unemployment is high.

**译文：** 加拿大及其他地区的相关努力充其量只能说进展迟缓，这造成了一种局面：我们经常听到雇主求着找工人，即便有些时候、有些地区的失业率很高。

**主干：** 并列谓语：Efforts have been languid and have given us a situation（相关努力迟缓，并造成一种局面）

**逻辑：** 两个并列谓语先评价政策努力，再说明其后果；两个 where 分别限定“局面”和“地区”，构成高失业与缺工并存的反差。

**结构：**

- 主语：Efforts in Canada and elsewhere
-   ├ 谓语①：have been arguably languid at best
-   └ 并列谓语②：have given us a situation
-       └ 定语从句(where)：where we frequently hear of employers begging for workers
-           └ 让步状语：even at times and in regions where unemployment is high

**考点：**

- languid at best：充其量只能说进展迟缓。
- hear of somebody doing：听说某人正在做某事。
- beg for workers：迫切寻找工人。

## E2-2021-T1-P1-S18

That seems to be the case in Sweden: When forced to furlough 90 per cent of their cabin staff, Scandinavian Airlines decided to start up a short retraining program that reskilled the laid-off workers to support hospital staff.

**译文：** 瑞典的情况似乎正是如此：斯堪的纳维亚航空公司被迫让90%的客舱员工暂时停职时，决定启动一项短期再培训计划，把这些被停职人员培训成医院辅助人员。

**主干：** That seems to be the case: Scandinavian Airlines decided to start a program（情况似乎正是如此：航空公司决定启动计划）

**逻辑：** 冒号后的瑞典案例解释 that 指代的可弥合技能缺口情形；when 说明触发背景，that 从句说明培训计划的作用。

**结构：**

- 引导句：That seems to be the case in Sweden
- 冒号后主句：Scandinavian Airlines decided
-   ├ 时间/条件省略结构：When forced to furlough 90 per cent of their cabin staff
-   └ 宾语不定式：to start up a short retraining program
-       └ 定语从句(that)：that reskilled the laid-off workers to support hospital staff

**考点：**

- furlough：让员工暂时停职。
- laid-off workers：被裁撤或停职的员工。
- reskill somebody to do：为某人再培训，使其能够做某事。
