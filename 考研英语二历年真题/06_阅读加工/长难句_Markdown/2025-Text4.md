# 2025 年考研英语二阅读 Text 4 · 长难句

## E2-2025-T4-P1-S2

Often appearing as trodden dirt tracks through otherwise neat green spaces, these routes of collective disobedience cut corners, bisect lawns and cross hills, representing the natural capability of people (and animals) to go from point A to point B most effectively.

**译文：** 这些集体"不服从"的路线常以踩出来的土路出现在原本整洁的绿地中；它们抄近路、穿过草坪和山坡，体现了人和动物以最高效率从A点到B点的天然能力。

**主干：** These routes cut corners, bisect lawns and cross hills（这些路线抄近路、穿草坪、越山坡）

**逻辑：** 句首描述小径如何出现，主干列出三种穿行方式，末尾representing将这些行为概括为人和动物寻找最高效路线的能力。

**结构：**

- 现在分词短语〔描述外观〕：Often appearing as trodden dirt tracks through otherwise neat green spaces
- 主句：these routes of collective disobedience cut corners, bisect lawns and cross hills
-   └ 现在分词短语〔结果/说明〕：representing the natural capability of people (and animals)
-       └ 不定式〔修饰 capability〕：to go from point A to point B most effectively

**考点：**

- trodden dirt tracks 指被反复踩出的土路。
- cut corners 既可字面指切过拐角，也有“走捷径”之意。
- otherwise neat 表示若没有这些小径，绿地本来很整齐。

## E2-2025-T4-P1-S6

A Reddit webpage devoted to the phenomenon, boasting nearly 50,000 members, showcases images of local desire paths adorned with signs instructing pedestrians to adhere to designated walkways, underscoring the rebellious nature inherent in these human-made tracks.

**译文：** 一个专门讨论这种现象、拥有近五万名成员的Reddit页面，展示了各地意愿小径的图片；有些小径旁竖着要求行人走指定道路的标牌，反而凸显了这些人为踩出的小路内在的反叛性质。

**主干：** A Reddit webpage showcases images（一个Reddit页面展示图片）

**逻辑：** 主干很短，其余多个分词短语逐层修饰页面、路径和标牌，最后underscoring点出“越禁止越体现反叛”的含义。

**结构：**

- 主句：A Reddit webpage ... showcases images of local desire paths
-   ├ 过去分词短语〔修饰 webpage〕：devoted to the phenomenon
-   ├ 现在分词短语〔补充 webpage〕：boasting nearly 50,000 members
-   ├ 过去分词短语〔修饰 paths〕：adorned with signs
-   │   └ 现在分词短语〔修饰 signs〕：instructing pedestrians to adhere to designated walkways
-   └ 结果状语：underscoring the rebellious nature inherent in these human-made tracks

**考点：**

- boast 在此意为“拥有”，不一定含贬义。
- adhere to designated walkways 指遵守要求、走指定人行道。
- inherent in 意为“内在于、固有于”。

## E2-2025-T4-P1-S15

As desire paths criss-cross through both physical and virtual landscapes, they stand as a proof of the collective insistence on forging unconventional routes and embracing the spirit of communal choice.

**译文：** 当意愿小径纵横于现实和虚拟空间时，它们证明了群体坚持开辟非常规路线、拥抱共同选择精神的力量。

**主干：** They stand as a proof of the collective insistence（它们成为群体坚持的证明）

**逻辑：** As从句把意愿小径的存在范围扩展到现实与网络，主句用两个并列动名词概括其象征意义：开辟非常规路线和坚持共同选择。

**结构：**

- 时间/伴随状语从句(As)：As desire paths criss-cross through both physical and virtual landscapes
- 主句：they stand as a proof of the collective insistence
-   └ 介词短语(on)：on forging unconventional routes and embracing the spirit of communal choice
-       └ 并列动名词：forging ... and embracing ...

**考点：**

- criss-cross 意为“纵横交错地穿过”。
- insistence on 指“对……的坚持”。
- communal choice 强调群体共同形成的选择。
