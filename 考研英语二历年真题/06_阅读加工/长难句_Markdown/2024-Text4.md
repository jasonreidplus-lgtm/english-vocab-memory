# 2024 年考研英语二阅读 Text 4 · 长难句

## E2-2024-T4-P1-S5

Existing health privacy law, such as the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act, is primarily focused on the way hospitals, doctors' offices, clinics and insurance companies store health records online.

**译文：** 现行健康隐私法，例如《健康保险可携性与责任法案》，主要关注医院、诊所、医生办公室和保险公司如何在线存储健康记录。

**主干：** Existing health privacy law is focused on the way（现行健康隐私法主要关注这种方式）

**逻辑：** 句子核心是法律关注the way；中间such as给出法律实例，末尾从句具体说明受监管主体如何在线保存健康记录。

**结构：**

- 主句：Existing health privacy law is primarily focused on the way
-   ├ 插入举例：such as the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act
-   └ 定语从句〔省略that/in which，修饰 way〕：hospitals, doctors' offices, clinics and insurance companies store health records online

**考点：**

- be focused on 意为“重点关注”。
- the way 后的关系词常被省略。
- Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act 常简称HIPAA。

## E2-2024-T4-P1-S10

Flo Health and the FTC settled the matter with a Consent Order requiring the company to get app users' express affirmative consent before sharing their health information as well as to instruct the third parties to delete the data they had obtained.

**译文：** Flo Health与联邦贸易委员会以同意令方式和解：公司必须先取得应用用户明确的肯定同意，才能分享其健康信息，同时还须指示第三方删除它们已经获取的数据。

**主干：** Flo Health and the FTC settled the matter with a Consent Order（Flo Health与联邦贸易委员会以同意令和解）

**逻辑：** 主干交代和解方式；requiring说明同意令的两项要求，as well as连接取得同意和指示删数，that从句限定已被第三方获取的数据。

**结构：**

- 主句：Flo Health and the FTC settled the matter with a Consent Order
-   └ 现在分词短语〔修饰 Consent Order〕：requiring the company
-       ├ 宾补一：to get app users' express affirmative consent before sharing ...
-       └ 宾补二：as well as to instruct the third parties to delete the data
-           └ 定语从句(that)：they had obtained

**考点：**

- Consent Order 是监管和解中的“同意令”。
- express affirmative consent 指明确、主动作出的同意。
- before sharing 的逻辑主语是 company。

## E2-2024-T4-P1-S12

While the FTC is doing what it can to ensure apps are keeping their promises to consumers around the handling of their sensitive health information, the rate at which these health apps are hitting the market demonstrates just how immense of a challenge this is.

**译文：** 联邦贸易委员会正尽力确保各应用兑现妥善处理消费者敏感健康信息的承诺，但健康应用进入市场的速度表明，这项挑战极其艰巨。

**主干：** The rate demonstrates how immense a challenge this is（这种速度表明挑战极其艰巨）

**逻辑：** While承认监管机构正在尽力，主句随后转折指出应用上市速度揭示了问题规模；at which把rate与上市动作连接起来。

**结构：**

- 让步状语从句(While)：While the FTC is doing what it can
-   ├ 目的状语(to)：to ensure
-   └ 宾语从句：apps are keeping their promises to consumers around the handling of ... information
- 主句：the rate ... demonstrates
-   ├ 定语从句(at which)：at which these health apps are hitting the market
-   └ 宾语从句：just how immense of a challenge this is

**考点：**

- do what it can 指“尽其所能”。
- keep one's promises 指“兑现承诺”。
- hit the market 意为“进入市场、上市”。
