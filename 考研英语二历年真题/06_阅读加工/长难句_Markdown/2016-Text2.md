# 2016 年考研英语二阅读 Text 2 · 长难句

## E2-2016-T2-P1-S8

In particular, they called for forging closer collaborations with western state governments, which are often uneasy with federal action, and with the private landowners who control an estimated 95% of the prairie chicken's habitat.

**译文：** 具体而言，他们呼吁同往往对联邦行动心存不安的西部各州政府，以及控制着约95%小草原松鸡栖息地的私人土地所有者，建立更紧密的合作。

**主干：** 主句：they called for forging closer collaborations（他们呼吁建立更紧密的合作）

**逻辑：** collaborations之后用两个with并列列出合作对象；两个定语从句分别解释州政府的顾虑和私人地主的重要性。

**结构：**

- 状语：In particular
- 主句：they called for forging closer collaborations
-   ├ 介词宾语①：with western state governments
-   │   └ 非限制性定语从句(which)：which are often uneasy with federal action
-   └ 介词宾语②：and with the private landowners
-       └ 定语从句(who)：who control an estimated 95% of the prairie chicken's habitat

**考点：**

- call for doing表示“呼吁/要求做某事”。
- be uneasy with表示“对……感到不安或有顾虑”。
- an estimated 95%意为“估计约95%”。

## E2-2016-T2-P1-S9

Under the plan, for example, the agency said it would not prosecute landowners or businesses that unintentionally kill, harm, or disturb the bird, as long as they had signed a range-wide management plan to restore prairie chicken habitat.

**译文：** 例如，根据该计划，该机构表示，只要土地所有者或企业已经签署一项覆盖整个分布区、旨在恢复小草原松鸡栖息地的管理方案，就不会因其无意杀死、伤害或惊扰这种鸟而起诉他们。

**主干：** 主句：the agency said it would not prosecute landowners or businesses（该机构称不会起诉土地所有者或企业）

**逻辑：** 主句转述机构承诺；that从句限定不起诉的对象；as long as提出豁免起诉的前提条件。

**结构：**

- 状语：Under the plan, for example
- 主句：the agency said
-   └ 宾语从句〔省略that〕：it would not prosecute landowners or businesses
-       └ 定语从句(that，修饰 landowners or businesses)：that unintentionally kill, harm, or disturb the bird
- 条件状语从句(as long as)：as long as they had signed a range-wide management plan
-   └ 不定式作后置定语〔修饰 plan〕：to restore prairie chicken habitat

**考点：**

- prosecute表示“起诉”，不是一般的“迫害”。
- as long as在此表示条件“只要”。
- range-wide指覆盖该物种整个分布范围。

## E2-2016-T2-P1-S10

Negotiated by USFWS and the states, the plan requires individuals and businesses that damage habitat as part of their operations to pay into a fund to replace every acre destroyed with 2 new acres of suitable habitat.

**译文：** 该计划由美国鱼类及野生动物管理局与各州协商制定，要求在经营活动中破坏栖息地的个人和企业向基金缴款，以便每破坏一英亩土地，就用两英亩新的适宜栖息地加以补偿。

**主干：** 主句：the plan requires individuals and businesses to pay into a fund（该计划要求个人和企业向基金缴款）

**逻辑：** 句首分词说明计划由谁协商制定；require A to do B给出义务；末尾不定式说明基金用途及二比一补偿规则。

**结构：**

- 过去分词短语作状语：Negotiated by USFWS and the states
- 主句：the plan requires individuals and businesses to pay into a fund
-   └ 定语从句(that，修饰 individuals and businesses)：that damage habitat as part of their operations
-   └ 不定式作目的状语：to replace every acre destroyed with 2 new acres of suitable habitat
-       └ 过去分词作后置定语：destroyed〔修饰 every acre〕

**考点：**

- require sb to do sth表示“要求某人做某事”。
- pay into a fund意为“向基金缴款”。
- replace A with B表示“用B替代/补偿A”。
