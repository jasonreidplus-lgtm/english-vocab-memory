# 2023 年考研英语二阅读 Text 3 · 长难句

## E2-2023-T3-P1-S2

In 2011, Sparrow led a study in which participants were asked to record 40 factoids in a computer ("an ostrich's eye is bigger than its brain," for example).

**译文：** 2011年，斯帕罗主持了一项研究，要求参与者把40条零碎事实记录到电脑里（例如“鸵鸟的眼睛比它的大脑还大”）。

**主干：** S+V+O：Sparrow led a study（斯帕罗主持了一项研究）

**逻辑：** 主句只交代研究；in which 从句展开研究任务，被动结构 were asked 后接不定式；括号内容给出 factoid 的具体例子。

**结构：**

- 时间状语：In 2011
- 主句：Sparrow led a study
-   └ 定语从句(in which)：in which participants were asked
-       └ 不定式宾补：to record 40 factoids in a computer
-       └ 括号举例："an ostrich's eye is bigger than its brain," for example

**考点：**

- factoid 在此指一条简短、零碎的事实。
- in which 可替换为 where，但正式程度更高。
- be asked to do 表示被要求做某事。

## E2-2023-T3-P1-S13

Perhaps, she suggests, the trend will change our approach to learning from a focus on individual facts and memorization to an emphasis on more conceptual thinking—something that is not available on the Internet.

**译文：** 她认为，这一趋势或许会改变我们的学习方式：从关注个别事实和死记硬背，转向更重视概念性思考——而这种思考无法从互联网上直接获得。

**主干：** S+V+O：The trend will change our approach to learning（这一趋势会改变我们的学习方式）

**逻辑：** from...to...明确学习重心的迁移；破折号后的 something 回指 conceptual thinking，并指出这种能力不能直接从互联网取得。

**结构：**

- 主句：Perhaps the trend will change our approach to learning
- 插入语：she suggests
- 变化范围：from a focus on individual facts and memorization
-   └ 对应终点(to)：to an emphasis on more conceptual thinking
- 破折号同位补充：something
-   └ 定语从句(that)：that is not available on the Internet

**考点：**

- approach to 中 to 是介词，后接 learning。
- a focus on...to an emphasis on... 是平行结构。
- available on the Internet 在此不是“联网可用”，而是“网上现成可得”。

## E2-2023-T3-P1-S17

And surfing the web exercised the brain more than reading did among computer-savvy older adults in a 2008 study involving 24 participants at the Semel Institute for Neuroscience and Human Behavior at the University of California, Los Angeles.

**译文：** 此外，2008年加州大学洛杉矶分校塞梅尔神经科学与人类行为研究所对24名参与者开展的一项研究发现，在熟悉电脑的老年人中，上网比阅读更能调动大脑。

**主干：** 倒装比较：Surfing the web exercised the brain more than reading did（上网比阅读更能调动大脑）

**逻辑：** 句首 And 承接前文证据；核心是 more than 比较，上网与阅读是两个并列比较项；后面连续介词短语只补充人群、研究和机构。

**结构：**

- 主句：surfing the web exercised the brain more than reading did
- 适用人群：among computer-savvy older adults
- 研究背景：in a 2008 study
-   └ 现在分词短语：involving 24 participants
-       └ 地点机构：at the Semel Institute... at the University of California, Los Angeles

**考点：**

- reading did 中 did 代替 exercised the brain，避免重复。
- computer-savvy 表示熟悉、善于使用电脑。
- involving 24 participants 修饰 study。
