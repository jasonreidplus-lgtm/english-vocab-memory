# 2011 年考研英语二阅读 Text 4 · 长难句

## E2-2011-T4-P1-S5

Markets have lost faith that the euro zone's economies, weaker or stronger, will one day converge thanks to the discipline of sharing a single currency, which denies uncompetitive members the quick fix of devaluation.

**译文：** 市场已经不再相信，欧元区各经济体无论强弱，终有一天会因共享单一货币所带来的纪律约束而趋同；这种货币安排使缺乏竞争力的成员国无法通过货币贬值迅速脱困。

**主干：** S+V+O：Markets have lost faith that the economies will converge（市场已不再相信各经济体会趋同）

**逻辑：** that 说明市场失去的是何种信心；thanks to 提出原本被寄望的趋同机制；which 补充单一货币如何约束竞争力不足的成员。

**结构：**

- 主句：Markets have lost faith
-   └ 同位语从句(that)〔说明 faith 的内容〕：that the euro zone's economies ... will one day converge
-       ├ 插入概括：weaker or stronger
-       ├ 原因状语：thanks to the discipline of sharing a single currency
-       └ 非限制性定语从句〔which，修饰 sharing a single currency 这一安排〕：which denies uncompetitive members the quick fix of devaluation

**考点：**

- lose faith that... 可译为“不再相信……”。
- weaker or stronger 是插入语，表示无论经济强弱。
- quick fix 指快速但往往治标不治本的解决办法。

## E2-2011-T4-P1-S10

It insists that economic co-ordination should involve all 27 members of the EU club, among whom there is a small majority for free-market liberalism and economic rigour; in the inner core alone, Germany fears, a small majority favour French interference.

**译文：** 德国坚持，经济协调应让欧盟俱乐部全部27个成员国参与；在这些国家中，支持自由市场的自由主义和严格经济纪律者占微弱多数，而德国担心，仅在内部核心国家中，支持法国式干预的反而会占微弱多数。

**主干：** S+V+O：It insists that co-ordination should involve all 27 members（德国坚持经济协调应包括全部27国）

**逻辑：** 句子用分号对比两个范围：欧盟27国整体中自由市场派占微弱多数，而仅限核心国家时法国式干预派可能占多数。

**结构：**

- 主句：It〔Germany〕 insists
-   └ 宾语从句(that)：economic co-ordination should involve all 27 members of the EU club
-       └ 非限制性定语从句(among whom)〔修饰 members〕：among whom there is a small majority for free-market liberalism and economic rigour
- 分号后对比分句：in the inner core alone, ... a small majority favour French interference
-   └ 插入语：Germany fears

**考点：**

- It 回指上一句的 Germany。
- among whom 是“在这些成员中”，whom 指27个成员国。
- Germany fears 是插入评注，主句仍是 a small majority favour...。

## E2-2011-T4-P1-S12

Translated, that means politicians intervening in monetary policy and a system of redistribution from richer to poorer members, via cheaper borrowing for governments through common Eurobonds or complete fiscal transfers.

**译文：** 换句话说，这意味着政客干预货币政策，并建立一套从富裕成员向较贫困成员再分配的制度，具体方式包括通过共同发行欧洲债券让各国政府以更低成本借款，或直接进行财政转移。

**主干：** S+V+C：That means A and B（那意味着A以及B）

**逻辑：** means 后并列“政治干预货币政策”和“成员间再分配制度”；via 后再并列两种再分配路径：共同欧债带来的低成本借款，或直接财政转移。

**结构：**

- 评注状语：Translated〔换句话说〕
- 主句：that means
-   ├ 宾语①：politicians intervening in monetary policy
-   └ 宾语②：a system of redistribution from richer to poorer members
-       └ 方式状语(via)：via cheaper borrowing for governments ... or complete fiscal transfers
-           └ 方式状语(through)：through common Eurobonds

**考点：**

- Translated 是省略式评注，可译为“换句话说”。
- intervening 与 redistribution 在语义上是并列内容。
- Eurobonds 在此指由成员共同承担的欧洲债券。
