# 2013 年考研英语二阅读 Text 3 · 长难句

## E2-2013-T3-P1-S1

Scientists have found that although we are prone to snap overreactions, if we take a moment and think about how we are likely to react, we can reduce or even eliminate the negative effects of our quick, hard-wired responses.

**译文：** 科学家发现，尽管我们容易瞬间反应过度，但如果能停顿片刻，想一想自己可能会如何反应，就可以减轻甚至消除这种快速、本能反应所产生的负面影响。

**主干：** 主干：Scientists have found that we can reduce or eliminate the negative effects ...

**逻辑：** although 先承认人类容易本能过度反应，if 再提出“暂停并预判”的条件，主句给出可减轻负面影响的结果。

**结构：**

- 宾语从句(that)：that ... we can reduce or even eliminate ...
- 让步状语从句(although)：although we are prone to snap overreactions
- 条件状语从句(if)：if we take a moment and think about ...
- 宾语从句(how)：how we are likely to react
- 介词短语：of our quick, hard-wired responses，修饰 negative effects

**考点：**

- be prone to 意为“容易/倾向于”，to 后接名词。
- take a moment 不是“花很久”，而是“稍作停顿”。
- hard-wired 比喻“天生设定的、本能的”。

## E2-2013-T3-P1-S13

John Gottman, the marriage expert, explains that we quickly "thin slice" information reliably only after we ground such snap reactions in "thick sliced" long-term study.

**译文：** 婚姻专家约翰·戈特曼解释说，只有先把这类瞬间反应建立在“厚切片”式的长期研究之上，我们才能可靠地对信息进行快速的“薄切片”判断。

**主干：** 主干：John Gottman explains that we ... 'thin slice' information reliably only after ...

**逻辑：** only after 限定“快速判断可靠”的前提：长期、充分观察先提供基础，随后形成的瞬间判断才可信。

**结构：**

- 同位语插入：the marriage expert，补充说明 John Gottman
- 宾语从句(that)：that we quickly 'thin slice' information reliably
- 时间限制：only after we ground such snap reactions in ... long-term study
- ground A in B：把 such snap reactions 建立在 'thick sliced' long-term study 之上

**考点：**

- thin slice 作动词，指凭少量信息快速判断。
- thick sliced 与 thin slice 构成概念对照，强调长期、丰富的信息基础。
- only after 位于句中，因此这里不发生主谓倒装。

## E2-2013-T3-P1-S15

Our ability to mute our hard-wired reactions by pausing is what differentiates us from animals: dogs can think about the future only intermittently or for a few minutes.

**译文：** 我们通过停顿来抑制本能反应的能力，正是我们区别于动物之处：狗只能偶尔思考未来，或者只能思考几分钟。

**主干：** 主干：Our ability ... is what differentiates us from animals

**逻辑：** 冒号前提出人与动物的关键差异是“暂停并抑制本能反应”，冒号后以狗对未来思考能力有限作为具体对照。

**结构：**

- 主语中心：Our ability
- 不定式作后置定语：to mute our hard-wired reactions
- 方式状语：by pausing
- 表语从句(what)：what differentiates us from animals
- 冒号后解释说明：dogs can think about the future only intermittently or for a few minutes

**考点：**

- what = the thing that，在表语从句中兼作 differentiates 的主语。
- differentiate A from B 表示“把A与B区分开”。
- by pausing 是手段，不是时间状语。
