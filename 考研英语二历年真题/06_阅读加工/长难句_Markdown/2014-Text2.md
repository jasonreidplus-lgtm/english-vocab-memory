# 2014 年考研英语二阅读 Text 2 · 长难句

## E2-2014-T2-P1-S3

Social psychologists have amassed oceans of research into what they call the "above average effect", or "illusory superiority", and shown that, for example, 70% of us rate ourselves as above average in leadership, 93% in driving and 85% at getting on well with others – all obviously statistical impossibilities.

**译文：** 社会心理学家对所谓的“高于平均效应”或“虚幻优越感”积累了海量研究，并发现，例如，70%的人认为自己的领导力高于平均水平，93%的人认为自己的驾驶水平高于平均水平，85%的人认为自己比一般人更善于与人相处——这些在统计上显然都不可能。

**主干：** 并列谓语主干：Social psychologists have amassed ... and shown that ...

**逻辑：** 先说明研究积累，再用三组不可能同时成立的高比例，实证展示人们普遍高估自己的“虚幻优越感”。

**结构：**

- 谓语①：have amassed oceans of research into what they call ...
- what 宾语从句：what they call the 'above average effect' or 'illusory superiority'
- 谓语②(and)：(have) shown that ...
- that 从句列举三项自评比例：70% ... leadership, 93% ... driving, 85% ... getting on well with others
- 破折号总结：all obviously statistical impossibilities

**考点：**

- what = the phenomenon that，同时作 call 的宾语。
- rate ourselves as above average 意为“把自己评为高于平均水平”。
- oceans of 是夸张表达，意为“海量的”。

## E2-2014-T2-P1-S8

Rather than have people simply rate their beauty compared with others, he asked them to identify an original photograph of themselves from a lineup including versions that had been altered to appear more and less attractive.

**译文：** 他没有让人们简单地把自己的外貌与他人比较并评分，而是让他们从一组照片中找出自己的原始照片；这组照片还包括经过处理、显得更漂亮或更不漂亮的版本。

**主干：** 主干：he asked them to identify an original photograph of themselves

**逻辑：** 研究设计避开主观打分，改用看似更自动的照片识别任务，以检验受试者是否真的把美化后的形象当作自己。

**结构：**

- 取舍状语：Rather than have people simply rate their beauty compared with others
- 主句结构：ask + 宾语 them + 不定式 to identify
- 来源范围：from a lineup
- 现在分词短语修饰 lineup：including versions
- 定语从句(that，修饰 versions)：that had been altered to appear more and less attractive

**考点：**

- rather than 后与主句形成“不是……而是……”的选择。
- have people rate 中 have 是使役动词。
- had been altered 是过去完成时的被动，表示照片在识别前已被处理。

## E2-2014-T2-P1-S13

In fact, those who thought that the images higher up the attractiveness scale were real directly corresponded with those who showed other markers for having higher self-esteem.

**译文：** 事实上，把吸引力等级中更漂亮的照片当成真实照片的人，恰好也是在其他指标上表现出较高自尊的人。

**主干：** 主干：those ... directly corresponded with those ...

**逻辑：** 两组“those”其实指同一趋势：越把更美化照片认作真实自我的人，也越表现出其他高自尊指标。

**结构：**

- 主语：those who thought that the images higher up the attractiveness scale were real
- 定语从句①(who，修饰前一 those)：who thought that ...
- thought 的宾语从句(that)：the images ... were real
- 谓语：directly corresponded with
- 宾语：those who showed other markers for having higher self-esteem；who 引导定语从句

**考点：**

- higher up the attractiveness scale 是后置修饰 images。
- correspond with 在此表示“与……相对应”。
- markers for having higher self-esteem 指显示较高自尊的其他指标。
