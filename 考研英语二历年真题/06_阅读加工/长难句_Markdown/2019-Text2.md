# 2019 年考研英语二阅读 Text 2 · 长难句

## E2-2019-T2-P1-S2

Even as we humans count on forests to soak up a good share of the carbon dioxide we produce, we are threatening their ability to do so.

**译文：** 就在我们人类指望森林吸收自己排放的大量二氧化碳时，我们也在威胁森林完成这一任务的能力。

**主干：** 让步复句：Even as we count on forests..., we are threatening their ability...（尽管我们依赖森林……，却在威胁其能力）

**逻辑：** Even as 把“依赖森林吸碳”和“削弱其吸碳能力”并置，形成鲜明反差；do so 指代 soak up carbon dioxide。

**结构：**

- 让步/同时状语从句(Even as)：we humans count on forests
-   └ 目的不定式：to soak up a good share of the carbon dioxide
-       └ 定语从句〔省略 that，修饰 carbon dioxide〕：we produce
- 主句：we are threatening their ability
-   └ 不定式作后置定语：to do so

**考点：**

- count on：依赖、指望。
- soak up：吸收。
- do so 用来避免重复前面的动作。

## E2-2019-T2-P1-S16

That's only a small share of the total acreage that could benefit, about half a million acres in all, so it will be vital to prioritize areas at greatest risk of fire or drought.

**译文：** 这只占全部可受益土地的一小部分，总计约50万英亩，因此优先治理火灾或干旱风险最大的地区至关重要。

**主干：** 并列因果复句：That is only a small share..., so it will be vital to prioritize...（那只占一小部分，因此优先处理……至关重要）

**逻辑：** 前半句用规模对比说明资源有限，so 引出政策优先级；it 是形式主语，真正主语是后面的不定式。

**结构：**

- 分句①：That is only a small share of the total acreage
-   ├ 定语从句(that)：that could benefit
-   └ 同位补充：about half a million acres in all
- 结果分句(so)：it will be vital
-   └ 真正主语不定式：to prioritize areas
-       └ 介词短语作后置定语：at greatest risk of fire or drought

**考点：**

- acreage：土地面积、英亩数。
- in all：总计。
- prioritize areas at greatest risk：优先处理风险最大的地区。

## E2-2019-T2-P1-S17

The strategy also aims to ensure that carbon in woody material removed from the forests is locked away in the form of solid lumber or burned as biofuel in vehicles that would otherwise run on fossil fuels.

**译文：** 该策略还旨在确保，从森林中移除的木质材料所含的碳，要么以实木木材的形式封存，要么作为生物燃料在原本会使用化石燃料的车辆中燃烧。

**主干：** S+V+O从句：The strategy aims to ensure that carbon is locked away or burned（该策略旨在确保碳被封存或燃烧利用）

**逻辑：** ensure 后的 that 从句由两个并列被动谓语构成，说明木材中的碳有两种去向；otherwise 突出以生物燃料替代化石燃料。

**结构：**

- 主句：The strategy also aims
-   └ 目的不定式：to ensure
-       └ 宾语从句(that)：carbon ... is locked away ... or burned as biofuel
-           ├ 过去分词短语〔修饰 material〕：removed from the forests
-           └ 定语从句(that，修饰 vehicles)：that would otherwise run on fossil fuels

**考点：**

- woody material：木质材料。
- lock away：封存。
- would otherwise do：否则本来会做……。
