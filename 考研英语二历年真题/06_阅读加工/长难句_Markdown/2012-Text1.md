# 2012 年考研英语二阅读 Text 1 · 长难句

## E2-2012-T1-P1-S3

Unfortunately, L.A. Unified has produced an inflexible policy which mandates that with the exception of some advanced courses, homework may no longer count for more than 10% of a student's academic grade.

**译文：** 遗憾的是，洛杉矶联合学区制定了一项僵化政策，规定除少数高级课程外，家庭作业在学生学业成绩中所占比例不得超过10%。

**主干：** S+V+O：L.A. Unified has produced an inflexible policy（洛杉矶联合学区制定了一项僵化政策）

**逻辑：** which 从句说明政策内容；mandates 后 that 从句给出具体规定，with the exception of... 限定规定的适用范围。

**结构：**

- 主句：Unfortunately, L.A. Unified has produced an inflexible policy
-   └ 定语从句〔which，修饰 policy〕：which mandates
-       └ 宾语从句(that)：homework may no longer count for more than 10% of a student's academic grade
-           └ 例外状语：with the exception of some advanced courses

**考点：**

- mandate 在此是动词，意为“强制规定”。
- count for more than 10% 指在总成绩中占比超过10%。
- with the exception of：除……之外。

## E2-2012-T1-P1-S7

But if the district is essentially giving a pass to students who do not do their homework because of complicated family lives, it is going riskily close to the implication that standards need to be lowered for poor children.

**译文：** 但如果学区实质上因为学生家庭生活复杂，就放过那些不做作业的学生，那便危险地接近于暗示：针对贫困儿童，标准需要降低。

**主干：** 条件复句：If the district is giving a pass..., it is going close to the implication...（如果学区放过……，就接近于暗示……）

**逻辑：** if 建立政策做法与危险含义之间的条件推论；who 说明被放过的学生；that 从句把潜在暗示具体化。

**结构：**

- 条件状语从句(if)：the district is essentially giving a pass to students
-   ├ 定语从句〔who，修饰 students〕：who do not do their homework
-   └ 原因状语：because of complicated family lives
- 主句：it is going riskily close to the implication
-   └ 同位语从句(that)〔说明 implication〕：that standards need to be lowered for poor children

**考点：**

- give a pass to somebody 指对某人免予追究、放宽要求。
- go close to the implication 可理解为“几乎等于暗示”。
- need to be lowered 是被动结构，指标准被降低。

## E2-2012-T1-P1-S16

Meanwhile, this policy does nothing to ensure that the homework students receive is meaningful or appropriate to their age and the subject, or that teachers are not assigning more than they are willing to review and correct.

**译文：** 同时，这项政策完全无法确保学生收到的作业有意义、符合其年龄和学科特点，也无法确保教师不会布置超出自己愿意审阅和批改范围的作业。

**主干：** S+V+O：This policy does nothing to ensure A or B（这项政策无法确保A或B）

**逻辑：** ensure 后有两个并列 that 从句：一是作业本身合适，二是教师布置量不超过其批改能力；第一个从句内部 meaningful 与 appropriate 并列。

**结构：**

- 主句：this policy does nothing
-   └ 目的/结果不定式：to ensure
-       ├ 宾语从句①(that)：that the homework students receive is meaningful or appropriate to their age and the subject
-       │   └ 定语从句〔省略 that，修饰 homework〕：students receive
-       └ 并列宾语从句②(or that)：that teachers are not assigning more than they are willing to review and correct
-           └ 比较从句(than)：than they are willing to review and correct

**考点：**

- does nothing to ensure 表示“完全没有保障”。
- the homework students receive 中省略了关系代词 that。
- more than they are willing to review and correct 指超过教师愿意审阅、批改的数量。
