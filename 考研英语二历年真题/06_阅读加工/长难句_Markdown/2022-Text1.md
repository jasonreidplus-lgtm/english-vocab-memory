# 2022 年考研英语二阅读 Text 1 · 长难句

## E2-2022-T1-P1-S4

This all seems random, but it's by design, part of what the $6.1 billion U.S. egg industry bets will be its next big thing: climate-friendly eggs.

**译文：** 这一切看似随意，其实是有意安排的，是产值61亿美元的美国蛋品行业押注下一项热门产品——气候友好型鸡蛋——的一部分。

**主干：** S+V：This all seems random, but it's by design（这一切看似随意，其实是有意安排的）

**逻辑：** but 先完成“看似随机—实为设计”的转折；part of...补充说明这种设计属于行业布局的一部分，what 从句作 of 的宾语，内部又含省略引导词的宾语从句。

**结构：**

- 并列分句1：This all seems random
- 并列分句2：but it's by design
-   └ 同位语：part of what the $6.1 billion U.S. egg industry bets will be its next big thing
-       └ 同位语：climate-friendly eggs〔解释 next big thing〕

**考点：**

- by design 意为“有意安排”，与 by accident 相对。
- what the industry bets will be... 可理解为“该行业押注将成为……的事物”。
- $6.1 billion 修饰 U.S. egg industry，表示行业规模。

## E2-2022-T1-P1-S5

These eggs, which are making their debut now on shelves for as much as $8 a dozen, are still labeled organic and animal-friendly, but they're also from birds that live on farms using regenerative agriculture—special techniques to cultivate rich soils that can trap greenhouse gases.

**译文：** 这些鸡蛋如今初次登上货架，售价最高可达每打8美元；它们仍被贴上有机、动物友好的标签，但还来自采用再生农业的农场所饲养的禽类——再生农业运用特殊技术培育肥沃土壤，使其能够吸收温室气体。

**主干：** S+V+C：These eggs are still labeled..., but they're also from birds（这些鸡蛋仍被贴上……标签，但也来自……禽类）

**逻辑：** 句子先给传统的“有机、动物友好”属性，再用 but 增加“再生农业”这一新卖点；破折号后的名词短语解释再生农业，最后的 that 从句说明土壤的功能。

**结构：**

- 主句分句1：These eggs are still labeled organic and animal-friendly
-   └ 非限制性定语从句(which)：which are making their debut now on shelves for as much as $8 a dozen
- 并列分句2(but)：they're also from birds
-   └ 定语从句(that)：that live on farms using regenerative agriculture
-       └ 同位语：special techniques to cultivate rich soils
-           └ 定语从句(that)：that can trap greenhouse gases

**考点：**

- make one's debut 意为“首次亮相”。
- for as much as $8 a dozen 表示售价最高可达每打8美元。
- rich soils 在此是肥沃、富含有机质的土壤。

## E2-2022-T1-P1-S17

Surveys show that younger generations are more concerned about climate change, and some of the success of plant-based meat can be chalked up to shoppers wanting to signal their desire to protect the environment.

**译文：** 调查显示，年轻一代更关注气候变化；植物肉取得的部分成功，也可归因于购物者希望借消费行为表达保护环境的愿望。

**主干：** S+V+O：Surveys show that..., and some success can be chalked up to...（调查显示……，而部分成功可归因于……）

**逻辑：** and 连接两条相互支撑的证据：年轻人更关注气候，以及消费者用购买行为表达环保立场；第二分句用被动短语 be chalked up to 表示原因归属。

**结构：**

- 分句1：Surveys show
-   └ 宾语从句(that)：that younger generations are more concerned about climate change
- 并列分句2(and)：some of the success of plant-based meat can be chalked up to shoppers wanting to signal their desire
-   └ 动名词复合结构：shoppers wanting to signal their desire
-       └ 不定式后置定语：to protect the environment〔修饰 desire〕

**考点：**

- chalk A up to B 意为“把A归因于B”。
- signal one's desire 不是发出字面信号，而是“表明愿望/立场”。
- shoppers wanting... 是介词 to 的宾语，不是完整从句。
