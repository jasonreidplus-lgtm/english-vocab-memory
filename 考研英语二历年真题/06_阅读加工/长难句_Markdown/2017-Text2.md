# 2017 年考研英语二阅读 Text 2 · 长难句

## E2-2017-T2-P1-S8

Infants are wired to look at parents' faces to try to understand their world, and if those faces are blank and unresponsive - as they often are when absorbed in a device - it can be extremely disconcerting for the children.

**译文：** 婴幼儿天生会观察父母的面孔，试图借此理解周围的世界；如果这些面孔毫无表情、没有回应——父母沉浸于电子设备时往往如此——孩子就会感到极度不安。

**主干：** 并列句：Infants are wired to look at parents' faces…, and if those faces are blank…, it can be disconcerting（婴儿天生观察父母面孔；若面孔无回应，孩子会不安）

**逻辑：** 第一分句说明婴儿观察面孔的本能；第二分句以if推出父母沉浸设备、面无回应时给孩子造成的后果。

**结构：**

- 分句①：Infants are wired to look at parents' faces
-   └ 目的状语：to try to understand their world
- 并列分句②(and)：if those faces are blank and unresponsive, it can be extremely disconcerting for the children
-   ├ 条件状语从句(if)：if those faces are blank and unresponsive
-   │   └ 插入性方式从句(as)：as they often are when absorbed in a device
-   └ 主句：it can be extremely disconcerting for the children

**考点：**

- be wired to do表示“天生/生理上被设定为会做某事”。
- as they often are中的are后省略blank and unresponsive。
- when absorbed in a device是when they are absorbed的省略形式。

## E2-2017-T2-P1-S10

In it, a mother is asked to interact with her child in a normal way before putting on a blank expression and not giving them any visual social feedback: The child becomes increasingly distressed as she tries to capture her mother's attention.

**译文：** 在实验中，母亲先被要求以正常方式与孩子互动，随后摆出一张毫无表情的脸，不再提供任何视觉上的社交反馈；孩子在试图吸引母亲注意时会变得越来越痛苦。

**主干：** 主干：a mother is asked to interact… before putting on…; the child becomes increasingly distressed（母亲先互动再面无表情；孩子越来越痛苦）

**逻辑：** before连接实验的两个阶段；冒号后的独立分句描述母亲停止反馈后，孩子试图重新获得注意并逐渐痛苦的结果。

**结构：**

- 主句①：a mother is asked to interact with her child in a normal way
-   └ 时间状语(before)：before putting on a blank expression and not giving them any visual social feedback
- 冒号后结果分句：The child becomes increasingly distressed
-   └ 时间状语从句(as)：as she tries to capture her mother's attention

**考点：**

- be asked to do是被动结构“被要求做”。
- putting与giving共享before，且由and并列。
- capture one's attention意为“吸引某人的注意”。

## E2-2017-T2-P1-S12

On the other hand, Tronick himself is concerned that the worries about kids' use of screens are born out of an "oppressive ideology that demands that parents should always be interacting" with their children: "It's based on a somewhat fantasised, very white, very upper-middle-class ideology that says if you're failing to expose your child to 30,000 words you are neglecting them."

**译文：** 另一方面，特罗尼克本人担心，对儿童使用屏幕的忧虑源自一种“强迫父母必须始终与孩子互动的压迫性观念”；他说：“它建立在一种多少有些幻想化、非常白人化、非常中上阶层化的观念之上，这种观念声称，如果你没让孩子接触到3万个单词，就是在疏于照顾他们。”

**主干：** 主句：Tronick is concerned that the worries are born out of an ideology（特罗尼克担心这些忧虑源于一种观念）

**逻辑：** 前半指出忧虑背后的意识形态来源；冒号后引语进一步批评这种意识形态的阶层与族群局限，并概括其条件式判断。

**结构：**

- 转折状语：On the other hand
- 主句：Tronick himself is concerned
-   └ 宾语从句(that)：that the worries about kids' use of screens are born out of an oppressive ideology
-       └ 定语从句(that，修饰 ideology)：that demands that parents should always be interacting with their children
- 冒号后直接引语：It's based on a somewhat fantasised…ideology
-   └ 定语从句(that)：that says
-       └ 宾语从句：if you're failing to expose your child to 30,000 words, you are neglecting them

**考点：**

- be born out of表示“源于、产生于”。
- 第一个that引导concerned的宾语从句；第二个that引导修饰ideology的定语从句。
- expose a child to words指“让孩子接触语言输入”。
