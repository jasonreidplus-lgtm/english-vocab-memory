# 2015 年考研英语二阅读 Text 2 · 长难句

## E2-2015-T2-P1-S4

This has created "a paradox" in that recruiting first-generation students, but then watching many of them fail, means that higher education has "continued to reproduce and widen, rather than close" an achievement gap based on social class, according to the depressing beginning of a paper forthcoming in the journal Psychological Science.

**译文：** 即将发表于《心理科学》期刊的一篇论文在令人沮丧的开头指出，这造成了一个“悖论”：招收第一代大学生后却眼看许多人失败，意味着高等教育“没有缩小，反而继续复制并扩大”由社会阶层造成的成就差距。

**主干：** 主干：This has created 'a paradox'

**逻辑：** 悖论在于，高校一边扩大第一代学生招生，一边未能帮助许多人成功，结果反而复制并扩大阶层成就差距。

**结构：**

- 解释结构(in that)：in that recruiting first-generation students, but then watching many of them fail, means that ...
- 动名词并列主语：recruiting ... but then watching ...
- means 的宾语从句(that)：higher education has continued to reproduce and widen ... an achievement gap
- 插入对比：rather than close
- 来源状语：according to the depressing beginning of a paper forthcoming ...

**考点：**

- in that 用来具体解释 paradox，意为“因为/体现在”。
- recruiting 与 watching 共同构成长主语，谓语用单数 means。
- forthcoming 表示论文“即将发表”。

## E2-2015-T2-P1-S5

But the article is actually quite optimistic, as it outlines a potential solution to this problem, suggesting that an approach (which involves a one-hour, next-to-no-cost program) can close 63 percent of the achievement gap (measured by such factors as grades) between first-generation and other students.

**译文：** 不过，这篇论文实际上相当乐观，因为它提出了一种可能的解决办法：一项只需一小时、几乎没有成本的干预项目，可能缩小第一代大学生与其他学生之间63%的成就差距；该差距以成绩等因素衡量。

**主干：** 主干：the article is actually quite optimistic

**逻辑：** 先以 optimistic 反转上一句的悲观基调，再逐层说明方案的低成本、短时长与可量化效果。

**结构：**

- 原因状语从句(as)：as it outlines a potential solution to this problem
- 现在分词短语作进一步说明：suggesting that an approach ... can close 63 percent of the achievement gap
- 定语从句(which，修饰 approach)：which involves a one-hour, next-to-no-cost program
- 过去分词括注：measured by such factors as grades，修饰 achievement gap
- 介词结构：between first-generation and other students

**考点：**

- as 在此表原因。
- next-to-no-cost 表示“几乎零成本”。
- suggesting 的逻辑主语是前面整件“文章提出方案”的事实。

## E2-2015-T2-P1-S8

Most of the first-generation students (59.1 percent) were recipients of Pell Grants, a federal grant for undergraduates with financial need, while this was true only for 8.6 percent of the students with at least one parent with a four-year degree.

**译文：** 第一代大学生中，大多数人（59.1%）领取佩尔助学金——这是一种面向有经济困难本科生的联邦助学金；而在父母至少一方拥有四年制大学学位的学生中，这一比例仅为8.6%。

**主干：** 对比主干：Most of the first-generation students were recipients of Pell Grants, while this was true only for 8.6 percent ...

**逻辑：** 59.1%与8.6%的鲜明对比，用经济资助数据呈现第一代大学生更明显的阶层劣势。

**结构：**

- 主句①：Most ... (59.1 percent) were recipients of Pell Grants
- 同位语解释：a federal grant for undergraduates with financial need
- 对比并列从句(while)：while this was true only for 8.6 percent of the students
- 介词宾语扩展：with at least one parent with a four-year degree

**考点：**

- while 在此表示对比“而”，不是时间。
- this was true 指“领取 Pell Grants”这一情况。
- 两个 with 分别表示“学生具有某父母条件”和“父母具有某学位”。
