# 2016 年考研英语二阅读 Text 1 · 长难句

## E2-2016-T1-P1-S4

When younger kids learn computer science, they learn that it's not just a confusing, endless string of letters and numbers - but a tool to build apps, or create artwork, or test hypotheses.

**译文：** 年龄较小的孩子学习计算机科学时会明白，它不只是一串令人困惑、似乎无穷无尽的字母和数字，而是一种可以用来开发应用、创作艺术作品或检验假设的工具。

**主干：** 主句：they learn that it is not just A but B（他们认识到，计算机科学不只是A，而是B）

**逻辑：** when交代条件与时间；that从句用not just…but…纠正对计算机科学的误解；三个并列不定式说明工具的用途。

**结构：**

- 时间状语从句(when)：When younger kids learn computer science
- 主句：they learn
-   └ 宾语从句(that)：that it's not just a confusing, endless string of letters and numbers - but a tool
-       └ 不定式并列作后置定语〔修饰 tool〕：to build apps, or create artwork, or test hypotheses

**考点：**

- not just A but B 表示“不只是A，而是B”。
- build、create、test由or连接，结构平行。
- hypothesis意为“假设”，复数为hypotheses。

## E2-2016-T1-P1-S8

Students also benefit from learning something about coding before they get to college, where introductory computer-science classes are packed to the brim, which can drive the less-experienced or -determined students away.

**译文：** 学生在进入大学前先学一点编程也会受益；大学的计算机科学入门课常常人满为患，这可能把经验较少或意志不够坚定的学生拒之门外。

**主干：** 主句：Students also benefit from learning something about coding（学生也会从先学一点编程中受益）

**逻辑：** 句子先给出“提前学编程有益”的结论，再用where说明大学入门课拥挤，并用which补充这一情况可能造成的后果。

**结构：**

- 主句：Students also benefit from learning something about coding
-   └ 时间状语从句(before)：before they get to college
-   └ 非限制性定语从句(where，修饰 college)：where introductory computer-science classes are packed to the brim
-       └ 非限制性定语从句(which，指代前述拥挤情形)：which can drive the less-experienced or -determined students away

**考点：**

- be packed to the brim意为“挤得满满当当、爆满”。
- which在此指代前面整个“课程爆满”的事实，而非只指classes。
- less-experienced or -determined中第二个连字符表示省略less，即less experienced or less determined。

## E2-2016-T1-P1-S18

The younger they learn how computers think, how to coax the machine into producing what they want - the earlier they learn that they have the power to do that - the better.

**译文：** 他们越早学会计算机如何思考、如何诱导机器产出自己想要的结果，也就越早明白自己有能力做到这一点，因而越好。

**主干：** 关联比较结构：The younger…, the earlier…, the better（越早……就越早……也就越好）

**逻辑：** 连续使用the+比较级构成递进关系：越早理解计算机并学会操控机器，就越早意识到自身能力，结果也越有利。

**结构：**

- 第一层the+比较级：The younger they learn
-   ├ 宾语从句①(how)：how computers think
-   └ 宾语从句②(how)：how to coax the machine into producing what they want
-       └ 宾语从句(what)：what they want
- 第二层the+比较级：the earlier they learn
-   └ 宾语从句(that)：that they have the power to do that
- 结论：the better

**考点：**

- coax A into doing B意为“诱导/设法使A做B”。
- how to coax是“疑问词+不定式”，与how computers think并列作learn的宾语。
- 最后的the better省略了完整主句，属于固定的比较级关联结构。
