# 2024 年考研英语二阅读 Text 2 · 长难句

## E2-2024-T2-P1-S4

Coming at a time of fresh incentives from the UK government for landowners to grow more trees, the trade body says these don't go far enough and fail to promote the benefits of planting them to boost timber supplies.

**译文：** 英国政府最近推出新激励措施，鼓励土地所有者种更多树，但该行业组织表示，这些措施力度不够，也没有宣传植树对于增加木材供应的益处。

**主干：** The trade body says these don't go far enough and fail to promote the benefits（行业组织说这些措施力度不足，也未能宣传益处）

**逻辑：** 句首Coming短语交代政策背景；主句的核心评价是并列的“不够”和“未能宣传”，末尾to boost说明植树的目的。

**结构：**

- 现在分词短语〔时间背景〕：Coming at a time of fresh incentives from the UK government
-   └ 不定式复合结构〔说明 incentives〕：for landowners to grow more trees
- 主句：the trade body says
-   └ 宾语从句〔省略that〕：these don't go far enough and fail to promote the benefits
-       └ of动名词短语〔修饰 benefits〕：of planting them to boost timber supplies

**考点：**

- these 指前文英国政府推出的 incentives。
- go far enough 意为“力度足够、做得足够深入”。
- them 指 trees，而不是 incentives。

## E2-2024-T2-P1-S11

While productive tree planting can deliver real financial benefits to rural economies and contribute to the UK's net-zero strategy, the focus of government support continues to be on food production and the rewilding and planting of native woodland solely for biodiversity.

**译文：** 尽管生产性植树能给农村经济带来切实的经济收益，并助力英国的净零战略，政府支持的重点却仍然放在粮食生产、土地再野化以及仅为保护生物多样性而种植本土林地上。

**主干：** The focus of government support continues to be on A and B（政府支持的重点仍在A和B上）

**逻辑：** While建立“植树有经济和减排收益”与“支持重点仍另有所属”的让步转折；主句说明政府资源配置与前述收益并未对齐。

**结构：**

- 让步状语从句(While)：While productive tree planting can deliver ... and contribute ...
-   └ 并列谓语：can deliver real financial benefits ... and contribute to the UK's net-zero strategy
- 主句：the focus of government support continues to be on food production and ... woodland
-   ├ 并列宾语一：food production
-   └ 并列宾语二：the rewilding and planting of native woodland solely for biodiversity

**考点：**

- productive tree planting 指以生产木材等实际产出为目标的植树。
- rewilding 指让土地恢复更自然、少人工干预的生态状态。
- solely for biodiversity 限定再野化和本土林地种植的政策目的。

## E2-2024-T2-P1-S12

Goodall added: "While food production and biodiversity health are clearly of critical importance, we need our land to also provide secure supplies of wood for construction, manufacturing and contribute to net-zero."

**译文：** 古德尔补充说："虽然粮食生产和生物多样性健康显然至关重要，但我们的土地还应当为建筑和制造提供有保障的木材供应，并为实现净零目标作出贡献。"

**主干：** We need our land to provide supplies and contribute to net-zero（我们需要土地提供木材并助力净零）

**逻辑：** 先承认粮食和生物多样性的重要性，再用need + 宾语 + to do提出土地还应承担的两项功能，语气并非否定前者，而是要求兼顾。

**结构：**

- 引述主句：Goodall added
- 让步状语从句(While)：While food production and biodiversity health are clearly of critical importance
- 主句：we need our land
-   └ 宾语补足语(to)：to also provide secure supplies of wood ... and contribute to net-zero
-       ├ 并列谓语一：provide secure supplies of wood for construction, manufacturing
-       └ 并列谓语二：contribute to net-zero

**考点：**

- be of critical importance 等于 be critically important。
- secure supplies 指稳定、有保障的供应。
- net-zero 指温室气体净零排放目标。
