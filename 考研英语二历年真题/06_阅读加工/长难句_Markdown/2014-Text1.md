# 2014 年考研英语二阅读 Text 1 · 长难句

## E2-2014-T1-P1-S8

It is far better to spend money on experiences, say Ms Dunn and Mr Norton, like interesting trips, unique meals or even going to the cinema.

**译文：** 邓恩女士和诺顿先生认为，把钱花在体验上要好得多，例如有趣的旅行、独特的餐饮，甚至只是去看电影。

**主干：** 主干：It is far better to spend money on experiences

**逻辑：** 句子把研究结论先明确提出，再用插入语标明观点来源，并列举三类体验型消费。

**结构：**

- It 作形式主语，真正主语是不定式：to spend money on experiences
- 比较评价：far better，far 强化比较级
- 插入语：say Ms Dunn and Mr Norton
- 举例同位说明：like interesting trips, unique meals or even going to the cinema

**考点：**

- spend money on 表示“把钱花在……上”。
- say ... 是报道式倒装插入语，不是句子主干。
- like 在此表示“例如”，后列名词短语和动名词短语。

## E2-2014-T1-P1-S11

It seems most people would be better off if they could shorten their commutes to work, spend more time with friends and family and less of it watching television (something the average American spends a whopping two months a year doing, and is hardly jollier for it).

**译文：** 如果人们能缩短通勤时间，多陪伴亲友，少看些电视，似乎大多数人都会过得更好；普通美国人每年竟花整整两个月看电视，却几乎没有因此更快乐。

**主干：** 主干：It seems (that) most people would be better off if ...

**逻辑：** 主句提出改善幸福感的条件，括号则用美国人的看电视时长与低幸福收益形成反讽式证据。

**结构：**

- It seems 后省略连接词 that，后接主语从句内容
- 条件从句(if)：if they could shorten ..., spend more time ... and less of it watching television
- 三个并列动作：shorten / spend more / (spend) less
- 括号内同位说明 something，指 watching television
- 定语从句〔省略 that，修饰 something〕：the average American spends ... doing, and is hardly jollier for it

**考点：**

- be better off 表示“处境更好”。
- less of it 中 it 指 time。
- spend time doing 是固定结构；be jollier for it 表示“因此更快乐”。

## E2-2014-T1-P1-S16

Yet the link between feeling good and spending money on others can be seen among rich and poor people around the world, and scarcity enhances the pleasure of most things for most people.

**译文：** 不过，无论贫富，在世界各地都能看到为他人花钱与良好感受之间的联系；而且对大多数人而言，稀缺会增强大多数事物带来的愉悦。

**主干：** 并列主干：the link ... can be seen ..., and scarcity enhances the pleasure ...

**逻辑：** 句子并列给出两项跨人群规律：利他消费与良好感受相关，稀缺性则会提升享用事物的愉悦。

**结构：**

- 主句①主语：the link between feeling good and spending money on others
- 被动谓语：can be seen among rich and poor people around the world
- 并列主句②(and)：scarcity enhances the pleasure of most things
- 范围状语：for most people

**考点：**

- between A and B 连接 feeling 与 spending 两个动名词结构。
- among rich and poor people 强调该联系并不限于富人。
- scarcity 在此指供应有限或不常获得。
