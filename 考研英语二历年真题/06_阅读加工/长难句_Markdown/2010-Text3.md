# 2010 年考研英语二阅读 Text 3 · 长难句

## E2-2010-T3-P1-S3

"There are fundamental public health problems, like dirty hands instead of a soap habit, that remain killers only because we can't figure out how to change people's habits," said Dr. Curtis, the director of the Hygiene Center at the London School of Hygiene & Tropical Medicine.

**译文：** 伦敦卫生与热带医学学院卫生中心主任柯蒂斯博士说：“有些根本性的公共卫生问题，例如人们手很脏却没有养成用肥皂洗手的习惯，之所以至今仍能致命，仅仅是因为我们还不知道如何改变人们的习惯。”

**主干：** 引语主干：There are public health problems that remain killers（有些公共卫生问题仍会致命）

**逻辑：** 核心因果是“问题仍致命，只因不会改变习惯”；that 限定 problems，only because 强调原因单一。

**结构：**

- 直接引语主句：There are fundamental public health problems
-   ├ 举例插入语：like dirty hands instead of a soap habit
-   └ 定语从句〔that，修饰 problems〕：that remain killers
-       └ 原因状语从句(only because)：only because we can't figure out
-           └ 疑问词+不定式作宾语：how to change people's habits
- 引语后的报告语：said Dr. Curtis
-   └ 同位语：the director of the Hygiene Center at the London School of Hygiene & Tropical Medicine

**考点：**

- remain killers 表示“仍然是致命因素”，不是“杀手仍存在”。
- figure out how to... 意为“弄明白如何……”。
- instead of a soap habit 表示缺少使用肥皂的习惯。

## E2-2010-T3-P1-S5

The companies that Dr. Curtis turned to - Procter & Gamble, Colgate-Palmolive and Unilever - had invested hundreds of millions of dollars finding the subtle cues in consumers' lives that corporations could use to introduce new routines.

**译文：** 柯蒂斯博士求助的公司——宝洁、高露洁-棕榄和联合利华——已经投入数亿美元，寻找消费者生活中那些可供企业用来引入新惯例的细微信号。

**主干：** S+V+O：The companies had invested hundreds of millions of dollars（这些公司已投入数亿美元）

**逻辑：** 第一个 that 说明她求助于哪些公司；第二个 that 说明公司要寻找什么样的 cues；finding 表明巨额投入用于寻找线索。

**结构：**

- 主句：The companies ... had invested hundreds of millions of dollars
-   ├ 定语从句〔that，修饰 companies〕：that Dr. Curtis turned to
-   ├ 破折号同位列举：Procter & Gamble, Colgate-Palmolive and Unilever
-   └ 方式/内容状语：finding the subtle cues in consumers' lives
-       └ 定语从句〔that，修饰 cues〕：that corporations could use to introduce new routines
-           └ 目的状语(to)：to introduce new routines

**考点：**

- turn to somebody 意为“向某人求助”。
- invest money doing 在此表示“投入资金做某事”。
- cue 指触发习惯行为的“提示、信号”。

## E2-2010-T3-P1-S13

"Our products succeed when they become part of daily or weekly patterns," said Carol Berning, a consumer psychologist who recently retired from Procter & Gamble, the company that sold $76 billion of Tide, Crest and other products last year.

**译文：** 宝洁公司最近退休的消费者心理学家卡罗尔·伯宁说：“当我们的产品成为日常或每周惯例的一部分时，它们就成功了。”该公司去年销售了价值760亿美元的汰渍、佳洁士及其他产品。

**主干：** 引语主干：Our products succeed when they become part of patterns（产品成为惯例的一部分时就成功了）

**逻辑：** 引语给出产品成功的条件；said 后人物身份通过同位语和两层定语关系连续补充，阅读时应先抓住引语和报告语。

**结构：**

- 直接引语：Our products succeed
-   └ 时间状语从句(when)：when they become part of daily or weekly patterns
- 报告语：said Carol Berning
-   └ 同位语：a consumer psychologist
-       └ 定语从句〔who，修饰 psychologist〕：who recently retired from Procter & Gamble
-           └ Procter & Gamble 的同位说明：the company
-               └ 定语从句〔that，修饰 company〕：that sold $76 billion of Tide, Crest and other products last year

**考点：**

- patterns 在语境中指反复发生的日常/每周惯例。
- $76 billion of... 表示价值760亿美元的产品销售额。
- who 修饰 Carol Berning 的职业身份，that 则修饰 Procter & Gamble。
