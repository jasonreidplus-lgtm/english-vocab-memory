# 2012 年考研英语二阅读 Text 3 · 逐句精读

## 1. E2-2012-T3-P1-S1

In 2010, a federal judge shook America's biotech industry to its core.

**译文：** 2010年，一名联邦法官的判决从根本上震动了美国生物技术行业。

## 2. E2-2012-T3-P1-S2

Companies had won patents for isolated DNA for decades - by 2005 some 20% of human genes were patented.

**译文：** 几十年来，企业一直能够为分离出的DNA取得专利；到2005年，人类基因中约有20%已被申请专利。

## 3. E2-2012-T3-P1-S3

But in March 2010 a judge ruled that genes were unpatentable.

**译文：** 但2010年3月，一名法官裁定基因不能获得专利。

## 4. E2-2012-T3-P1-S4

Executives were violently agitated.

**译文：** 企业高管对此极为激动不安。

## 5. E2-2012-T3-P1-S5

The Biotechnology Industry Organisation (BIO), a trade group, assured members that this was just a "preliminary step" in a longer battle.

**译文：** 行业组织“生物技术产业组织”（BIO）向会员保证，这只是一场长期斗争中的“初步步骤”。

## 6. E2-2012-T3-P1-S6

On July 29th they were relieved, at least temporarily.

**译文：** 7月29日，他们终于松了一口气，至少暂时如此。

## 7. E2-2012-T3-P1-S7

A federal appeals court overturned the prior decision, ruling that Myriad Genetics could indeed hold patents to two genes that help forecast a woman's risk of breast cancer.

**译文：** 一家联邦上诉法院推翻了先前的判决，裁定万基遗传公司确实可以持有两种基因的专利，这两种基因有助于预测女性患乳腺癌的风险。

**长难句拆解：**

- 主干：S+V+O：A federal appeals court overturned the prior decision（联邦上诉法院推翻了先前判决）
- 逻辑：主句先给出推翻判决的动作；ruling 说明新判决内容；最内层 that 限定两种基因的功能。
- 结构：
  - 主句：A federal appeals court overturned the prior decision
  -   └ 现在分词短语作结果/伴随状语：ruling
  -       └ 宾语从句(that)：that Myriad Genetics could indeed hold patents to two genes
  -           └ 定语从句〔that，修饰 genes〕：that help forecast a woman's risk of breast cancer
- 考点：
  - appeals court 指上诉法院。
  - rule that... 指法院裁定……。
  - forecast a risk 不是预言必然患病，而是预测风险。

## 8. E2-2012-T3-P1-S8

The chief executive of Myriad, a company in Utah, said the ruling was a blessing to firms and patients alike.

**译文：** 这家犹他州公司的首席执行官表示，该裁决对企业和患者都是福音。

## 9. E2-2012-T3-P1-S9

But as companies continue their attempts at personalised medicine, the courts will remain rather busy.

**译文：** 但随着企业继续尝试发展个性化医疗，法院仍会相当忙碌。

## 10. E2-2012-T3-P1-S10

The Myriad case itself is probably not over.

**译文：** 万基案本身很可能尚未结束。

## 11. E2-2012-T3-P1-S11

Critics make three main arguments against gene patents: a gene is a product of nature, so it may not be patented; gene patents suppress innovation rather than reward it; and patents' monopolies restrict access to genetic tests such as Myriad's.

**译文：** 批评者反对基因专利主要有三条理由：基因是自然产物，因而不应获专利；基因专利压制创新，而非奖励创新；专利造成的垄断会限制人们获得万基公司这类基因检测服务。

**长难句拆解：**

- 主干：S+V+O：Critics make three main arguments against gene patents（批评者提出三条反对基因专利的理由）
- 逻辑：冒号引出三项平行论据，由分号清晰分隔；第一项内部用 so 表因果，第二项用 rather than 表对照。
- 结构：
  - 总领句：Critics make three main arguments against gene patents
  - 冒号后理由①：a gene is a product of nature, so it may not be patented
  - 理由②：gene patents suppress innovation rather than reward it
  - 理由③：patents' monopolies restrict access to genetic tests such as Myriad's
- 考点：
  - may not be patented 在法律语境中指不具备可专利性。
  - rather than：而不是。
  - Myriad's 省略了 genetic tests，指万基公司的基因检测。

## 12. E2-2012-T3-P1-S12

A growing number seem to agree.

**译文：** 似乎有越来越多的人赞同这些观点。

## 13. E2-2012-T3-P1-S13

Last year a federal task-force urged reform for patents related to genetic tests.

**译文：** 去年，一个联邦工作组敦促对与基因检测有关的专利制度进行改革。

## 14. E2-2012-T3-P1-S14

In October the Department of Justice filed a brief in the Myriad case, arguing that an isolated DNA molecule "is no less a product of nature…than are cotton fibres that have been separated from cotton seeds."

**译文：** 10月，美国司法部在万基案中提交了一份意见书，主张分离出的DNA分子“与从棉籽中分离出的棉纤维相比，同样都是自然产物”。

## 15. E2-2012-T3-P1-S15

Despite the appeals court's decision, big questions remain unanswered.

**译文：** 尽管上诉法院已经作出判决，重大问题仍未得到解答。

## 16. E2-2012-T3-P1-S16

For example, it is unclear whether the sequencing of a whole genome violates the patents of individual genes within it.

**译文：** 例如，对完整基因组进行测序是否侵犯其中单个基因的专利，目前尚不明确。

## 17. E2-2012-T3-P1-S17

The case may yet reach the Supreme Court.

**译文：** 此案最终仍可能提交最高法院审理。

## 18. E2-2012-T3-P1-S18

As the industry advances, however, other suits may have an even greater impact.

**译文：** 然而，随着行业发展，其他诉讼可能产生更为重大的影响。

## 19. E2-2012-T3-P1-S19

Companies are unlikely to file many more patents for human DNA molecules - most are already patented or in the public domain.

**译文：** 企业不太可能再为人类DNA分子申请太多新专利，因为大多数分子要么已经获得专利，要么已进入公有领域。

## 20. E2-2012-T3-P1-S20

Firms are now studying how genes interact, looking for correlations that might be used to determine the causes of disease or predict a drug's efficacy.

**译文：** 企业如今正在研究基因如何相互作用，寻找可用于确定疾病成因或预测药物疗效的关联。

## 21. E2-2012-T3-P1-S21

Companies are eager to win patents for "connecting the dots," explains Hans Sauer, a lawyer for the BIO.

**译文：** BIO的律师汉斯·绍尔解释说，企业都急于为“把各个点连接起来”申请专利。

## 22. E2-2012-T3-P1-S22

Their success may be determined by a suit related to this issue, brought by the Mayo Clinic, which the Supreme Court will hear in its next term.

**译文：** 它们能否成功，可能取决于梅奥诊所就这一问题提起的一宗诉讼；最高法院将在下一个审期审理此案。

**长难句拆解：**

- 主干：S+V：Their success may be determined by a suit（它们能否成功可能由一宗诉讼决定）
- 逻辑：两个后置成分连续修饰 suit：先说明起诉方是梅奥诊所，再说明最高法院将在下一审期审理。
- 结构：
  - 主句：Their success may be determined by a suit related to this issue
  -   ├ 过去分词短语〔修饰 suit〕：brought by the Mayo Clinic
  -   └ 非限制性定语从句〔which，修饰 suit〕：which the Supreme Court will hear in its next term
- 考点：
  - Their success 回指企业取得“连接各点”相关专利的成功与否。
  - bring a suit 指提起诉讼。
  - hear a case 指法院审理案件，不是单纯“听取”。

## 23. E2-2012-T3-P1-S23

The BIO recently held a convention which included sessions to coach lawyers on the shifting landscape for patents.

**译文：** BIO最近举行了一次大会，其中设有若干培训环节，指导律师应对不断变化的专利形势。

## 24. E2-2012-T3-P1-S24

Each meeting was packed.

**译文：** 每场会议都座无虚席。
