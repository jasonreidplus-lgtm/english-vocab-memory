# 2010 年考研英语二阅读 Text 4 · 逐句精读

## 1. E2-2010-T4-P1-S1

Many Americans regard the jury system as a concrete expression of crucial democratic values, including the principles that all citizens who meet minimal qualifications of age and literacy are equally competent to serve on juries; that jurors should be selected randomly from a representative cross section of the community; that no citizen should be denied the right to serve on a jury on account of race, religion, sex, or national origin; that defendants are entitled to trial by their peers; and that verdicts should represent the conscience of the community and not just the letter of the law.

**译文：** 许多美国人把陪审团制度视为重要民主价值观的具体体现，其中包括以下原则：凡年龄和读写能力达到最低要求的公民，都同样有能力担任陪审员；陪审员应从具有社区代表性的各阶层中随机选出；任何公民都不应因种族、宗教、性别或国籍而被剥夺担任陪审员的权利；被告有权接受同等身份者的审判；裁决应代表社区的良知，而不应只是拘泥于法律条文。

**长难句拆解：**

- 主干：S+V+O+C：Many Americans regard the jury system as an expression of values（许多美国人把陪审团制度视为民主价值观的体现）
- 逻辑：主干之后由 including 引出 principles，五个平行的 that 从句逐项说明民主原则；分号是并列项目的分隔标志。
- 结构：
  - 主句：Many Americans regard the jury system as a concrete expression of crucial democratic values
  -   └ 补充说明(including)：including the principles
  -       ├ 同位语从句①(that)：all citizens who meet minimal qualifications ... are equally competent to serve on juries
  -       ├ 同位语从句②(that)：jurors should be selected randomly from a representative cross section of the community
  -       ├ 同位语从句③(that)：no citizen should be denied the right ... on account of race, religion, sex, or national origin
  -       ├ 同位语从句④(that)：defendants are entitled to trial by their peers
  -       └ 同位语从句⑤(that)：verdicts should represent the conscience of the community and not just the letter of the law
- 考点：
  - regard A as B：把A视为B。
  - who meet... 是修饰 all citizens 的定语从句。
  - the conscience of the community 与 the letter of the law 构成“社会良知—法律字面”的对照。

## 2. E2-2010-T4-P1-S2

The jury is also said to be the best surviving example of direct rather than representative democracy.

**译文：** 陪审团还被称为现存最好的直接民主实例，而非代议制民主的实例。

## 3. E2-2010-T4-P1-S3

In a direct democracy, citizens take turns governing themselves, rather than electing representatives to govern for them.

**译文：** 在直接民主制度中，公民轮流管理自身事务，而不是选出代表替他们治理。

## 4. E2-2010-T4-P1-S4

But as recently as in 1968, jury selection procedures conflicted with these democratic ideals.

**译文：** 然而，直到不久前的1968年，陪审员遴选程序仍与这些民主理想相冲突。

## 5. E2-2010-T4-P1-S5

In some states, for example, jury duty was limited to persons of supposedly superior intelligence, education, and moral character.

**译文：** 例如，在一些州，只有那些被认为智力、教育水平和道德品质更优越的人才有资格履行陪审义务。

## 6. E2-2010-T4-P1-S6

Although the Supreme Court of the United States had prohibited intentional racial discrimination in jury selection as early as the 1880 case of Strauder v. West Virginia, the practice of selecting so-called elite or blue-ribbon juries provided a convenient way around this and other antidiscrimination laws.

**译文：** 尽管早在1880年的“斯特劳德诉西弗吉尼亚州案”中，美国最高法院就已禁止在遴选陪审员时进行蓄意的种族歧视，但遴选所谓精英陪审团或“蓝丝带陪审团”的做法，为规避这项及其他反歧视法律提供了便利途径。

**长难句拆解：**

- 主干：让步复句主干：Although the Supreme Court had prohibited discrimination, the practice provided a way around laws（尽管最高法院已禁止歧视，这种做法仍提供了规避法律的途径）
- 逻辑：Although 建立“法律已明令禁止—实际仍可绕开”的让步转折；主句真正的施事是 the practice，而不是 juries。
- 结构：
  - 让步状语从句(Although)：the Supreme Court of the United States had prohibited intentional racial discrimination in jury selection
  -   └ 时间/案件背景：as early as the 1880 case of Strauder v. West Virginia
  - 主句：the practice ... provided a convenient way around this and other antidiscrimination laws
  -   └ 动名词短语作后置说明〔修饰 practice〕：of selecting so-called elite or blue-ribbon juries
- 考点：
  - as early as 强调“早在……”。
  - a way around laws 指规避法律的方法，并非围绕法律。
  - blue-ribbon jury 指由所谓优秀或精英人士组成的陪审团。

## 7. E2-2010-T4-P1-S7

The system also failed to regularly include women on juries until the mid-20th century.

**译文：** 直到20世纪中叶，这一制度也一直未能经常把女性纳入陪审团。

## 8. E2-2010-T4-P1-S8

Although women first served on state juries in Utah in 1898, it was not until the 1940s that a majority of states made women eligible for jury duty.

**译文：** 虽然早在1898年，女性就在犹他州首次担任州法院陪审员，但直到20世纪40年代，大多数州才允许女性履行陪审义务。

## 9. E2-2010-T4-P1-S9

Even then several states automatically exempted women from jury duty unless they personally asked to have their names included on the jury list.

**译文：** 即便到了那时，仍有几个州会自动免除女性的陪审义务，除非她们亲自要求把自己的名字列入陪审员名单。

## 10. E2-2010-T4-P1-S10

This practice was justified by the claim that women were needed at home, and it kept juries unrepresentative of women through the 1960s.

**译文：** 这种做法以“家庭需要女性”为由获得辩护，并使陪审团在整个20世纪60年代始终无法充分代表女性。

## 11. E2-2010-T4-P1-S11

In 1968, the Congress of the United States passed the Jury Selection and Service Act, ushering in a new era of democratic reforms for the jury.

**译文：** 1968年，美国国会通过《陪审员遴选与服务法》，由此开启了陪审团民主改革的新时代。

## 12. E2-2010-T4-P1-S12

This law abolished special educational requirements for federal jurors and required them to be selected at random from a cross section of the entire community.

**译文：** 该法废除了对联邦陪审员的特殊教育要求，并规定陪审员必须从整个社区的各阶层中随机选出。

## 13. E2-2010-T4-P1-S13

In the landmark 1975 decision Taylor v. Louisiana, the Supreme Court extended the requirement that juries be representative of all parts of the community to the state level.

**译文：** 在具有里程碑意义的1975年“泰勒诉路易斯安那州案”判决中，最高法院把陪审团必须代表社区所有群体这一要求扩展到了州一级。

**长难句拆解：**

- 主干：S+V+O：The Supreme Court extended the requirement to the state level（最高法院把该要求扩展到州一级）
- 逻辑：句首介词短语交代案件；that 从句不是普通宾语从句，而是说明 requirement 的具体内容；extend A to B 表示把要求适用到州层面。
- 结构：
  - 背景状语：In the landmark 1975 decision Taylor v. Louisiana
  - 主句：the Supreme Court extended the requirement ... to the state level
  -   └ 同位语从句(that)〔说明 requirement〕：that juries be representative of all parts of the community
- 考点：
  - landmark decision 指具有里程碑意义的判决。
  - requirement 后的 that 从句使用 be 原形，体现要求/规定类名词后的虚拟语气。
  - all parts of the community 指社区各个群体或阶层。

## 14. E2-2010-T4-P1-S14

The Taylor decision also declared sex discrimination in jury selection to be unconstitutional and ordered states to use the same procedures for selecting male and female jurors.

**译文：** 泰勒案判决还宣布，陪审员遴选中的性别歧视违反宪法，并命令各州采用相同程序遴选男性和女性陪审员。
