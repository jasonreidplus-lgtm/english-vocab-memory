# 2014 年考研英语二阅读 Text 3 · 逐句精读

## 1. E2-2014-T3-P1-S1

The concept of man versus machine is at least as old as the industrial revolution, but this phenomenon tends to be most acutely felt during economic downturns and fragile recoveries.

**译文：** 人与机器对抗的概念至少和工业革命一样古老，但在经济衰退和脆弱复苏时期，人们往往最强烈地感受到这一现象。

## 2. E2-2014-T3-P1-S2

And yet, it would be a mistake to think we are right now simply experiencing the painful side of a boom and bust cycle.

**译文：** 然而，如果以为我们眼下只是在经历繁荣—萧条周期中痛苦的一面，那就错了。

## 3. E2-2014-T3-P1-S3

Certain jobs have gone away for good, outmoded by machines.

**译文：** 某些工作已经永远消失，被机器淘汰了。

## 4. E2-2014-T3-P1-S4

Since technology has such an insatiable appetite for eating up human jobs, this phenomenon will continue to restructure our economy in ways we cannot immediately foresee.

**译文：** 由于技术对吞噬人类工作岗位有着永不满足的胃口，这一现象将继续以我们一时无法预见的方式重塑经济。

**长难句拆解：**

- 主干：主干：this phenomenon will continue to restructure our economy
- 逻辑：Since 给出持续重构的动力——技术不断吞噬岗位；主句进一步强调这种重构方式目前难以预见。
- 结构：
  - 原因状语从句(Since)：Since technology has such an insatiable appetite for eating up human jobs
  - 主句谓语：will continue to restructure
  - 方式介词短语：in ways
  - 定语从句〔省略 that，修饰 ways〕：we cannot immediately foresee
- 考点：
  - since 在此表示原因，不是“自从”。
  - have an appetite for 是比喻性表达，指强烈且持续的需求。
  - eat up human jobs 指技术大量取代就业岗位。

## 5. E2-2014-T3-P1-S5

When there is rapid improvement in the price and performance of technology, jobs that were once thought to be immune from automation suddenly become threatened.

**译文：** 当技术的价格和性能迅速改善时，曾被认为不会受到自动化影响的工作会突然面临威胁。

## 6. E2-2014-T3-P1-S6

This argument has attracted a lot of attention, via the success of the book Race Against the Machine, by Erik Brynjolfsson and Andrew McAfee, who both hail from MIT's Center for Digital Business.

**译文：** 埃里克·布莱恩约尔松和安德鲁·麦卡菲合著的《与机器赛跑》大获成功，使这一论点受到广泛关注；两位作者都来自麻省理工学院数字商业中心。

**长难句拆解：**

- 主干：主干：This argument has attracted a lot of attention
- 逻辑：句子先说明论点受到关注，再把关注度归因于相关著作的成功，最后补充两位作者的机构背景以增强权威性。
- 结构：
  - 方式/原因补充：via the success of the book Race Against the Machine
  - 同位作者信息：by Erik Brynjolfsson and Andrew McAfee
  - 非限制性定语从句(who，修饰两位作者)：who both hail from MIT's Center for Digital Business
- 考点：
  - via 表示“通过、凭借”。
  - by ... 说明书的作者。
  - hail from 在此意为“来自”，并非“向……欢呼”。

## 7. E2-2014-T3-P1-S7

This is a powerful argument, and a scary one.

**译文：** 这是一个有力的论点，也是一个令人恐惧的论点。

## 8. E2-2014-T3-P1-S8

And yet, John Hagel, author of The Power of Pull and other books, says Brynjolfsson and McAfee miss the reason why these jobs are so vulnerable to technology in the first place.

**译文：** 然而，《拉动力》等书的作者约翰·哈格尔认为，布莱恩约尔松和麦卡菲忽略了这些工作最初为何如此容易受到技术冲击。

## 9. E2-2014-T3-P1-S9

Hagel says we have designed jobs in the U.S. that tend to be "tightly scripted" and "highly standardized" ones that leave no room for "individual initiative or creativity".

**译文：** 哈格尔说，我们在美国设计出的工作往往“按严格脚本执行”且“高度标准化”，不给“个人主动性或创造力”留下任何空间。

**长难句拆解：**

- 主干：宾语从句主干：Hagel says (that) we have designed jobs in the U.S.
- 逻辑：哈格尔把岗位易被机器替代的根源归于岗位设计本身：流程被严密规定并高度标准化，排除了人的主动性和创造性。
- 结构：
  - says 后省略连接词 that
  - 定语从句①(that，修饰 jobs)：that tend to be 'tightly scripted' and 'highly standardized' ones
  - 并列表语：tightly scripted / highly standardized
  - 定语从句②(that，修饰 ones/jobs)：that leave no room for individual initiative or creativity
- 考点：
  - scripted 在此不是“写了剧本”，而是“按预设流程执行”。
  - leave no room for 表示“不给……留下空间”。
  - ones 回指 jobs，用于承接后面的定语从句。

## 10. E2-2014-T3-P1-S10

In short, these are the types of jobs that machines can perform much better at than human beings.

**译文：** 简言之，机器完成这类工作的能力远胜于人类。

## 11. E2-2014-T3-P1-S11

That is how we have put a giant target sign on the backs of American workers, Hagel says.

**译文：** 哈格尔说，正是这样，我们在美国劳动者背后放上了一个巨大的靶标。

## 12. E2-2014-T3-P1-S12

It's time to reinvent the formula for how work is conducted, since we are still relying on a very 20th century notion of work, Hagel says.

**译文：** 哈格尔认为，我们仍依赖一种非常具有20世纪色彩的工作观念，因此现在该重新设计工作的运行方式了。

## 13. E2-2014-T3-P1-S13

In our rapidly changing economy, we more than ever need people in the workplace who can take initiative and exercise their imagination "to respond to unexpected events".

**译文：** 在迅速变化的经济中，我们比以往任何时候都更需要能够主动行动、发挥想象力来“应对突发事件”的职场人士。

## 14. E2-2014-T3-P1-S14

That is not something machines are good at.

**译文：** 这并不是机器擅长的事情。

## 15. E2-2014-T3-P1-S15

They are designed to perform very predictable activities.

**译文：** 机器被设计用来执行高度可预测的活动。

## 16. E2-2014-T3-P1-S16

As Hagel notes, Brynjolfsson and McAfee indeed touched on this point in their book.

**译文：** 正如哈格尔指出的，布莱恩约尔松和麦卡菲确实在书中谈到了这一点。

## 17. E2-2014-T3-P1-S17

We need to reframe race against the machine as race with the machine.

**译文：** 我们需要把“与机器对抗的竞赛”重新表述为“与机器协作的竞赛”。

## 18. E2-2014-T3-P1-S18

In other words, we need to look at the ways in which machines can augment human labor rather than replace it.

**译文：** 换言之，我们应当研究机器如何增强人类劳动，而不是取代人类劳动。

## 19. E2-2014-T3-P1-S19

So then the problem is not really about technology, but rather, "how do we innovate our institutions and our work practices?"

**译文：** 因此，问题其实不在技术本身，而在于“我们该如何革新制度和工作实践？”
