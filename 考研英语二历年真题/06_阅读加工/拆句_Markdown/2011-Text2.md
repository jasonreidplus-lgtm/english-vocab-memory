# 2011 年考研英语二阅读 Text 2 · 逐句精读

## 1. E2-2011-T2-P1-S1

Whatever happened to the death of newspapers?

**译文：** 报纸不是说要消亡吗，后来怎么样了？

## 2. E2-2011-T2-P1-S2

**译文：** 一年前，末日似乎近在眼前。

## 3. E2-2011-T2-P1-S3

**译文：** 经济衰退威胁着要夺走那些尚未转向互联网的广告商和读者。

## 4. E2-2011-T2-P1-S4

Newspapers like the San Francisco Chronicle were chronicling their own doom.

**译文：** 《旧金山纪事报》等报纸当时正在报道自己的末日。

## 5. E2-2011-T2-P1-S5

America's Federal Trade Commission launched a round of talks about how to save newspapers.

**译文：** 美国联邦贸易委员会发起了一轮讨论，主题是如何挽救报纸。

## 6. E2-2011-T2-P1-S6

## 7. E2-2011-T2-P1-S7

**译文：** 政府是否应该补贴它们？

## 8. E2-2011-T2-P1-S8

It will hold another meeting soon.

**译文：** 该委员会很快还将再举行一次会议。

## 9. E2-2011-T2-P1-S9

**译文：** 然而，这些讨论如今似乎已经过时。

## 10. E2-2011-T2-P1-S10

In much of the world there is little sign of crisis.

**译文：** 在世界许多地方，几乎看不到危机的迹象。

## 11. E2-2011-T2-P1-S11

German and Brazilian papers have shrugged off the recession.

**译文：** 德国和巴西的报纸已经摆脱了经济衰退的影响。

## 12. E2-2011-T2-P1-S12

Even American newspapers, which inhabit the most troubled corner of the global industry, have not only survived but often returned to profit.

**译文：** 即使是身处全球报业困境最严重角落的美国报纸，也不仅生存了下来，而且往往已经恢复盈利。

**长难句拆解：**

- 主干：S+V：Even American newspapers have not only survived but returned to profit（即使美国报纸也不仅存活下来，而且恢复盈利）
- 逻辑：主句用 not only...but... 并列两个谓语；which 从句插入说明美国报纸处境最艰难，因而强化 even 所表达的出乎意料。
- 结构：
  - 主句：Even American newspapers have not only survived but often returned to profit
  -   └ 非限制性定语从句〔which，修饰 American newspapers〕：which inhabit the most troubled corner of the global industry
- 考点：
  - inhabit the most troubled corner 是比喻，意为处在行业困境最严重的部分。
  - return to profit 指恢复盈利。
  - not only 与 but 后分别对应 survived 和 returned。

## 16. E2-2011-T2-P1-S16

The American Society of News Editors reckons that 13,500 newsroom jobs have gone since 2007.

**译文：** 美国新闻编辑协会估计，自2007年以来，新闻编辑部已经减少了13500个职位。

## 17. E2-2011-T2-P1-S17

Readers are paying more for slimmer products.

## 18. E2-2011-T2-P1-S18

Some papers even had the nerve to refuse delivery to distant suburbs.

**译文：** 有些报纸甚至胆敢拒绝向偏远郊区送报。

## 19. E2-2011-T2-P1-S19

Yet these desperate measures have proved the right ones and, sadly for many journalists, they can be pushed further.

**译文：** 然而事实证明，这些孤注一掷的措施是正确的；令许多记者难过的是，这些措施还可以进一步推进。

**长难句拆解：**

- 主干：并列主干：These measures have proved the right ones, and they can be pushed further（这些措施已证明正确，而且还可进一步实施）
- 逻辑：Yet 承接前文那些令人不快的削减措施并转折评价其有效；and 后继续指出还有进一步削减空间，插入语点出记者将承担代价。
- 结构：
  - 分句①：Yet these desperate measures have proved the right ones
  - 并列分句②(and)：they can be pushed further
  -   └ 评注插入语：sadly for many journalists
- 考点：
  - prove + 名词补语：事实证明是……。
  - the right ones 中 ones 替代 measures。
  - push measures further 指把措施进一步推进或加码。

## 20. E2-2011-T2-P1-S20

Newspapers are becoming more balanced businesses, with a healthier mix of revenues from readers and advertisers.

**译文：** 报纸正在成为收入结构更加均衡的企业，来自读者和广告商的收入组合也更加健康。

## 21. E2-2011-T2-P1-S21

American papers have long been highly unusual in their reliance on ads.

**译文：** 长期以来，美国报纸对广告的依赖程度一直高得异乎寻常。

## 22. E2-2011-T2-P1-S22

Fully 87% of their revenues came from advertising in 2008, according to the Organization for Economic Cooperation & Development (OECD).

**译文：** 经济合作与发展组织（OECD）的数据显示，2008年，美国报纸足足有87%的收入来自广告。

## 23. E2-2011-T2-P1-S23

In Japan the proportion is 35%.

**译文：** 在日本，这一比例为35%。

## 24. E2-2011-T2-P1-S24

Not surprisingly, Japanese newspapers are much more stable.

**译文：** 毫不奇怪，日本报纸稳定得多。

## 25. E2-2011-T2-P1-S25

The whirlwind that swept through newsrooms harmed everybody, but much of the damage has been concentrated in areas where newspapers are least distinctive.

**译文：** 席卷各新闻编辑部的旋风伤害了所有人，但大部分损失集中在报纸最缺乏独特性的领域。

**长难句拆解：**

- 主干：转折复句：The whirlwind harmed everybody, but much damage has been concentrated in areas（风暴伤害所有人，但损失集中在一些领域）
- 逻辑：先用 whirlwind 比喻行业裁员冲击，再用 but 缩小范围：受损最重的是报纸最没有独特优势的内容领域。
- 结构：
  - 分句①：The whirlwind harmed everybody
  -   └ 定语从句〔that，修饰 whirlwind〕：that swept through newsrooms
  - 转折分句②(but)：much of the damage has been concentrated in areas
  -   └ 定语从句(where)〔修饰 areas〕：where newspapers are least distinctive
- 考点：
  - sweep through 表示迅速席卷。
  - newsroom 指新闻编辑部，不只是实体房间。
  - least distinctive 指最缺少报纸独有价值，而不是最不显眼。
