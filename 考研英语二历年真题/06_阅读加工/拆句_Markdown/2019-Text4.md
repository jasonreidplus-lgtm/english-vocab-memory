# 2019 年考研英语二阅读 Text 4 · 逐句精读

## 1. E2-2019-T4-P1-S1

Arnold Schwarzenegger, Dia Mirza and Adrian Grenier have a message for you: It's easy to beat plastic.

**译文：** 阿诺德·施瓦辛格、迪娅·米尔扎和阿德里安·格雷尼尔要向你传达一个信息：战胜塑料很容易。

## 2. E2-2019-T4-P1-S2

They're part of a bunch of celebrities starring in a new video for World Environment Day - encouraging you, the consumer, to swap out your single-use plastic staples like straws and cutlery to combat the plastics crisis.

**译文：** 他们是一群出演世界环境日新视频的名人中的一员；该视频鼓励作为消费者的你更换吸管、餐具等一次性塑料日用品，以应对塑料危机。

## 3. E2-2019-T4-P1-S3

The key messages that have been put together for World Environment Day do include a call for governments to enact legislation to curb single-use plastics.

**译文：** 为世界环境日汇总的关键信息确实包括呼吁各国政府制定法律，遏制一次性塑料制品。

## 4. E2-2019-T4-P1-S4

But the overarching message is directed at individuals.

**译文：** 但总的信息是面向个人的。

## 5. E2-2019-T4-P1-S5

My concern with leaving it up to the individual, however, is our limited sense of what needs to be achieved.

**译文：** 然而，我对把事情交给个人来做的担忧在于，我们对于必须实现什么目标认识有限。

## 6. E2-2019-T4-P1-S6

On their own, taking our own bags to the grocery store or quitting plastic straws, for example, will accomplish little and require very little of us.

**译文：** 例如，单靠我们自己带购物袋去食品店或放弃塑料吸管，收效甚微，也不需要我们付出多少。

## 7. E2-2019-T4-P1-S7

They could even be detrimental, satisfying a need to have "done our bit" without ever progressing onto bigger, bolder, more effective actions - a kind of "moral licensing" that allays our concerns and stops us doing more and asking more of those in charge.

**译文：** 这些行动甚至可能产生负面影响：它们满足了我们“尽到自己一份力”的需要，却从未推进到更大、更大胆、更有效的行动；这是一种“道德许可”，会缓解我们的担忧，使我们不再做得更多，也不再向当权者提出更多要求。

**长难句拆解：**

- 主干：S+V+C：They could even be detrimental（这些行动甚至可能有害）
- 逻辑：句子先判断小行动可能有害，再用分词和 without 说明其心理机制，最后用同位语定义“道德许可”，并由 that 从句展开后果。
- 结构：
  - 主句：They could even be detrimental
  -   └ 结果/伴随分词：satisfying a need
  -       ├ 不定式作后置定语：to have done our bit
  -       └ without 介词结构：without ever progressing onto bigger... actions
  - 同位解释：a kind of moral licensing
  -   └ 定语从句(that)：that allays our concerns and stops us doing more and asking more of those in charge
- 考点：
  - do our bit：尽自己的一份力。
  - allay concerns：缓解担忧。
  - those in charge：掌权者、负责人。

## 8. E2-2019-T4-P1-S8

While the conversation around our environment and our responsibility toward it remains centered on shopping bags and straws, we're ignoring the balance of power that implies that as "consumers" we must shop sustainably, rather than as "citizens" hold our governments and industries to account to push for real systemic change.

**译文：** 当有关环境及我们对环境责任的讨论仍围绕购物袋和吸管展开时，我们忽视了其中的权力关系：它暗示我们必须以“消费者”身份进行可持续购物，而不是以“公民”身份要求政府和行业承担责任、推动真正的系统性变革。

**长难句拆解：**

- 主干：时间/让步复句：While the conversation remains centered..., we're ignoring the balance of power（当讨论仍聚焦……时，我们忽视了权力关系）
- 逻辑：while 把表层环保讨论与被忽视的权力结构对照；两个 that 依次修饰 balance 并给出其隐含要求，rather than 对比消费者和公民两种身份。
- 结构：
  - while 状语从句：the conversation ... remains centered on shopping bags and straws
  - 主句：we're ignoring the balance of power
  -   └ 定语从句(that)：that implies
  -       └ 宾语从句(that)：as consumers we must shop sustainably, rather than as citizens hold our governments and industries to account
  -           └ 目的不定式：to push for real systemic change
- 考点：
  - remain centered on：仍以……为中心。
  - hold somebody to account：要求某人承担责任。
  - systemic change：系统性变革。

## 9. E2-2019-T4-P1-S9

It's important to acknowledge that the environment isn't everyone's priority - or even most people's.

**译文：** 重要的是要承认，环境并非每个人的优先事项，甚至也不是大多数人的优先事项。

## 10. E2-2019-T4-P1-S10

We shouldn't expect it to be.

**译文：** 我们不应期待它成为所有人的优先事项。

## 11. E2-2019-T4-P1-S11

In her latest book, Why Good People Do Bad Environmental Things, Wellesley College professor Elizabeth R. DeSombre argues that the best way to collectively change the behavior of large numbers of people is for the change to be structural.

**译文：** 韦尔斯利学院教授伊丽莎白·R. 德松布尔在其新作《好人为何做出有害环境之事》中主张，要集体改变大量人群的行为，最佳方式是让这种改变具有结构性。

**长难句拆解：**

- 主干：S+V+O从句：Elizabeth R. DeSombre argues that...（德松布尔主张……）
- 逻辑：that 从句使用“the best way is...”框架；两个不定式分别说明要改变什么，以及最佳方式的结构性本质。
- 结构：
  - 主句：Wellesley College professor Elizabeth R. DeSombre argues
  -   └ 宾语从句(that)：the best way ... is for the change to be structural
  -       ├ 不定式作后置定语：to collectively change the behavior of large numbers of people
  -       └ 表语：for the change to be structural
- 考点：
  - large numbers of people：大量人群。
  - structural change：通过制度或结构实现的改变。
  - for + 名词 + to do 是不定式的逻辑主语结构。

## 12. E2-2019-T4-P1-S12

This might mean implementing policy such as a plastic tax that adds a cost to environmentally problematic action, or banning single-use plastics altogether.

**译文：** 这可能意味着实施塑料税等政策，给危害环境的行为增加成本，或者彻底禁止一次性塑料制品。

**译文：** 印度刚刚宣布，将“在2022年前消除该国所有一次性塑料”。

## 14. E2-2019-T4-P1-S14

There are also incentive-based ways of making better environmental choices easier, such as ensuring recycling is at least as easy as trash disposal.

**译文：** 也有一些以激励为基础的方法可以让更环保的选择变得更容易，例如确保回收至少与丢弃垃圾一样方便。

DeSombre isn't saying people should stop caring about the environment.

**译文：** 德松布尔并不是说人们应该停止关心环境。

**译文：** 她说，只是个人行动过于缓慢，不能把它作为改变普遍行为的唯一途径，甚至不能作为主要途径。

## 17. E2-2019-T4-P1-S17

None of this is about writing off the individual.

**译文：** 所有这些都不是要否定个人。

## 18. E2-2019-T4-P1-S18

## 19. E2-2019-T4-P1-S19

We don't have time to wait.

**译文：** 我们没有时间等待。

We need progressive policies that shape collective action (and rein in polluting businesses), alongside engaged citizens pushing for change.

**译文：** 我们需要能够塑造集体行动并约束污染企业的进步政策，同时也需要积极参与、推动变革的公民。
