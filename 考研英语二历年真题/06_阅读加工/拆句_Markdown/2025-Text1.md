# 2025 年考研英语二阅读 Text 1 · 逐句精读

## 1. E2-2025-T1-P1-S1

U.S. customers historically tipped people they assumed were earning most of their income via tips, such as restaurant servers earning less than the minimum wage.

**译文：** 过去，美国顾客会给那些被认为主要依靠小费获得收入的人付小费，例如工资低于最低工资标准的餐厅服务员。

## 2. E2-2025-T1-P1-S2

In the early 2010s, a wide range of businesses started processing purchases with iPads and other digital payment systems.

**译文：** 2010年代初，各类商家开始使用iPad和其他数字支付系统处理消费付款。

## 3. E2-2025-T1-P1-S3

These systems often prompted customers to tip for services that were not previously tipped.

**译文：** 这些系统常常会提示顾客为过去不用给小费的服务支付小费。

## 4. E2-2025-T1-P1-S4

Today's tip requests are often not connected to the salary and service norms that used to determine when and how people tip.

**译文：** 如今的索要小费往往已脱离过去决定人们何时以及如何给小费的工资和服务惯例。

## 5. E2-2025-T1-P1-S5

Customers in the past nearly always paid tips after receiving a service, such as at the conclusion of a restaurant meal, after getting a haircut or once a pizza was delivered.

**译文：** 过去，顾客几乎总是在接受服务之后才付小费，例如一顿餐饭结束、理完发或披萨送达之后。

## 6. E2-2025-T1-P1-S6

That timing could reward high-quality service and give workers an incentive to provide it.

**译文：** 这样的时间安排能够奖励优质服务，并激励员工提供优质服务。

## 7. E2-2025-T1-P1-S7

It's becoming more common for tips to be requested beforehand.

**译文：** 而现在，事先要求支付小费的情况越来越常见。

## 8. E2-2025-T1-P1-S8

And new tipping technology may even automatically add tips.

**译文：** 新的小费技术甚至可能自动加上小费。

## 9. E2-2025-T1-P1-S9

The prevalence of digital payment devices has made it easier to ask customers for a tip.

**译文：** 数字支付设备的普及，让商家更容易向顾客索要小费。

## 10. E2-2025-T1-P1-S10

That helps explain why tip requests are creeping into new kinds of services.

**译文：** 这有助于解释为什么索要小费正逐渐渗入新的服务类型。

## 11. E2-2025-T1-P1-S11

Customers now routinely see menus of suggested default options - often well above 20% of what they owe.

**译文：** 如今，顾客经常会看到一组建议的默认小费选项，而这些数额往往远高于应付金额的20%。

## 12. E2-2025-T1-P1-S12

The amounts have risen from 10% or less in the 1950s to 15% around the year 2000 to 20% or higher today.

**译文：** 小费比例已经从20世纪50年代的10%或更低，上升到2000年前后的15%，再升至如今的20%或更高。

## 13. E2-2025-T1-P1-S13

This increase is sometimes called tipflation - the expectation of ever-higher tip amounts.

**译文：** 这种上涨有时被称为"小费通胀"，即人们被期待支付越来越高的小费。

## 14. E2-2025-T1-P1-S14

Tipping has always been a vital source of income for workers in historically tipped services, like restaurants, where the tipped minimum wage can be as low as US$2.13 an hour.

**译文：** 小费一直是传统上依赖小费的服务业劳动者的重要收入来源，例如餐饮业；这些行业中领取小费者的最低时薪可低至2.13美元。

**长难句拆解：**

- 主干：Tipping has been a vital source of income（小费一直是重要收入来源）
- 逻辑：主句说明小费的重要性；in短语限定传统依赖小费的行业，where从句再用极低的法定时薪解释为什么小费对这些劳动者至关重要。
- 结构：
  - 主句：Tipping has always been a vital source of income for workers
  -   ├ 介词短语〔限定 workers〕：in historically tipped services
  -   ├ 举例：like restaurants
  -   └ 非限制性定语从句(where)：where the tipped minimum wage can be as low as US$2.13 an hour
- 考点：
  - tipped services 指按惯例会给从业者小费的服务行业。
  - tipped minimum wage 指适用于领取小费劳动者的最低工资。
  - as low as 强调数额低至某一水平。

## 15. E2-2025-T1-P1-S15

Tip creep and tipflation are now further supplementing the income of many low-wage service workers.

**译文：** 小费适用范围扩张和小费通胀，如今正进一步补充许多低工资服务业劳动者的收入。

## 16. E2-2025-T1-P1-S16

Notably, tipping primarily benefits some of these workers, such as waiters, but not others, such as cooks and dishwashers.

**译文：** 值得注意的是，小费主要让其中一部分劳动者受益，例如服务员，却没有惠及另一部分人，例如厨师和洗碗工。

## 17. E2-2025-T1-P1-S17

To ensure that all employees were paid fair wages, some restaurants banned tipping and increased prices, but this movement towards no-tipping services has largely fizzled out.

**译文：** 为了确保所有员工都获得公平工资，一些餐厅曾禁止收取小费并提高价格，但这场转向无小费服务的运动基本已经偃旗息鼓。

**长难句拆解：**

- 主干：Some restaurants banned tipping and increased prices, but this movement has fizzled out（一些餐厅禁收小费并涨价，但这场运动已式微）
- 逻辑：不定式先说明改革目的，主句给出禁小费和涨价两项做法，but随后指出这一改革趋势最终没有延续。
- 结构：
  - 目的状语(to)：To ensure that all employees were paid fair wages
  -   └ 宾语从句(that)：that all employees were paid fair wages
  - 并列分句一：some restaurants banned tipping and increased prices
  - 转折分句二(but)：this movement towards no-tipping services has largely fizzled out
- 考点：
  - ensure that 意为“确保……”。
  - movement towards 指“向某种模式转变的运动或趋势”。
  - fizzle out 意为“逐渐失败、不了了之”。

## 18. E2-2025-T1-P1-S18

So to increase employee wages without raising prices, more employers are succumbing to temptations of tip creep and tipflation.

**译文：** 因此，为了在不涨价的情况下提高员工工资，越来越多雇主开始屈从于扩大小费范围和推高小费的诱惑。

## 19. E2-2025-T1-P1-S19

However, many customers are frustrated because they feel they are being asked for too high of a tip too often.

**译文：** 然而，许多顾客感到恼火，因为他们觉得自己被过于频繁地要求支付过高的小费。

## 20. E2-2025-T1-P1-S20

And, as our research emphasizes, tipping now seems to be more coercive, less generous, and often completely dissociated from service quality.

**译文：** 而且，正如我们的研究所强调的，如今付小费似乎更带有强迫性、更少出于慷慨，并且往往与服务质量完全脱节。

**长难句拆解：**

- 主干：Tipping seems to be more coercive, less generous, and dissociated from service quality（付小费似乎更具强迫性、更少出于慷慨且与服务质量脱节）
- 逻辑：句子用三个并列表语概括现代小费制度的变化：压力增强、慷慨意味减弱、与服务质量失去联系；插入语标明这是研究结论。
- 结构：
  - 主句：tipping now seems to be more coercive, less generous, and often completely dissociated from service quality
  -   ├ 插入状语：as our research emphasizes
  -   └ 并列表语：more coercive / less generous / dissociated from service quality
- 考点：
  - coercive 指带有强迫或施压性质。
  - dissociated from 意为“与……分离、脱节”。
  - less generous 描述给小费不再主要是自愿慷慨行为。
