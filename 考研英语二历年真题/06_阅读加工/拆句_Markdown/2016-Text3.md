# 2016 年考研英语二阅读 Text 3 · 逐句精读

## 1. E2-2016-T3-P1-S1

That everyone's too busy these days is a cliché.

**译文：** 如今人人都太忙，这已经是老生常谈。

## 2. E2-2016-T3-P1-S2

But one specific complaint is made especially mournfully: There's never any time to read.

**译文：** 但有一种具体的抱怨说起来尤其令人伤感：根本没有时间读书。

## 3. E2-2016-T3-P1-S3

What makes the problem thornier is that the usual time-management techniques don't seem sufficient.

**译文：** 让这个问题更加棘手的是，通常的时间管理技巧似乎并不足以解决它。

## 4. E2-2016-T3-P1-S4

The web's full of articles offering tips on making time to read: "Give up TV" or "Carry a book with you at all times."

**译文：** 网上到处都是教人如何挤出时间阅读的文章，例如“别看电视”或“随身带一本书”。

## 5. E2-2016-T3-P1-S5

But in my experience, using such methods to free up the odd 30 minutes doesn't work.

**译文：** 但依我的经验，靠这类办法腾出零散的30分钟并不起作用。

## 6. E2-2016-T3-P1-S6

Sit down to read and the flywheel of work-related thoughts keeps spinning - or else you're so exhausted that a challenging book's the last thing you need.

**译文：** 你坐下来读书，工作相关的念头却像飞轮一样不停旋转；要不就是你已经累得筋疲力尽，最不需要的恰恰是一本有难度的书。

## 7. E2-2016-T3-P1-S7

The modern mind, Tim Parks, a novelist and critic, writes, "is overwhelmingly inclined toward communication… It is not simply that one is interrupted; it is that one is actually inclined to interruption."

**译文：** 小说家兼评论家蒂姆·帕克斯写道，现代人的头脑“极度倾向于交流……问题不只在于一个人会被打断，还在于他实际上乐于被打断”。

**长难句拆解：**

- 主干：引语主干：The modern mind is overwhelmingly inclined toward communication（现代人的头脑极度倾向于交流）
- 逻辑：作者署名插在主语和谓语之间；后半用not simply that A; it is that B强调问题不只是被动受扰，更是主动倾向于中断。
- 结构：
  - 引语主语：The modern mind
  - 插入语：Tim Parks, a novelist and critic, writes
  - 引语谓语：is overwhelmingly inclined toward communication
  - 解释性并列：It is not simply that one is interrupted; it is that one is actually inclined to interruption
- 考点：
  - 读句时先移开插入语，把The modern mind与is复位。
  - be inclined toward表示“倾向于”。
  - interrupted强调被动事件，inclined to interruption强调心理倾向。

## 8. E2-2016-T3-P1-S8

Deep reading requires not just time, but a special kind of time which can't be obtained merely by becoming more efficient.

**译文：** 深度阅读不仅需要时间，还需要一种特殊的时间，而这种时间无法仅靠提高效率来获得。

## 9. E2-2016-T3-P1-S9

In fact, "becoming more efficient" is part of the problem.

**译文：** 事实上，“提高效率”本身就是问题的一部分。

## 10. E2-2016-T3-P1-S10

Thinking of time as a resource to be maximised means you approach it instrumentally, judging any given moment as well spent only in so far as it advances progress toward some goal.

**译文：** 把时间看成一种需要最大限度利用的资源，意味着你会以工具性的方式对待它；只有当某一刻推动你朝某个目标前进时，你才会认为这一刻没有虚度。

**长难句拆解：**

- 主干：主句：Thinking of time as a resource means you approach it instrumentally（把时间看成资源意味着以工具性方式对待时间）
- 逻辑：动名词主语提出一种时间观；means引出后果；judging补充说明这种工具性态度如何评价每一刻。
- 结构：
  - 动名词短语作主语：Thinking of time as a resource
  -   └ 不定式作后置定语：to be maximised〔修饰 resource〕
  - 谓语：means
  - 宾语从句〔省略that〕：you approach it instrumentally
  -   └ 现在分词作伴随状语：judging any given moment as well spent
  -       └ 条件限制：only in so far as it advances progress toward some goal
- 考点：
  - think of A as B意为“把A看作B”。
  - judge A as well spent意为“认为A没有虚度/花得值得”。
  - in so far as相当于insofar as，表示“在……范围内；只要”。

## 11. E2-2016-T3-P1-S11

Immersive reading, by contrast, depends on being willing to risk inefficiency, goallessness, even time-wasting.

**译文：** 相比之下，沉浸式阅读有赖于你愿意承担低效、没有目标甚至浪费时间的风险。

## 12. E2-2016-T3-P1-S12

Try to slot it in as a to-do list item and you'll manage only goal-focused reading - useful, sometimes, but not the most fulfilling kind.

**译文：** 如果你试图把阅读塞进待办事项清单，就只能做到目标导向型阅读——它有时确实有用，却不是最令人满足的阅读。

## 13. E2-2016-T3-P1-S13

"The future comes at us like empty bottles along an unstoppable and nearly infinite conveyor belt," writes Gary Eberle in his book Sacred Time, and "we feel a pressure to fill these different-sized bottles (days, hours, minutes) as they pass, for if they get by without being filled, we will have wasted them."

**译文：** 加里·埃伯利在《神圣时间》一书中写道：“未来像空瓶一样，沿着一条无法停下、近乎无穷的传送带向我们涌来；我们感到一种压力，要在这些大小不一的瓶子（日、小时、分钟）经过时把它们装满，因为如果它们空着过去，我们就算浪费了它们。”

**长难句拆解：**

- 主干：两段引语并列：The future comes at us… and we feel a pressure to fill…（未来向我们涌来，我们因而感到必须填满时间）
- 逻辑：整句以传送带上的空瓶比喻不断流逝的时间；第二段引语用as说明瓶子经过，用for if解释人们急于填满它们的原因。
- 结构：
  - 引语①：The future comes at us like empty bottles along an unstoppable and nearly infinite conveyor belt
  - 插入性引述：writes Gary Eberle in his book Sacred Time
  - 并列引语②：and we feel a pressure to fill these different-sized bottles
  -   ├ 同位说明：(days, hours, minutes)
  -   ├ 时间状语从句(as)：as they pass
  -   └ 原因状语从句(for)：for if they get by without being filled, we will have wasted them
  -       └ 条件状语从句(if)：if they get by without being filled
- 考点：
  - come at sb表示“向某人逼近/涌来”。
  - get by在此表示“经过、过去”，不是“勉强度日”。
  - for是并列连词，表示补充原因；其内部又包含if条件句。

## 14. E2-2016-T3-P1-S14

No mind-set could be worse for losing yourself in a book.

**译文：** 再没有哪种心态比这更不利于让人沉浸在书中了。

## 15. E2-2016-T3-P1-S15

So what does work?

**译文：** 那么，什么办法有效呢？

## 16. E2-2016-T3-P1-S16

Perhaps surprisingly, scheduling regular times for reading.

**译文：** 答案也许出人意料：固定安排规律的阅读时间。

## 17. E2-2016-T3-P1-S17

You'd think this might fuel the efficiency mind-set, but in fact, Eberle notes, such ritualistic behaviour helps us "step outside time's flow" into "soul time."

**译文：** 你可能以为这会助长效率至上的心态，但埃伯利指出，事实上，这种仪式化的行为能帮助我们“走出时间之流”，进入“心灵时间”。

## 18. E2-2016-T3-P1-S18

You could limit distractions by reading only physical books, or on single-purpose e-readers.

**译文：** 你可以只读纸质书，或者只使用功能单一的电子阅读器，以减少干扰。

## 19. E2-2016-T3-P1-S19

"Carry a book with you at all times" can actually work, too - providing you dip in often enough, so that reading becomes the default state from which you temporarily surface to take care of business, before dropping back down.

**译文：** “始终随身带一本书”其实也能奏效——前提是你足够频繁地翻开它，让阅读成为默认状态：你只是偶尔从中浮出水面处理事务，之后再潜回去。

## 20. E2-2016-T3-P1-S20

On a really good day, it no longer feels as if you're "making time to read," but just reading, and making time for everything else.

**译文：** 在状态特别好的一天，你不再觉得自己是在“挤时间阅读”，而只是自然而然地读书，并为其他一切事情另作安排。
