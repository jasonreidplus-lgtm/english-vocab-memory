# 2011 年考研英语二阅读 Text 3 · 逐句精读

## 1. E2-2011-T3-P1-S1

We tend to think of the decades immediately following World War II as a time of prosperity and growth, with soldiers returning home by the millions, going off to college on the G. I. Bill and lining up at the marriage bureaus.

**译文：** 我们往往把第二次世界大战结束后紧接着的几十年视为繁荣与增长的时期：数百万士兵返乡，凭借《退伍军人权利法案》上大学，并在婚姻登记处排起长队。

## 2. E2-2011-T3-P1-S2

But when it came to their houses, it was a time of common sense and a belief that less could truly be more.

**译文：** 但说到他们的住房，那却是一个崇尚常识、相信“少确实可以胜过多”的时代。

## 3. E2-2011-T3-P1-S3

During the Depression and the war, Americans had learned to live with less, and that restraint, in combination with the postwar confidence in the future, made small, efficient housing positively stylish.

**译文：** 在大萧条和战争期间，美国人已经学会用更少的东西生活；这种克制与战后对未来的信心结合起来，使小巧高效的住宅成为一种十足的时尚。

## 4. E2-2011-T3-P1-S4

Economic condition was only a stimulus for the trend toward efficient living.

**译文：** 经济状况只是推动高效生活趋势的一个因素。

## 5. E2-2011-T3-P1-S5

The phrase "less is more" was actually first popularized by a German, the architect Ludwig Mies van der Rohe, who like other people associated with the Bauhaus, a school of design, emigrated to the United States before World War II and took up posts at American architecture schools.

**译文：** “少即是多”这句话其实最先由一位德国人推广开来，他就是建筑师路德维希·密斯·凡德罗；与包豪斯设计学院的其他相关人士一样，他在二战前移居美国，并在美国建筑院校任职。

**长难句拆解：**

- 主干：S+V：The phrase was first popularized by a German（这句话最先由一位德国人推广）
- 逻辑：主句被动说明名言的推广者；German 后以同位语点名密斯，再由 who 从句补充其移民和任教经历。
- 结构：
  - 主句：The phrase "less is more" was actually first popularized by a German
  -   └ 同位语：the architect Ludwig Mies van der Rohe
  -       └ 非限制性定语从句〔who，修饰 Mies〕：who ... emigrated to the United States ... and took up posts at American architecture schools
  -           ├ 比较/伴随插入语：like other people associated with the Bauhaus
  -           │   ├ 过去分词短语〔修饰 people〕：associated with the Bauhaus
  -           │   └ the Bauhaus 的同位语：a school of design
  -           └ 并列谓语：emigrated ... and took up posts ...
- 考点：
  - popularize 指“使普及、推广开来”。
  - like other people... 是插入比较，不是 who 的谓语。
  - take up posts 指就任职位。

## 6. E2-2011-T3-P1-S6

These designers came to exert enormous influence on the course of American architecture, but none more so than Mies.

**译文：** 这些设计师开始对美国建筑的发展进程产生巨大影响，但没有谁的影响超过密斯。

## 7. E2-2011-T3-P1-S7

Mies's signature phrase means that less decoration, properly organized, has more impact than a lot.

**译文：** 密斯的标志性名言意为：经过恰当组织的少量装饰，比大量装饰更具感染力。

## 8. E2-2011-T3-P1-S8

Elegance, he believed, did not derive from abundance.

**译文：** 他认为，优雅并非源于丰盛。

## 9. E2-2011-T3-P1-S9

Like other modern architects, he employed metal, glass and laminated wood - materials that we take for granted today but that in the 1940s symbolized the future.

**译文：** 与其他现代主义建筑师一样，他使用金属、玻璃和层压木材；这些材料如今被我们视为理所当然，在20世纪40年代却象征着未来。

## 10. E2-2011-T3-P1-S10

Mies's sophisticated presentation masked the fact that the spaces he designed were small and efficient, rather than big and often empty.

**译文：** 密斯精巧的设计呈现掩盖了这样一个事实：他设计的空间小巧高效，而不是宽大却常常空旷。

## 11. E2-2011-T3-P1-S11

The apartments in the elegant towers Mies built on Chicago's Lake Shore Drive, for example, were smaller - two-bedroom units under 1,000 square feet - than those in their older neighbors along the city's Gold Coast.

**译文：** 例如，密斯在芝加哥湖滨大道建造的优雅高楼中，公寓面积比该市黄金海岸沿线那些年代更久的邻近公寓还小，其中两居室不到1000平方英尺。

**长难句拆解：**

- 主干：S+V+C：The apartments were smaller than those（这些公寓比那些公寓更小）
- 逻辑：比较主干是 apartments were smaller than those；those 替代 apartments，避免重复；两个破折号间补充说明其面积。
- 结构：
  - 主句：The apartments ... were smaller ... than those in their older neighbors along the city's Gold Coast
  -   ├ 地点定语：in the elegant towers
  -   │   └ 定语从句〔省略 that，修饰 towers〕：Mies built on Chicago's Lake Shore Drive
  -   ├ 插入语：for example
  -   └ 破折号同位说明：two-bedroom units under 1,000 square feet
- 考点：
  - Mies built... 是省略关系代词的定语从句，不能把 Mies 当主句主语。
  - those 指 older neighboring buildings 中的公寓，而不是建筑本身。
  - under 1,000 square feet 表示不足1000平方英尺。

## 12. E2-2011-T3-P1-S12

But they were popular because of their airy glass walls, the views they afforded and the elegance of the buildings' details and proportions, the architectural equivalent of the abstract art so popular at the time.

**译文：** 但这些公寓很受欢迎，因为它们拥有通透的玻璃墙、开阔的景观，以及建筑细部和比例的优雅；这种建筑风格相当于当时广受欢迎的抽象艺术。

## 13. E2-2011-T3-P1-S13

The trend toward "less" was not entirely foreign.

**译文：** 这种走向“少”的趋势并不完全来自国外。

## 14. E2-2011-T3-P1-S14

In the 1930s Frank Lloyd Wright started building more modest and efficient houses - usually around 1,200 square feet - than the spreading two-story ones he had designed in the 1890s and the early 20th century.

**译文：** 20世纪30年代，弗兰克·劳埃德·赖特开始建造更加朴素高效的住宅，通常约1200平方英尺；这些住宅比他在19世纪90年代和20世纪初设计的宽展两层住宅更小。

## 15. E2-2011-T3-P1-S15

The "Case Study Houses" commissioned from talented modern architects by California Arts & Architecture magazine between 1945 and 1962 were yet another homegrown influence on the "less is more" trend.

**译文：** 1945年至1962年间，《加州艺术与建筑》杂志委托才华出众的现代主义建筑师设计的“案例研究住宅”，是“少即是多”趋势的又一项本土影响。

## 16. E2-2011-T3-P1-S16

Aesthetic effect came from the landscape, new materials and forthright detailing.

**译文：** 审美效果来自景观、新材料以及直截了当的细部处理。

## 17. E2-2011-T3-P1-S17

In his Case Study House, Ralph Rapson may have mispredicted just how the mechanical revolution would impact everyday life - few American families acquired helicopters, though most eventually got clothes dryers - but his belief that self-sufficiency was both desirable and inevitable was widely shared.

**译文：** 在自己的案例研究住宅中，拉尔夫·拉普森也许误判了机械革命将如何影响日常生活——很少有美国家庭购置直升机，尽管大多数家庭最终都有了干衣机——但他关于自给自足既值得追求又不可避免的信念，当时得到了广泛认同。

**长难句拆解：**

- 主干：转折主干：Ralph Rapson may have mispredicted..., but his belief was widely shared（拉普森或许误判了……，但他的信念广受认同）
- 逻辑：may have done 对过去判断作保留性批评；破折号用现实结果举证；but 转向作者真正肯定的信念内容。
- 结构：
  - 分句①：Ralph Rapson may have mispredicted
  -   └ 宾语从句(how)：how the mechanical revolution would impact everyday life
  -   └ 破折号插入例证：few American families acquired helicopters, though most eventually got clothes dryers
  - 转折分句②(but)：his belief ... was widely shared
  -   └ 同位语从句(that)〔说明 belief〕：that self-sufficiency was both desirable and inevitable
- 考点：
  - mispredict how... 指对……将如何发展作出错误预测。
  - though 在插入例证内部形成“直升机少见—干衣机普及”的让步对照。
  - both desirable and inevitable：既值得追求，又不可避免。
