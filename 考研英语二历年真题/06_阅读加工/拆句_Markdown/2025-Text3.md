# 2025 年考研英语二阅读 Text 3 · 逐句精读

## 1. E2-2025-T3-P1-S1

Heat action plans, or HAPs, have been proliferating in India in the past few years.

**译文：** 过去几年，热浪行动计划（简称HAP）在印度迅速增多。

## 2. E2-2025-T3-P1-S2

In general, an HAP spells out when and how officials should issue heat warnings and alert hospitals and other institutions.

**译文：** 一般来说，热浪行动计划会明确规定，官员应在何时、以何种方式发布高温预警，并通知医院及其他机构。

## 3. E2-2025-T3-P1-S3

Nagpur's plan, for instance, calls for hospitals to set aside "cold wards" in the summer for treating heatstroke patients, and advises builders to give construction laborers a break from work on very hot days.

**译文：** 例如，那格浦尔的计划要求医院在夏季预留"冷却病房"治疗中暑患者，并建议建筑商在极热天气让建筑工人暂停工作、休息一下。

**长难句拆解：**

- 主干：Nagpur's plan calls for hospitals to set aside wards and advises builders to give laborers a break（那格浦尔计划要求医院预留病房，并建议建筑商让工人休息）
- 逻辑：plan作共同主语，通过calls for和advises并列提出医疗与劳动保护两类措施；两个不定式分别说明医院和建筑商应采取的行动。
- 结构：
  - 主句并列谓语一：Nagpur's plan calls for hospitals to set aside cold wards
  -   ├ 时间状语：in the summer
  -   └ 目的状语(for)：for treating heatstroke patients
  - 并列谓语二：and advises builders to give construction laborers a break from work
  -   └ 时间状语：on very hot days
- 考点：
  - call for sb to do 意为“要求某人做某事”。
  - set aside 在此意为“留出、预留”。
  - heatstroke 指中暑，尤指严重热射病。

## 4. E2-2025-T3-P1-S4

But implementation of existing HAPs has been uneven, according to a report from the center for Policy Research.

**译文：** 不过，政策研究中心的一份报告指出，现有热浪行动计划的落实情况并不均衡。

## 5. E2-2025-T3-P1-S5

Many lack adequate funding, it found.

**译文：** 报告发现，许多计划缺少足够资金。

## 6. E2-2025-T3-P1-S6

And their triggering thresholds often are not customized to the local climate.

**译文：** 而且，这些计划触发预警的阈值往往没有根据当地气候定制。

## 7. E2-2025-T3-P1-S7

In some areas, high daytime temperatures alone might serve as an adequate trigger for alerts.

**译文：** 在一些地区，仅凭白天的高温也许就足以触发预警。

## 8. E2-2025-T3-P1-S8

But in other places, nighttime temperatures or humidity might be as important a gauge of risk as daytime highs.

**译文：** 但在其他地方，夜间温度或湿度对衡量风险的重要性可能不亚于白天的最高温度。

## 9. E2-2025-T3-P1-S9

Mumbai's April heat stroke deaths highlighted the need for more nuanced and localized warnings, researchers say.

**译文：** 研究人员说，孟买4月发生的中暑死亡事件凸显了更细致、更贴合当地情况的预警之必要。

## 10. E2-2025-T3-P1-S10

That day's high temperature of roughly 36°C was 1°C shy of the heat wave alert threshold for coastal cities set by national meteorological authorities.

**译文：** 当天最高温度约为36摄氏度，仅比国家气象部门为沿海城市设定的热浪预警阈值低1摄氏度。

## 11. E2-2025-T3-P1-S11

But the effects of the heat were amplified by humidity - an often neglected factor in heat alert systems - and the lack of shade at the late-morning outdoor ceremony.

**译文：** 但高温的影响因湿度这一在高温预警系统中常被忽视的因素而加剧，上午较晚时段举行的户外活动又缺少遮阴。

**长难句拆解：**

- 主干：The effects were amplified by humidity and the lack of shade（高温影响因湿度和缺少遮阴而加剧）
- 逻辑：被动主干指出两项放大高温影响的因素；破折号之间的同位语专门解释湿度为何容易被现行预警忽略。
- 结构：
  - 主句：the effects of the heat were amplified by humidity and the lack of shade
  -   ├ 同位插入说明〔修饰 humidity〕：an often neglected factor in heat alert systems
  -   └ 介词短语〔修饰 lack of shade〕：at the late-morning outdoor ceremony
- 考点：
  - amplify 在此意为“加剧、放大影响”。
  - neglected factor 指常被忽视的因素。
  - late-morning 指上午较晚、接近中午的时段。

## 12. E2-2025-T3-P1-S12

To help improve HAPs, urban planner Kotharkar's team is working on a model plan that outlines best practices and could be adapted to local conditions.

**译文：** 为帮助改进热浪行动计划，城市规划师科塔卡尔的团队正在制定一份示范方案；它概述最佳实践，并可根据当地条件加以调整。

## 13. E2-2025-T3-P1-S13

Among other things, she says, all cities should create a vulnerability map to help focus responses on the populations most at risk.

**译文：** 她说，除此之外，所有城市都应绘制一张脆弱性地图，以便将应对措施集中到风险最高的人群。

## 14. E2-2025-T3-P1-S14

Such mapping doesn't need to be complex, Kotharkar says.

**译文：** 科塔卡尔说，这种地图不必复杂。

## 15. E2-2025-T3-P1-S15

"A useful map can be created by looking at even a few key parameters."

**译文：** "哪怕只考察几个关键参数，也能绘制出一张实用的地图。"

## 16. E2-2025-T3-P1-S16

For example, neighborhoods with a large elderly population or informal dwellings that cope poorly with heat could get special warnings or be bolstered with cooling centers.

**译文：** 例如，老年人口众多的社区，或者难以抵御高温的非正规住房区，可以收到专项预警，也可以通过增设纳凉中心来增强应对能力。

## 17. E2-2025-T3-P1-S17

The Nagpur project has already created a risk and vulnerability map, which enabled Kotharkar to tell officials which neighborhoods to focus on in the event of a heat wave this summer.

**译文：** 那格浦尔项目已经绘制了风险与脆弱性地图，使科塔卡尔能够告诉官员，今年夏季一旦发生热浪，应重点关注哪些社区。

## 18. E2-2025-T3-P1-S18

HAPs shouldn't just include short-term emergency responses, researchers say, but also recommend medium- to long-term measures that could make communities cooler.

**译文：** 研究人员说，热浪行动计划不应只包括短期应急措施，还应提出能够让社区更加凉爽的中长期措施。

**长难句拆解：**

- 主干：HAPs shouldn't just include responses but also recommend measures（行动计划不应只含应急响应，还应提出中长期措施）
- 逻辑：not just...but also...把计划从应急处置扩展到长期降温；that从句进一步界定中长期措施的目标。
- 结构：
  - 主句：HAPs shouldn't just include short-term emergency responses but also recommend medium- to long-term measures
  -   ├ 并列结构：not just include A but also recommend B
  -   └ 定语从句(that)：that could make communities cooler
  - 插入引述来源：researchers say
- 考点：
  - medium- to long-term 是共享term的省略写法。
  - emergency responses 指突发高温时的应急响应。
  - make communities cooler 包括绿化、建筑改造等结构性措施。

## 19. E2-2025-T3-P1-S19

In Nagpur, for example, Kotharkar's team has been able to advise city officials about where to plant trees to provide shade.

**译文：** 例如在那格浦尔，科塔卡尔的团队已经能够就应在何处植树造阴，为市政官员提供建议。

## 20. E2-2025-T3-P1-S20

HAPs could also guide efforts to retrofit homes or modify building regulations.

**译文：** 热浪行动计划还可指导住宅改造或建筑法规修订工作。

## 21. E2-2025-T3-P1-S21

"Reducing deaths in an emergency is a good target to have, but it's the lowest target," says Climate researcher Chandni Singh.

**译文：** 气候研究人员钱德妮·辛格说："减少紧急情况下的死亡固然是一个应有的目标，但那只是最低层次的目标。"
