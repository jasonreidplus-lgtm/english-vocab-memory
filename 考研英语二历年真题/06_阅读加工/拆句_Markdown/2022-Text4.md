# 2022 年考研英语二阅读 Text 4 · 逐句精读

## 1. E2-2022-T4-P1-S1

Although ethics classes are common around the world, scientists are unsure if their lessons can actually change behavior; evidence either way is weak, relying on contrived laboratory tests or sometimes unreliable self-reports.

**译文：** 尽管伦理课在世界各地都很常见，科学家仍不确定课堂所教能否真正改变行为；支持或反对的证据都很薄弱，因为它们依赖人为设计的实验室测试，有时还依赖并不可靠的自我报告。

**长难句拆解：**

- 主干：S+V+C；S+V+C：Scientists are unsure...; evidence is weak（科学家并不确定……；证据很薄弱）
- 逻辑：Although 先承认伦理课普遍存在，再转出效果未知；分号后的分句解释判断依据不足，relying on...进一步指出现有证据的方法缺陷。
- 结构：
  - 让步状语从句(Although)：Although ethics classes are common around the world
  - 主分句1：scientists are unsure
  -   └ 宾语从句(if)：if their lessons can actually change behavior
  - 分号并列分句2：evidence either way is weak
  -   └ 现在分词状语：relying on contrived laboratory tests or sometimes unreliable self-reports
- 考点：
  - either way 指支持或反对两种方向。
  - contrived 表示人为安排、脱离自然情境。
  - self-report 指受试者对自身行为的报告。

## 2. E2-2022-T4-P1-S2

**译文：** 但发表于《认知》期刊的一项新研究发现，至少在一种真实情境中，一堂伦理课可能产生了持久影响。

## 3. E2-2022-T4-P1-S3

The researchers investigated one class session's impact on eating meat.

**译文：** 研究人员考察了一节课对吃肉行为的影响。

## 4. E2-2022-T4-P1-S4

They chose this particular behavior for three reasons, according to study co-author Eric Schwitzgebel, a philosopher at the University of California, Riverside: students' attitudes on the topic are variable and unstable, behavior is easily measurable, and ethics literature largely agrees that eating less meat is good because it reduces environmental harm and animal suffering.

**译文：** 研究合著者、加州大学河滨分校哲学家埃里克·施维茨格贝尔说，他们之所以选择这种特定行为，有三个原因：学生对这一话题的态度多变且不稳定，行为容易测量，而且伦理学文献大体认同少吃肉是好事，因为这能减少环境损害和动物痛苦。

**长难句拆解：**

- 主干：S+V+O：They chose this behavior for three reasons（他们因三个理由选择这种行为）
- 逻辑：冒号明确引出三个并列原因；第三个原因内部再嵌套 that 宾语从句和 because 原因从句，是全句层级最深的部分。
- 结构：
  - 主句：They chose this particular behavior for three reasons
  - 插入来源：according to study co-author Eric Schwitzgebel
  -   └ 同位语：a philosopher at the University of California, Riverside
  - 冒号后并列理由1：students' attitudes on the topic are variable and unstable
  - 并列理由2：behavior is easily measurable
  - 并列理由3：ethics literature largely agrees
  -   └ 宾语从句(that)：that eating less meat is good
  -       └ 原因状语(because)：because it reduces environmental harm and animal suffering
- 考点：
  - variable and unstable 强调态度存在差异且容易变化。
  - ethics literature 指伦理学领域的研究文献。
  - it 在 because 从句中指 eating less meat。

## 5. E2-2022-T4-P1-S5

Half of the students in four large philosophy classes read an article on the ethics of factory-farmed meat, optionally watched an 11-minute video on the topic and joined a 50-minute discussion.

**译文：** 四个大型哲学课班级中，一半学生阅读了一篇讨论工厂化养殖肉类伦理问题的文章，可自行选择是否观看一段11分钟的视频，并参加了50分钟的讨论。

## 7. E2-2022-T4-P1-S7

Then, unknown to the students, the researchers studied their anonymized meal-card purchases for that semester—nearly 14,000 receipts for almost 500 students.

**译文：** 随后，在学生不知情的情况下，研究人员分析了他们该学期匿名化的餐卡消费记录——近500名学生的将近1.4万张收据。

## 8. E2-2022-T4-P1-S8

Schwitzgebel predicted the intervention would have no effect; he had previously found that ethics professors do not differ from other professors on a range of behaviors, including voting rates, blood donation and returning library books.

**译文：** 施维茨格贝尔原本预测这种干预不会产生效果；他此前发现，从投票率、献血到归还图书等一系列行为来看，伦理学教授与其他教授并无不同。

## 9. E2-2022-T4-P1-S9

But among student subjects who discussed meat ethics, meal purchases containing meat decreased from 52 to 45 percent—and this effect held steady for the study's duration of several weeks.

**译文：** 但在讨论过肉食伦理的学生受试者中，含肉餐食的购买比例从52%降至45%，而且这一效果在长达数周的研究期间一直保持稳定。

## 10. E2-2022-T4-P1-S10

Purchases from the other group remained at 52 percent.

**译文：** 另一组的购买比例则维持在52%。

## 11. E2-2022-T4-P1-S11

"That's actually a pretty large effect for a pretty small intervention," Schwitzgebel says.

**译文：** “对于这么小的干预来说，这实际上是相当大的效果，”施维茨格贝尔说。

## 12. E2-2022-T4-P1-S12

Psychologist Nina Strohminger at the University of Pennsylvania, who was not involved in the study, says she wants the effect to be real but cannot rule out some unknown confounding variable.

**译文：** 宾夕法尼亚大学心理学家妮娜·斯特罗明格没有参与这项研究；她说自己希望这一效果是真的，却无法排除某个未知混杂变量的影响。

## 13. E2-2022-T4-P1-S13

And if real, she notes, it might be reversible by another nudge: "Easy come, easy go."

**译文：** 她还指出，即便效果确实存在，也可能被另一次轻推逆转：“来得容易，去得也快。”

## 14. E2-2022-T4-P1-S14

Schwitzgebel suspects the greatest impact came from social influence—classmates or teaching assistants leading the discussions may have shared their own vegetarianism, showing it as achievable or more common.

**译文：** 施维茨格贝尔认为，最大的影响可能来自社会影响：主持讨论的同学或助教或许谈到了自己吃素，从而让素食显得可行，或显得更加普遍。

**长难句拆解：**

- 主干：S+V+O：Schwitzgebel suspects the impact came from social influence（施维茨格贝尔认为最大影响来自社会影响）
- 逻辑：破折号后的内容具体解释 social influence 如何发生：主持讨论者分享素食经历，从而改变素食在学生眼中的可行性和普遍性。
- 结构：
  - 主句：Schwitzgebel suspects
  -   └ 宾语从句：the greatest impact came from social influence
  - 破折号后解释：classmates or teaching assistants leading the discussions may have shared their own vegetarianism
  -   ├ 现在分词短语：leading the discussions〔修饰 classmates or teaching assistants〕
  -   └ 现在分词结果/伴随状语：showing it as achievable or more common
- 考点：
  - vegetarianism 在此指吃素的生活方式或实践。
  - show A as B 表示“使A显得/被看作B”。
  - it 指代 vegetarianism，而非 discussion。
