## 10. E2-2010-T2-P1-S10

This episode crystallizes the irony that although American men tend to talk more than women in public situations, they often talk less at home.

**译文：** 这段插曲鲜明地揭示了一个反差：美国男性在公共场合往往比女性说得多，在家里却常常说得更少。

**长难句拆解：**

- 主干：S+V+O：This episode crystallizes the irony（这段插曲鲜明揭示了反差）
- 逻辑：that 从句具体说明 irony 是什么；从句内部用 although 构成“公共场合说得更多—家里说得更少”的反差。
- 结构：
  - 主句：This episode crystallizes the irony
  -   └ 同位语从句(that)〔说明 irony 的内容〕：that ... they often talk less at home
  -       └ 让步状语从句(although)：although American men tend to talk more than women in public situations
  -       └ 主句：they often talk less at home
- 考点：
  - crystallize 在此意为“使清楚呈现、使具体化”。
  - that 引导的是解释 irony 内容的同位语从句。
  - more than women 与 less at home 的比较维度不同，翻译时要保留场景对照。

## 13. E2-2010-T2-P1-S13

Sociologist Catherine Kohler Riessman reports in her new book Divorce Talk that most of the women she interviewed - but only a few of the men - gave lack of communication as the reason for their divorces.

**译文：** 社会学家凯瑟琳·科勒·里斯曼在新书《离婚谈话》中指出，她采访的大多数女性——而男性只有少数——都把缺乏沟通列为离婚的原因。

## 14. E2-2010-T2-P1-S14

Given the current divorce rate of nearly 50 percent, that amounts to millions of cases in the United States every year a virtual epidemic of failed conversation.

**译文：** 鉴于目前离婚率接近50%，这意味着美国每年有数百万起这样的案例，堪称一场“沟通失败”的流行病。

**长难句拆解：**

- 主干：S+V+O：That amounts to millions of cases（那相当于数百万起案例）
- 逻辑：Given... 提供计算背景；that 回指上一句所说因缺乏沟通而离婚的情况；破折号后的名词短语对数百万案例作总结性评价。
- 结构：
  - 背景状语：Given the current divorce rate of nearly 50 percent
  - 主句：that amounts to millions of cases in the United States every year
  -   └ 破折号同位说明：a virtual epidemic of failed conversation
- 考点：
  - given 作介词，意为“考虑到、鉴于”。
  - amount to 在此意为“总计达到、相当于”。
  - virtual epidemic 是比喻，表示问题普遍得如同流行病。

## 15. E2-2010-T2-P1-S15

In my own research, complaints from women about their husbands most often focused not on tangible inequities such as having given up the chance for a career to accompany a husband to his, or doing far more than their share of daily life-support work like cleaning, cooking and social arrangements.

**译文：** 在我自己的研究中，女性对丈夫的抱怨，最常针对的并不是切实可见的不平等，例如为了陪丈夫追求事业而放弃自己的职业机会，或在清洁、做饭和社交安排等日常生活维持工作中承担远超自己应有份额的劳动。

**长难句拆解：**

- 主干：S+V：Complaints ... focused not on tangible inequities（抱怨并不主要集中在有形的不平等上）
- 逻辑：not on... 与下一句 Instead... 形成跨句对照；such as 后有两个动名词结构，由 or 并列，分别说明职业牺牲和家务负担。
- 结构：
  - 范围状语：In my own research
  - 主句：complaints from women about their husbands most often focused not on tangible inequities
  -   └ 举例(such as)并列①：having given up the chance for a career
  -       └ 目的状语(to)：to accompany a husband to his〔his 后省略 career〕
  -   └ 举例并列②(or)：doing far more than their share of daily life-support work
  -       └ 举例(like)：cleaning, cooking and social arrangements
- 考点：
  - his 是名词性替代，完整含义为 his career。
  - far more than their share 指“远超她们应承担的份额”。
  - life-support work 在此不是医疗“生命支持”，而是维持日常生活运转的劳动。
