# 2013 年考研英语二阅读 Text 4 · 逐句精读

## 1. E2-2013-T4-P1-S1

Europe is not a gender-equality heaven.

**译文：** 欧洲并不是一个实现了性别平等的天堂。

## 2. E2-2013-T4-P1-S2

In particular, the corporate workplace will never be completely family-friendly until women are part of senior management decisions, and Europe's top corporate-governance positions remain overwhelmingly male.

**译文：** 尤其是，只有女性参与高级管理层的决策，企业职场才可能真正做到对家庭友好；而欧洲企业治理的最高职位目前仍由男性占据绝对多数。

**长难句拆解：**

- 主干：并列主干：the corporate workplace will never be ... until ..., and Europe's top ... positions remain ... male
- 逻辑：前半句提出职场真正家庭友好的必要条件，后半句转入现实：女性尚未充分进入最高决策层。
- 结构：
  - 状语：In particular
  - 主句①：the corporate workplace will never be completely family-friendly
  - 时间/条件从句(until)：until women are part of senior management decisions
  - 并列主句②(and)：Europe's top corporate-governance positions remain overwhelmingly male
- 考点：
  - not ... until 表示“直到……才”，翻译时宜正向处理。
  - be part of decisions 指参与决策过程。
  - overwhelmingly 在此表示比例上“压倒性地”。

## 3. E2-2013-T4-P1-S3

Indeed, women hold only 14 per cent of positions on European corporate boards.

**译文：** 事实上，欧洲公司董事会中的女性席位仅占14%。

## 4. E2-2013-T4-P1-S4

The European Union is now considering legislation to compel corporate boards to maintain a certain proportion of women – up to 60 per cent.

**译文：** 欧盟目前正在考虑立法，强制公司董事会保持一定的女性比例，最高可达60%。

## 5. E2-2013-T4-P1-S5

This proposed mandate was born of frustration.

**译文：** 这项拟议中的强制规定源于失望。

## 6. E2-2013-T4-P1-S6

Last year, European Commission Vice President Viviane Reding issued a call to voluntary action.

**译文：** 去年，欧盟委员会副主席维维亚娜·雷丁发出了自愿采取行动的号召。

## 7. E2-2013-T4-P1-S7

Reding invited corporations to sign up for gender balance goals of 40 per cent female board membership.

**译文：** 雷丁邀请企业签署承诺，将女性董事会成员的比例目标定为40%。

## 8. E2-2013-T4-P1-S8

But her appeal was considered a failure: only 24 companies took it up.

**译文：** 但她的呼吁被认为失败了：只有24家公司响应。

## 9. E2-2013-T4-P1-S9

Do we need quotas to ensure that women can continue to climb the corporate ladder fairly as they balance work and family?

**译文：** 我们是否需要配额制度，才能确保女性在兼顾工作与家庭的同时，继续公平地沿着企业晋升阶梯向上发展？

## 10. E2-2013-T4-P1-S10

"Personally, I don't like quotas," Reding said recently.

**译文：** “就我个人而言，我不喜欢配额，”雷丁最近说道。

## 11. E2-2013-T4-P1-S11

"But I like what the quotas do."

**译文：** “但我喜欢配额所产生的效果。”

## 12. E2-2013-T4-P1-S12

Quotas get action: they "open the way to equality and they break through the glass ceiling," according to Reding, a result seen in France and other countries with legally binding provisions on placing women in top business positions.

**译文：** 配额能够促成行动：用雷丁的话说，它们“为平等铺平道路，并打破玻璃天花板”；法国以及其他对女性进入企业高层设有法律约束性规定的国家已经出现了这种效果。

**长难句拆解：**

- 主干：主干：Quotas get action: they open the way ... and break through ...
- 逻辑：先用简短判断说明配额能推动行动，再引用雷丁概括其两种效果，最后以法国等国的制度实践作证。
- 结构：
  - 冒号前总述：Quotas get action
  - 冒号后并列谓语：open the way to equality and break through the glass ceiling
  - 插入来源状语：according to Reding
  - 同位语总结：a result
  - 过去分词短语作后置定语：seen in France and other countries with legally binding provisions ...
- 考点：
  - get action 意为“促成实际行动”。
  - glass ceiling 是阻碍女性等群体晋升的“玻璃天花板”。
  - a result 指代前面“打开平等道路并突破障碍”这一整体结果。

## 13. E2-2013-T4-P1-S13

I understand Reding's reluctance – and her frustration.

**译文：** 我理解雷丁的不情愿，也理解她的挫败感。

## 14. E2-2013-T4-P1-S14

I don't like quotas either; they run counter to my belief in meritocracy, governance by the capable.

**译文：** 我也不喜欢配额；它违背了我对任人唯贤制度的信念，也就是由有能力者进行管理。

## 15. E2-2013-T4-P1-S15

But, when one considers the obstacles to achieving the meritocratic ideal, it does look as if a fairer world must be temporarily ordered.

**译文：** 但是，当人们考虑到实现任人唯贤理想所面临的障碍时，似乎确实必须暂时通过强制规定来建立一个更公平的世界。

## 16. E2-2013-T4-P1-S16

After all, four decades of evidence has now shown that corporations in Europe as well as the US are evading the meritocratic hiring and promotion of women to top positions – no matter how much "soft pressure" is put upon them.

**译文：** 毕竟，四十年来的证据已经表明，欧洲和美国的企业一直在回避以能力为标准聘用和提拔女性进入高层职位，无论施加多少“软性压力”都无济于事。

## 17. E2-2013-T4-P1-S17

When women do break through to the summit of corporate power – as, for example, Sheryl Sandberg recently did at Facebook – they attract massive attention precisely because they remain the exception to the rule.

**译文：** 当女性确实突破障碍、登上企业权力顶峰时——例如谢丽尔·桑德伯格最近在脸书所做到的那样——她们会受到极大关注，恰恰因为她们仍然只是规则之外的例外。

## 18. E2-2013-T4-P1-S18

If appropriate public policies were in place to help all women – whether CEOs or their children's caregivers – and all families, Sandberg would be no more newsworthy than any other highly capable person living in a more just society.

**译文：** 如果已有适当的公共政策来帮助所有女性——无论是首席执行官，还是照顾她们孩子的人——以及所有家庭，那么在一个更加公正的社会里，桑德伯格就不会比任何其他能力出众的人更具新闻价值。

**长难句拆解：**

- 主干：虚拟条件主干：If appropriate policies were in place ..., Sandberg would be no more newsworthy than ...
- 逻辑：作者用虚拟条件句反推：如果制度已普遍支持女性和家庭，女性高管就不会因其性别而成为罕见新闻。
- 结构：
  - 与现在事实相反的条件从句：If appropriate public policies were in place
  - 不定式表目的：to help all women ... and all families
  - 插入让步范围：whether CEOs or their children's caregivers
  - 主句：Sandberg would be no more newsworthy than any other highly capable person
  - 现在分词短语作后置定语：living in a more just society
- 考点：
  - were ... would be 是对当前未实现状态的虚拟。
  - no more ... than 表示“并不比……更……”。
  - whether A or B 在此强调政策应覆盖不同社会角色的女性。
