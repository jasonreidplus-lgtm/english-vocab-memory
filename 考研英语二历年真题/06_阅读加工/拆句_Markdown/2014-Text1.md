# 2014 年考研英语二阅读 Text 1 · 逐句精读

## 1. E2-2014-T1-P1-S1

What would you do with $590m?

**译文：** 如果有5.9亿美元，你会怎么花？

## 2. E2-2014-T1-P1-S2

This is now a question for Gloria MacKenzie, an 84-year-old widow who recently emerged from her small, tin-roofed house in Florida to collect the biggest undivided lottery jackpot in history.

**译文：** 如今，这个问题摆在格洛丽亚·麦肯齐面前。这位84岁的寡妇最近走出佛罗里达州那座铁皮屋顶的小房子，领取了历史上金额最大的单人彩票头奖。

## 3. E2-2014-T1-P1-S3

If she hopes her new-found fortune will yield lasting feelings of fulfilment, she could do worse than read Happy Money by Elizabeth Dunn and Michael Norton.

**译文：** 如果她希望这笔新获得的财富带来持久的满足感，那么读一读伊丽莎白·邓恩和迈克尔·诺顿合著的《快乐金钱》会是个很好的选择。

## 4. E2-2014-T1-P1-S4

These two academics use an array of behavioral research to show that the most rewarding ways to spend money can be counterintuitive.

**译文：** 这两位学者借助大量行为研究说明，最有回报的花钱方式可能与直觉相反。

## 5. E2-2014-T1-P1-S5

Fantasies of great wealth often involve visions of fancy cars and extravagant homes.

**译文：** 人们幻想巨额财富时，常会想到豪华汽车和奢华住宅。

## 6. E2-2014-T1-P1-S6

Yet satisfaction with these material purchases wears off fairly quickly.

**译文：** 然而，这些物质消费带来的满足感消退得相当快。

## 7. E2-2014-T1-P1-S7

What was once exciting and new becomes old-hat; regret creeps in.

**译文：** 曾经令人兴奋而新鲜的东西会变得陈旧乏味，懊悔也会悄然滋生。

## 8. E2-2014-T1-P1-S8

It is far better to spend money on experiences, say Ms Dunn and Mr Norton, like interesting trips, unique meals or even going to the cinema.

**译文：** 邓恩女士和诺顿先生认为，把钱花在体验上要好得多，例如有趣的旅行、独特的餐饮，甚至只是去看电影。

**长难句拆解：**

- 主干：主干：It is far better to spend money on experiences
- 逻辑：句子把研究结论先明确提出，再用插入语标明观点来源，并列举三类体验型消费。
- 结构：
  - It 作形式主语，真正主语是不定式：to spend money on experiences
  - 比较评价：far better，far 强化比较级
  - 插入语：say Ms Dunn and Mr Norton
  - 举例同位说明：like interesting trips, unique meals or even going to the cinema
- 考点：
  - spend money on 表示“把钱花在……上”。
  - say ... 是报道式倒装插入语，不是句子主干。
  - like 在此表示“例如”，后列名词短语和动名词短语。

## 9. E2-2014-T1-P1-S9

These purchases often become more valuable with time – as stories or memories – particularly if they involve feeling more connected to others.

**译文：** 这些消费往往会随着时间推移而变得更有价值，因为它们会化为故事或回忆；如果这些经历能让人与他人联系得更紧密，尤其如此。

## 10. E2-2014-T1-P1-S10

This slim volume is packed with tips to help wage slaves as well as lottery winners get the most "happiness bang for your buck."

**译文：** 这本篇幅不长的书充满实用建议，既能帮助工薪族，也能帮助彩票赢家，让每一块钱都换来尽可能多的幸福。

## 11. E2-2014-T1-P1-S11

It seems most people would be better off if they could shorten their commutes to work, spend more time with friends and family and less of it watching television (something the average American spends a whopping two months a year doing, and is hardly jollier for it).

**译文：** 如果人们能缩短通勤时间，多陪伴亲友，少看些电视，似乎大多数人都会过得更好；普通美国人每年竟花整整两个月看电视，却几乎没有因此更快乐。

**长难句拆解：**

- 主干：主干：It seems (that) most people would be better off if ...
- 逻辑：主句提出改善幸福感的条件，括号则用美国人的看电视时长与低幸福收益形成反讽式证据。
- 结构：
  - It seems 后省略连接词 that，后接主语从句内容
  - 条件从句(if)：if they could shorten ..., spend more time ... and less of it watching television
  - 三个并列动作：shorten / spend more / (spend) less
  - 括号内同位说明 something，指 watching television
  - 定语从句〔省略 that，修饰 something〕：the average American spends ... doing, and is hardly jollier for it
- 考点：
  - be better off 表示“处境更好”。
  - less of it 中 it 指 time。
  - spend time doing 是固定结构；be jollier for it 表示“因此更快乐”。

## 12. E2-2014-T1-P1-S12

Buying gifts or giving to charity is often more pleasurable than purchasing things for oneself, and luxuries are most enjoyable when they are consumed sparingly.

**译文：** 购买礼物或捐助慈善通常比给自己买东西更令人愉快，而奢侈品只有偶尔享用时最能带来乐趣。

## 13. E2-2014-T1-P1-S13

This is apparently the reason McDonald's restricts the availability of its popular McRib – a marketing trick that has turned the pork sandwich into an object of obsession.

**译文：** 这显然正是麦当劳限制其热门产品“烤汁猪排堡”供应时间的原因；这一营销手段把这种猪肉三明治变成了人们痴迷的对象。

## 14. E2-2014-T1-P1-S14

Readers of Happy Money are clearly a privileged lot, anxious about fulfilment, not hunger.

**译文：** 《快乐金钱》的读者显然是一群生活优渥的人；他们焦虑的是满足感，而不是饥饿。

## 15. E2-2014-T1-P1-S15

Money may not quite buy happiness, but people in wealthier countries are generally happier than those in poor ones.

**译文：** 金钱或许不能完全买来幸福，但富裕国家的人总体上确实比贫穷国家的人更快乐。

## 16. E2-2014-T1-P1-S16

Yet the link between feeling good and spending money on others can be seen among rich and poor people around the world, and scarcity enhances the pleasure of most things for most people.

**译文：** 不过，无论贫富，在世界各地都能看到为他人花钱与良好感受之间的联系；而且对大多数人而言，稀缺会增强大多数事物带来的愉悦。

**长难句拆解：**

- 主干：并列主干：the link ... can be seen ..., and scarcity enhances the pleasure ...
- 逻辑：句子并列给出两项跨人群规律：利他消费与良好感受相关，稀缺性则会提升享用事物的愉悦。
- 结构：
  - 主句①主语：the link between feeling good and spending money on others
  - 被动谓语：can be seen among rich and poor people around the world
  - 并列主句②(and)：scarcity enhances the pleasure of most things
  - 范围状语：for most people
- 考点：
  - between A and B 连接 feeling 与 spending 两个动名词结构。
  - among rich and poor people 强调该联系并不限于富人。
  - scarcity 在此指供应有限或不常获得。

## 17. E2-2014-T1-P1-S17

Not everyone will agree with the authors' policy ideas, which range from mandating more holiday time to reducing tax incentives for American homebuyers.

**译文：** 并非所有人都会赞同作者的政策主张，这些主张从强制增加休假时间，到减少美国购房者的税收优惠，不一而足。

## 18. E2-2014-T1-P1-S18

But most people will come away from this book believing it was money well spent.

**译文：** 但多数人读完这本书时都会相信，买它的钱花得值。
