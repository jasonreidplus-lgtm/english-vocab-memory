# 2020 年考研英语二阅读 Text 3 · 逐句精读

## 1. E2-2020-T3-P1-S1

Madrid was hailed as a public health beacon last November when it rolled out ambitious restrictions on the most polluting cars.

**译文：** 去年11月，马德里推出针对污染最严重车辆的雄心勃勃的限制措施时，曾被誉为公共卫生的灯塔。

## 2. E2-2020-T3-P1-S2

Seven months and one election day later, a new conservative city council suspended enforcement of the clean air zone, a first step toward its possible demise.

**译文：** 七个月后，经历了一次选举，新上台的保守派市议会暂停执行清洁空气区规定，这是该区域可能走向终结的第一步。

## 3. E2-2020-T3-P1-S3

Mayor Jose Luis Martinez-Almeida made opposition to the zone a centrepiece of his election campaign, despite its success in improving air quality.

**译文：** 市长何塞·路易斯·马丁内斯-阿尔梅达把反对该区域作为竞选活动的核心议题，尽管它已成功改善空气质量。

## 4. E2-2020-T3-P1-S4

A judge has now overruled the city's decision to stop levying fines, ordering them reinstated.

**译文：** 如今，一名法官推翻了市政府停止罚款的决定，下令恢复罚款。

## 5. E2-2020-T3-P1-S5

But with legal battles ahead, the zone's future looks uncertain at best.

**译文：** 但由于前方还有法律争端，该区域的未来充其量只能说前景不明。

## 6. E2-2020-T3-P1-S6

Among other weaknesses, the measures cities must employ when left to tackle dirty air on their own are politically contentious, and therefore vulnerable.

**译文：** 城市在被迫单独应对空气污染时所能采取的措施还有一个弱点：它们在政治上极具争议，因而十分脆弱。

## 7. E2-2020-T3-P1-S7

That's because they inevitably put the costs of cleaning the air on to individual drivers - who must pay fees or buy better vehicles - rather than on to the car manufacturers whose cheating is the real cause of our toxic pollution.

**译文：** 这是因为，这些措施不可避免地把清洁空气的成本强加给单个驾驶者——他们必须付费或购买更好的车辆——而不是强加给那些作弊行为才是有毒污染真正根源的汽车制造商。

**长难句拆解：**

- 主干：表语从句：That's because they put the costs on drivers rather than manufacturers（这是因为成本被强加给司机而非制造商）
- 逻辑：because 解释措施为何脆弱；rather than 对比实际承担成本者与应负责任者，who 和 whose 分别补充两方信息。
- 结构：
  - 主句：That is
  -   └ 表语从句(because)：they inevitably put the costs of cleaning the air on to individual drivers
  -       ├ 非限制性定语从句(who)：who must pay fees or buy better vehicles
  -       └ 对比结构：rather than on to the car manufacturers
  -           └ 定语从句(whose)：whose cheating is the real cause of our toxic pollution
- 考点：
  - put the costs on to somebody：把成本转嫁给某人。
  - rather than：而不是。
  - whose cheating：这些制造商的作弊行为。

## 10. E2-2020-T3-P1-S10

And if Sadiq Khan wins and extends it to the North and South Circular roads in 2021 as he intends, it is sure to spark intense opposition from the far larger number of motorists who will then be affected.

**译文：** 如果萨迪克·汗获胜，并按计划于2021年把该区域扩展到南北环路，那么数量更多、届时将受影响的驾车者势必会强烈反对。

**长难句拆解：**

- 主干：条件复句：If Sadiq Khan wins and extends it..., it is sure to spark opposition（如果汗获胜并扩区，必将引发反对）
- 逻辑：if 给出扩区假设，as 插入说明这是既定意图；主句预测结果，who 解释反对者为何会反对。
- 结构：
  - 条件状语从句(if)：Sadiq Khan wins and extends it to ... roads in 2021
  -   └ 方式状语从句(as)：as he intends
  - 主句：it is sure to spark intense opposition
  -   └ 介词短语：from the far larger number of motorists
  -       └ 定语从句(who)：who will then be affected
- 考点：
  - be sure to：必定会。
  - spark opposition：引发反对。
  - the far larger number of：数量大得多的……。

The zones do deliver some improvements to air quality, and the science tells us that means real health benefits - fewer heart attacks, strokes and premature births, less cancer, dementia and asthma.

## 18. E2-2020-T3-P1-S18

Restrictions that keep highly polluting cars out of certain areas - city centres, "school streets", even individual roads - are a response to the absence of a larger effort to properly enforce existing regulations and require auto companies to bring their vehicles into compliance.

**译文：** 禁止高污染车辆进入特定区域——市中心、“学校街道”，甚至个别道路——是对更大范围行动缺失的一种回应；这种更大行动本应妥善执行现有法规，并要求汽车公司让其车辆达到规定标准。

**长难句拆解：**

- 主干：S+系动词+表语：Restrictions ... are a response to the absence of a larger effort（限制措施是对更大行动缺失的回应）
- 逻辑：that 界定限制措施内容；两个并列不定式说明缺失的全国性行动本应做什么。
- 结构：
  - 主语：Restrictions
  -   └ 定语从句(that)：that keep highly polluting cars out of certain areas
  -       └ 破折号举例：city centres, school streets, even individual roads
  - 系表结构：are a response to the absence of a larger effort
  -   ├ 不定式①：to properly enforce existing regulations
  -   └ 并列不定式②：(to) require auto companies to bring their vehicles into compliance
- 考点：
  - keep cars out of：禁止车辆进入。
  - bring...into compliance：使……达到规定要求。
  - absence of an effort：相关行动的缺失。
