## 14. E2-2014-T4-P1-S14

The communities minister, Don Foster, has hinted that George Osborne, Chancellor of the Exchequer, may introduce more flexibility to the current cap on the amount that local authorities can borrow against their housing stock debt.

**译文：** 社区事务大臣唐·福斯特暗示，财政大臣乔治·奥斯本可能会提高现行上限的灵活性；这一上限限制地方政府以其住房存量债务为基础所能借入的金额。

**长难句拆解：**

- 主干：主干：Don Foster has hinted that George Osborne may introduce more flexibility to the current cap
- 逻辑：句子通过两处同位语交代官员身份，再把政策变化具体限定为地方政府住房债务融资上限的灵活化。
- 结构：
  - 同位语①：The communities minister，说明 Don Foster 身份
  - 宾语从句(that)：George Osborne ... may introduce more flexibility to the current cap
  - 同位语②：Chancellor of the Exchequer，说明 George Osborne 身份
  - 介词短语：on the amount
  - 定语从句(that，修饰 amount)：that local authorities can borrow against their housing stock debt
- 考点：
  - hint that 表示“暗示可能……”。
  - cap on the amount 指“金额上限”。
  - borrow against 表示以某项资产或债务安排为依据融资。

## 15. E2-2014-T4-P1-S15

Evidence shows that 60,000 extra new homes could be built over the next five years if the cap were lifted, increasing GDP by 0.6%.

**译文：** 证据表明，如果取消这一上限，未来五年可额外建成6万套新房，使国内生产总值增长0.6%。

**长难句拆解：**

- 主干：主干：Evidence shows that 60,000 extra new homes could be built
- 逻辑：本句用量化证据建立政策链条：取消借款上限→新增住房建设→GDP增长。
- 结构：
  - 宾语从句(that)：60,000 extra new homes could be built over the next five years
  - 条件状语从句(if)：if the cap were lifted
  - 现在分词短语作结果状语：increasing GDP by 0.6%
- 考点：
  - could be built 是情态动词加被动语态。
  - lift the cap 表示“取消/解除上限”。
  - increasing 的逻辑主语是前面整个新增住房建设结果，而非 homes 本身。

## 18. E2-2014-T4-P1-S18

While these measures would be welcome in the short term, we must face up to the fact that the existing £4.5bn programme of grants to fund new affordable housing, set to expire in 2015, is unlikely to be extended beyond then.

**译文：** 这些措施短期内会受到欢迎，但我们必须正视这样一个事实：现有45亿英镑的经济适用房建设拨款计划将在2015年到期，此后不太可能延长。

**长难句拆解：**

- 主干：主干：we must face up to the fact
- 逻辑：作者先承认短期措施有益，再用 while 转折，强调公共拨款计划到期且难以续期这一长期财政现实。
- 结构：
  - 让步状语从句(While)：While these measures would be welcome in the short term
  - 主句：we must face up to the fact
  - 同位语从句(that，解释 fact)：the existing £4.5bn programme ... is unlikely to be extended beyond then
  - 介词短语修饰 programme：of grants to fund new affordable housing
  - 过去分词短语作后置定语：set to expire in 2015
- 考点：
  - face up to 表示“正视、勇敢面对”。
  - set to do 表示“预计/安排将……”。
  - beyond then 中 then 指2015年计划到期之时。
