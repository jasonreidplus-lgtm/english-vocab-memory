# 2017 年考研英语二阅读 Text 4 · 逐句精读

## 1. E2-2017-T4-P1-S1

Though often viewed as a problem for western states, the growing frequency of wildfires is a national concern because of its impact on federal tax dollars, says Professor Max Moritz, a specialist in fire ecology and management.

**译文：** 火灾生态与管理专家马克斯·莫里茨教授表示，野火愈发频繁虽然常被视为西部各州的问题，但由于它影响联邦税款的使用，因此也是全国性关切。

**长难句拆解：**

- 主干：主句：the growing frequency of wildfires is a national concern（野火日益频繁是全国性关切）
- 逻辑：though先承认常见的地区性看法，主句转而将问题提升为全国性关切，并用because of解释财政原因。
- 结构：
  - 让步状语〔过去分词省略结构〕：Though often viewed as a problem for western states
  - 主句：the growing frequency of wildfires is a national concern
  -   └ 原因状语(because of)：because of its impact on federal tax dollars
  - 插入性引述：says Professor Max Moritz
  -   └ 同位语：a specialist in fire ecology and management
- 考点：
  - Though often viewed…补全为Though it is often viewed…。
  - frequency在这里指火灾发生频率，而非“频繁程度”的简单形容。
  - says Professor…是新闻报道中的倒装引述结构。

## 3. E2-2017-T4-P1-S3

In effect, fewer federal funds today are going towards the agency's other work - such as forest conservation, watershed and cultural resources management, and infrastructure upkeep - that affect the lives of all Americans.

**译文：** 实际上，如今用于该机构其他工作的联邦资金变少了；这些工作包括森林保护、流域及文化资源管理和基础设施维护，都会影响全体美国人的生活。

**长难句拆解：**

- 主干：主句：fewer federal funds are going towards the agency's other work（用于该机构其他工作的联邦资金减少了）
- 逻辑：主句说明扑火预算挤占其他项目；破折号列举受挤压的工作；that从句强调这些工作与所有美国人都有关。
- 结构：
  - 总结状语：In effect
  - 主句：fewer federal funds today are going towards the agency's other work
  - 破折号内举例：such as forest conservation, watershed and cultural resources management, and infrastructure upkeep
  - 定语从句(that，修饰 other work)：that affect the lives of all Americans
- 考点：
  - go towards表示“被用于、投入”。
  - upkeep意为“维护、保养”。
  - that的先行词是work，破折号中的列举不改变句法主线。

## 18. E2-2017-T4-P1-S18

But acknowledging fire's inevitable presence in human life is an attitude crucial to developing the laws, policies, and practices that make it as safe as possible, she says.

**译文：** 但她表示，承认火在人类生活中不可避免，是制定法律、政策和实践、尽可能降低火灾风险的关键态度。

**长难句拆解：**

- 主干：主句：acknowledging fire's inevitable presence…is an attitude crucial to developing…（承认火不可避免是一种制定安全制度所必需的态度）
- 逻辑：动名词短语概括应有的认知态度；crucial to说明这种态度对制度建设的重要性；that从句给出制度建设的目标。
- 结构：
  - 转折连词：But
  - 动名词短语作主语：acknowledging fire's inevitable presence in human life
  - 系表结构：is an attitude
  -   └ 形容词短语作后置定语：crucial to developing the laws, policies, and practices
  -       └ 定语从句(that，修饰 laws, policies, and practices)：that make it as safe as possible
  - 插入性引述：she says
- 考点：
  - acknowledging是全句主语，谓语是is。
  - be crucial to中的to是介词，后接developing。
  - make it as safe as possible中的it指与火共存的处境或火的存在。
