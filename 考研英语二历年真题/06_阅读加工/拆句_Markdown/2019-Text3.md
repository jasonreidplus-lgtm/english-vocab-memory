# 2019 年考研英语二阅读 Text 3 · 逐句精读

## 1. E2-2019-T3-P1-S1

American farmers have been complaining of labor shortages for several years.

**译文：** 几年来，美国农场主一直在抱怨劳动力短缺。

## 2. E2-2019-T3-P1-S2

The complaints are unlikely to stop without an overhaul of immigration rules for farm workers.

**译文：** 如果不彻底改革针对农业工人的移民规定，这些抱怨不太可能停止。

## 3. E2-2019-T3-P1-S3

Congress has obstructed efforts to create a more straightforward visa for agricultural workers that would let foreign workers stay longer in the U.S. and change jobs within the industry.

**译文：** 国会阻挠了为农业工人设立一种更简明签证的努力；这种签证本可让外国工人在美国停留更久，并在该行业内部更换工作。

**长难句拆解：**

- 主干：S+V+O：Congress has obstructed efforts（国会阻挠了相关努力）
- 逻辑：不定式说明 efforts 的目标，that 从句继续说明这种签证能够赋予外国工人的两项并列便利。
- 结构：
  - 主句：Congress has obstructed efforts
  -   └ 不定式作后置定语：to create a more straightforward visa
  -       ├ 介词短语作后置定语：for agricultural workers
  -       └ 定语从句(that，修饰 visa)：that would let foreign workers stay longer in the U.S. and change jobs within the industry
- 考点：
  - obstruct efforts to do：阻挠做某事的努力。
  - straightforward 在此指程序直接、简明。
  - let somebody do 中不定式不带 to。

## 4. E2-2019-T3-P1-S4

If this doesn't change, American businesses, communities, and consumers will be the losers.

**译文：** 如果这种情况不改变，美国企业、社区和消费者都将成为输家。

## 5. E2-2019-T3-P1-S5

Perhaps half of U.S. farm laborers are undocumented immigrants.

**译文：** 美国农业劳工中可能有一半是无证移民。

## 6. E2-2019-T3-P1-S6

As fewer such workers enter the country, the characteristics of the agricultural workforce are changing.

**译文：** 随着进入美国的这类工人减少，农业劳动力的构成特征正在发生变化。

## 7. E2-2019-T3-P1-S7

Today's farm laborers, while still predominantly born in Mexico, are more likely to be settled rather than migrating and more likely to be married than single.

**译文：** 如今的农业劳工虽然仍主要出生在墨西哥，但比过去更可能定居而非迁徙，也更可能已婚而非单身。

**长难句拆解：**

- 主干：S+系动词+并列表语：Today's farm laborers are more likely to be settled and more likely to be married（如今的农业劳工更可能定居且已婚）
- 逻辑：while 插入语保留劳工来源未变这一背景，两个 more likely 对称比较，突出迁移方式和婚姻状态的变化。
- 结构：
  - 主句主语：Today's farm laborers
  -   ├ 让步插入语：while still predominantly born in Mexico
  -   ├ 表语①：are more likely to be settled rather than migrating
  -   └ 并列表语②：and more likely to be married than single
- 考点：
  - predominantly：主要地。
  - settled rather than migrating：定居而非迁徙。
  - more likely to... than... 表示相对可能性。

## 8. E2-2019-T3-P1-S8

They're also aging.

**译文：** 他们的年龄也在增长。

## 9. E2-2019-T3-P1-S9

At the start of this century, about one-third of crop workers were over the age of 35.

**译文：** 本世纪初，大约三分之一的作物工人年龄超过35岁。

## 10. E2-2019-T3-P1-S10

Now more than half are.

**译文：** 现在这一比例已超过一半。

## 11. E2-2019-T3-P1-S11

And picking crops is hard on older bodies.

**译文：** 而采摘作物对年长者的身体负担很大。

## 12. E2-2019-T3-P1-S12

One oft-debated cure for this labor shortage remains as implausible as it's been all along: Native U.S. workers won't be returning to the farm.

**译文：** 对于这种劳动力短缺，一个经常被争论的解决办法仍像从前一样不切实际：美国本土工人不会重返农场。

## 13. E2-2019-T3-P1-S13

Mechanization isn't the answer, either - not yet, at least.

**译文：** 机械化也不是答案，至少目前还不是。

## 14. E2-2019-T3-P1-S14

Production of corn, cotton, rice, soybeans, and wheat has been largely mechanized, but many high-value, labor-intensive crops, such as strawberries, need labor.

**译文：** 玉米、棉花、水稻、大豆和小麦的生产大多已经机械化，但草莓等许多高价值、劳动密集型作物仍然需要人工。

## 15. E2-2019-T3-P1-S15

Even dairy farms, where robots do a small share of milking, have a long way to go before they're automated.

**译文：** 即使在机器人承担少量挤奶工作的奶牛场，实现自动化也还有很长的路要走。

## 16. E2-2019-T3-P1-S16

As a result, farms have grown increasingly reliant on temporary guest workers using the H-2A visa to fill the gaps in the workforce.

**译文：** 因此，农场越来越依赖使用H-2A签证的临时客工来填补劳动力缺口。

## 17. E2-2019-T3-P1-S17

Starting around 2012, requests for the visas rose sharply; from 2011 to 2016 the number of visas issued more than doubled.

**译文：** 大约从2012年起，这类签证的申请量急剧上升；从2011年到2016年，签发的签证数量增加了一倍多。

## 18. E2-2019-T3-P1-S18

The H-2A visa has no numerical cap, unlike the H-2B visa for nonagricultural work, which is limited to 66,000 a year.

**译文：** H-2A签证没有数量上限，这一点不同于非农业工作的H-2B签证，后者每年限额为6.6万份。

**长难句拆解：**

- 主干：S+V+O：The H-2A visa has no numerical cap（H-2A签证没有数量上限）
- 逻辑：主句陈述H-2A无上限，unlike 引入H-2B作对比，which 从句给出后者的具体年度限额。
- 结构：
  - 主句：The H-2A visa has no numerical cap
  -   └ 比较插入语：unlike the H-2B visa for nonagricultural work
  -       └ 非限制性定语从句(which)：which is limited to 66,000 a year
- 考点：
  - numerical cap：数量上限。
  - nonagricultural work：非农业工作。
  - be limited to：被限制在……。

## 19. E2-2019-T3-P1-S19

Even so, employers complain they aren't given all the workers they need.

**译文：** 即便如此，雇主仍抱怨没有获得所需的全部工人。

## 20. E2-2019-T3-P1-S20

The process is cumbersome, expensive, and unreliable.

**译文：** 这一流程繁琐、昂贵且不可靠。

## 21. E2-2019-T3-P1-S21

One survey found that bureaucratic delays led the average H-2A worker to arrive on the job 22 days late.

**译文：** 一项调查发现，官僚程序造成的延误使普通H-2A工人平均晚22天到岗。

## 22. E2-2019-T3-P1-S22

The shortage is compounded by federal immigration raids, which remove some workers and drive others underground.

**译文：** 联邦移民突击搜查进一步加剧了短缺，因为它们会带走一些工人，并迫使另一些工人转入地下。

## 23. E2-2019-T3-P1-S23

In a 2012 survey, 71 percent of tree-fruit growers and almost 80 percent of raisin and berry growers said they were short of labor.

**译文：** 2012年的一项调查中，71%的树生水果种植者以及近80%的葡萄干和浆果种植者表示，他们缺少劳动力。

## 24. E2-2019-T3-P1-S24

Some western farmers have responded by moving operations to Mexico.

**译文：** 一些西部农场主以把经营活动迁往墨西哥作为应对。

## 25. E2-2019-T3-P1-S25

From 1998 to 2000, 14.5 percent of the fruit Americans consumed was imported.

**译文：** 1998年至2000年，美国人消费的水果中有14.5%依赖进口。

## 26. E2-2019-T3-P1-S26

Little more than a decade later, the share of imports was 25.8 percent.

**译文：** 仅十多年后，进口所占比例就达到25.8%。

## 27. E2-2019-T3-P1-S27

In effect, the U.S. can import food or it can import the workers who pick it.

**译文：** 实际上，美国要么进口食品，要么进口采摘食品的工人。
