# 2016 年考研英语二阅读 Text 1 · 逐句精读

## 1. E2-2016-T1-P1-S1

It's true that high-school coding classes aren't essential for learning computer science in college.

**译文：** 诚然，高中的编程课并不是在大学学习计算机科学的必备条件。

## 2. E2-2016-T1-P1-S2

Students without experience can catch up after a few introductory courses, said Tom Cortina, the assistant dean at Carnegie Mellon's School of Computer Science.

**译文：** 卡内基梅隆大学计算机科学学院副院长汤姆·科尔蒂纳说，没有相关经验的学生上过几门入门课程后也能赶上来。

## 3. E2-2016-T1-P1-S3

However, Cortina said, early exposure is beneficial.

**译文：** 不过，科尔蒂纳表示，早期接触编程是有益的。

## 4. E2-2016-T1-P1-S4

When younger kids learn computer science, they learn that it's not just a confusing, endless string of letters and numbers - but a tool to build apps, or create artwork, or test hypotheses.

**译文：** 年龄较小的孩子学习计算机科学时会明白，它不只是一串令人困惑、似乎无穷无尽的字母和数字，而是一种可以用来开发应用、创作艺术作品或检验假设的工具。

**长难句拆解：**

- 主干：主句：they learn that it is not just A but B（他们认识到，计算机科学不只是A，而是B）
- 逻辑：when交代条件与时间；that从句用not just…but…纠正对计算机科学的误解；三个并列不定式说明工具的用途。
- 结构：
  - 时间状语从句(when)：When younger kids learn computer science
  - 主句：they learn
  -   └ 宾语从句(that)：that it's not just a confusing, endless string of letters and numbers - but a tool
  -       └ 不定式并列作后置定语〔修饰 tool〕：to build apps, or create artwork, or test hypotheses
- 考点：
  - not just A but B 表示“不只是A，而是B”。
  - build、create、test由or连接，结构平行。
  - hypothesis意为“假设”，复数为hypotheses。

## 5. E2-2016-T1-P1-S5

It's not as hard for them to transform their thought processes as it is for older students.

**译文：** 与年龄较大的学生相比，他们转变思维方式并没有那么困难。

## 6. E2-2016-T1-P1-S6

Breaking down problems into bite-sized chunks and using code to solve them becomes normal.

**译文：** 把问题分解成便于处理的小块，再用代码解决问题，会成为一种习惯。

## 7. E2-2016-T1-P1-S7

Giving more children this training could increase the number of people interested in the field and help fill the jobs gap, Cortina said.

**译文：** 科尔蒂纳说，让更多孩子接受这种训练，可能会增加对该领域感兴趣的人数，并帮助填补人才缺口。

## 8. E2-2016-T1-P1-S8

Students also benefit from learning something about coding before they get to college, where introductory computer-science classes are packed to the brim, which can drive the less-experienced or -determined students away.

**译文：** 学生在进入大学前先学一点编程也会受益；大学的计算机科学入门课常常人满为患，这可能把经验较少或意志不够坚定的学生拒之门外。

**长难句拆解：**

- 主干：主句：Students also benefit from learning something about coding（学生也会从先学一点编程中受益）
- 逻辑：句子先给出“提前学编程有益”的结论，再用where说明大学入门课拥挤，并用which补充这一情况可能造成的后果。
- 结构：
  - 主句：Students also benefit from learning something about coding
  -   └ 时间状语从句(before)：before they get to college
  -   └ 非限制性定语从句(where，修饰 college)：where introductory computer-science classes are packed to the brim
  -       └ 非限制性定语从句(which，指代前述拥挤情形)：which can drive the less-experienced or -determined students away
- 考点：
  - be packed to the brim意为“挤得满满当当、爆满”。
  - which在此指代前面整个“课程爆满”的事实，而非只指classes。
  - less-experienced or -determined中第二个连字符表示省略less，即less experienced or less determined。

## 9. E2-2016-T1-P1-S9

The Flatiron School, where people pay to learn programming, started as one of the many coding bootcamps that's become popular for adults looking for a career change.

**译文：** 人们付费学习编程的弗莱铁伦学校，最初是众多编程训练营之一；这类训练营在希望转行的成年人中日益流行。

## 10. E2-2016-T1-P1-S10

The high-schoolers get the same curriculum, but "we try to gear lessons toward things they're interested in," said Victoria Friedman, an instructor.

**译文：** 高中生学习同样的课程，但授课教师维多利亚·弗里德曼说：“我们会尽量让课程内容贴近他们感兴趣的事物。”

## 11. E2-2016-T1-P1-S11

For instance, one of the apps the students are developing suggests movies based on your mood.

**译文：** 例如，学生正在开发的一款应用会根据你的心情推荐电影。

## 12. E2-2016-T1-P1-S12

The students in the Flatiron class probably won't drop out of high school and build the next Facebook.

**译文：** 弗莱铁伦班上的学生大概不会辍学去创建下一个脸书。

## 13. E2-2016-T1-P1-S13

Programming languages have a quick turnover, so the "Ruby on Rails" language they learned may not even be relevant by the time they enter the job market.

**译文：** 编程语言更新换代很快，因此等他们进入就业市场时，所学的“Ruby on Rails”语言甚至可能已经不再适用。

## 14. E2-2016-T1-P1-S14

But the skills they learn - how to think logically through a problem and organize the results - apply to any coding language, said Deborah Seehom, an education consultant for the state of North Carolina.

**译文：** 不过，北卡罗来纳州教育顾问黛博拉·西霍恩说，他们学到的技能——如何围绕问题进行逻辑思考并整理结果——适用于任何编程语言。

## 15. E2-2016-T1-P1-S15

Indeed, the Flatiron students might not go into IT at all.

**译文：** 事实上，弗莱铁伦的学生以后可能根本不会进入信息技术行业。

## 16. E2-2016-T1-P1-S16

But creating a future army of coders is not the sole purpose of the classes.

**译文：** 但培养一支未来的程序员大军并不是这些课程的唯一目的。

## 17. E2-2016-T1-P1-S17

These kids are going to be surrounded by computers - in their pockets, in their offices, in their homes - for the rest of their lives.

**译文：** 这些孩子的余生都将被计算机包围——口袋里、办公室里和家里，到处都是。

## 18. E2-2016-T1-P1-S18

The younger they learn how computers think, how to coax the machine into producing what they want - the earlier they learn that they have the power to do that - the better.

**译文：** 他们越早学会计算机如何思考、如何诱导机器产出自己想要的结果，也就越早明白自己有能力做到这一点，因而越好。

**长难句拆解：**

- 主干：关联比较结构：The younger…, the earlier…, the better（越早……就越早……也就越好）
- 逻辑：连续使用the+比较级构成递进关系：越早理解计算机并学会操控机器，就越早意识到自身能力，结果也越有利。
- 结构：
  - 第一层the+比较级：The younger they learn
  -   ├ 宾语从句①(how)：how computers think
  -   └ 宾语从句②(how)：how to coax the machine into producing what they want
  -       └ 宾语从句(what)：what they want
  - 第二层the+比较级：the earlier they learn
  -   └ 宾语从句(that)：that they have the power to do that
  - 结论：the better
- 考点：
  - coax A into doing B意为“诱导/设法使A做B”。
  - how to coax是“疑问词+不定式”，与how computers think并列作learn的宾语。
  - 最后的the better省略了完整主句，属于固定的比较级关联结构。
