# 2017 年考研英语二阅读 Text 1 · 逐句精读

## 1. E2-2017-T1-P1-S1

Every Saturday morning, at 9 am, more than 50,000 runners set off to run 5km around their local park.

**译文：** 每周六上午9点，超过5万名跑者从各自所在的社区公园出发，完成5公里跑。

## 2. E2-2017-T1-P1-S2

The Parkrun phenomenon began with a dozen friends and has inspired 400 events in the UK and more abroad.

**译文：** “公园跑”这一现象始于十几个朋友，如今已催生英国境内400场活动，并传播到更多海外地区。

## 3. E2-2017-T1-P1-S3

Events are free, staffed by thousands of volunteers.

**译文：** 这些活动免费参加，由数千名志愿者负责组织。

## 4. E2-2017-T1-P1-S4

Runners range from four years old to grandparents; their times range from Andrew Baddeley's world record 13 minutes 48 seconds up to an hour.

**译文：** 跑者小到4岁孩童、大到祖父母辈；成绩则从安德鲁·巴德利13分48秒的世界纪录，到一小时不等。

## 5. E2-2017-T1-P1-S5

Parkrun is succeeding where London's Olympic "legacy" is failing.

**译文：** “公园跑”在取得成功，而伦敦奥运会所谓的“遗产”却在失败。

## 6. E2-2017-T1-P1-S6

Ten years ago on Monday, it was announced that the Games of the 30th Olympiad would be in London.

**译文：** 十年前的那个星期一，人们宣布第30届奥运会将在伦敦举行。

## 7. E2-2017-T1-P1-S7

Planning documents pledged that the great legacy of the Games would be to lever a nation of sport lovers away from their couches.

**译文：** 规划文件承诺，奥运会留下的重要遗产将是把一个热爱体育的民族从沙发上拉起来。

**长难句拆解：**

- 主干：主句：Planning documents pledged that the great legacy would be to lever a nation…away from their couches（规划文件承诺，重要遗产将是让全民离开沙发去运动）
- 逻辑：pledged后接that从句说明奥运规划的承诺；从句用不定式定义所谓“重要遗产”的具体内容。
- 结构：
  - 主句：Planning documents pledged
  -   └ 宾语从句(that)：that the great legacy of the Games would be to lever a nation of sport lovers away from their couches
  -       ├ 主语：the great legacy of the Games
  -       └ 表语不定式：to lever a nation of sport lovers away from their couches
- 考点：
  - lever在此作动词，意为“撬动、促使”。
  - lever A away from B可理解为“把A从B处拉开”。
  - a nation of sport lovers是集合性表达，指整个热爱体育的民族。

## 8. E2-2017-T1-P1-S8

The population would be fitter, healthier and produce more winners.

**译文：** 民众会更强健、更健康，也会涌现出更多优胜者。

## 9. E2-2017-T1-P1-S9

It has not happened.

**译文：** 然而，这并没有发生。

## 10. E2-2017-T1-P1-S10

The number of adults doing weekly sport did rise, by nearly 2 million in the run-up to 2012 - but the general population was growing faster.

**译文：** 每周参加体育运动的成年人数量在2012年奥运会前夕确实增加了近200万，但总人口增长得更快。

## 11. E2-2017-T1-P1-S11

Worse, the numbers are now falling at an accelerating rate.

**译文：** 更糟的是，如今这一人数正以越来越快的速度下降。

## 12. E2-2017-T1-P1-S12

The opposition claims primary school pupils doing at least two hours of sport a week have nearly halved.

**译文：** 反对党声称，每周至少锻炼两小时的小学生人数已减少近一半。

## 13. E2-2017-T1-P1-S13

Obesity has risen among adults and children.

**译文：** 成年人和儿童中的肥胖率都上升了。

## 14. E2-2017-T1-P1-S14

Official retrospections continue as to why London 2012 failed to "inspire a generation."

**译文：** 官方仍在不断反思，为何2012年伦敦奥运会未能“激励一代人”。

## 15. E2-2017-T1-P1-S15

The success of Parkrun offers answers.

**译文：** “公园跑”的成功给出了答案。

## 16. E2-2017-T1-P1-S16

Parkrun is not a race but a time trial: Your only competitor is the clock.

**译文：** “公园跑”不是竞赛，而是计时跑：你唯一的对手是时钟。

## 17. E2-2017-T1-P1-S17

The ethos welcomes anybody.

**译文：** 这种理念欢迎任何人参与。

## 18. E2-2017-T1-P1-S18

There is as much joy over a puffed-out first-timer being clapped over the line as there is about top talent shining.

**译文：** 人们为气喘吁吁的初跑者鼓掌越过终点线所感到的喜悦，丝毫不亚于看到顶尖选手大放异彩。

**长难句拆解：**

- 主干：比较结构：There is as much joy over A as there is about B（人们为A感到的喜悦与为B感到的一样多）
- 逻辑：as much…as…把初跑者完赛与高手出彩带来的喜悦等量比较，突出活动不只重视精英成绩。
- 结构：
  - 主句前半：There is as much joy over a puffed-out first-timer being clapped over the line
  -   └ 动名词复合结构：a puffed-out first-timer being clapped over the line
  - 比较从句(as)：as there is about top talent shining
  -   └ 动名词复合结构：top talent shining
- 考点：
  - puffed-out意为“上气不接下气的”。
  - clap sb over the line指“鼓掌为某人加油，直到其越过终点”。
  - being clapped是被动动名词，first-timer是其逻辑主语。

## 19. E2-2017-T1-P1-S19

The Olympic bidders, by contrast, wanted to get more people doing sport and to produce more elite athletes.

**译文：** 相比之下，奥运会申办者既想让更多人参加体育运动，又想培养更多精英运动员。

## 20. E2-2017-T1-P1-S20

The dual aim was mixed up: The stress on success over taking part was intimidating for newcomers.

**译文：** 这两个目标被混为一谈：比起参与本身，人们更强调成功，这让新手望而生畏。

## 21. E2-2017-T1-P1-S21

Indeed, there is something a little absurd in the state getting involved in the planning of such a fundamentally "grassroots" concept as community sports associations.

**译文：** 事实上，让政府参与规划社区体育协会这种本质上属于“草根”的组织，多少有些荒谬。

## 22. E2-2017-T1-P1-S22

If there is a role for government, it should really be getting involved in providing common goods - making sure there is space for playing fields and the money to pave tennis and netball courts, and encouraging the provision of all these activities in schools.

**译文：** 如果政府确有角色，那就应当切实参与公共资源的供给——确保有运动场地和铺设网球场、无挡板篮球场的资金，并鼓励学校开展所有这些活动。

**长难句拆解：**

- 主干：条件句主干：If there is a role for government, it should be getting involved in providing common goods（如果政府有角色，就应参与提供公共资源）
- 逻辑：if先限定政府介入的前提与边界；破折号后的两个并列动名词说明政府应提供哪些基础条件。
- 结构：
  - 条件状语从句(if)：If there is a role for government
  - 主句：it should really be getting involved in providing common goods
  - 破折号后具体解释providing common goods：
  -   ├ making sure there is space for playing fields and the money to pave tennis and netball courts
  -   └ and encouraging the provision of all these activities in schools
- 考点：
  - common goods在此指公共资源或公共产品。
  - make sure后接两个由and连接的存在句成分：space与money。
  - the money to pave…中的不定式修饰money，说明资金用途。

## 23. E2-2017-T1-P1-S23

But successive governments have presided over selling green spaces, squeezing money from local authorities and declining attention on sport in education.

**译文：** 然而，历届政府眼看着绿地被出售，削减地方政府资金，并日益忽视教育中的体育。

## 24. E2-2017-T1-P1-S24

Instead of wordy, worthy strategies, future governments need to do more to provide the conditions for sport to thrive.

**译文：** 未来的政府需要少一些冗长而冠冕堂皇的战略，多为体育蓬勃发展创造条件。

## 25. E2-2017-T1-P1-S25

Or at least not make them worse.

**译文：** 或者至少别让情况变得更糟。
