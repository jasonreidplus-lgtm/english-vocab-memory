# 2020 年考研英语二阅读 Text 4 · 逐句精读

## 1. E2-2020-T4-P1-S1

Now that members of Generation Z are graduating college this spring - the most commonly-accepted definition says this generation was born after 1995, give or take a year - the attention has been rising steadily in recent weeks.

**译文：** 随着Z世代成员在今年春季大学毕业——最普遍接受的定义认为这一代人大约出生于1995年之后——近几周他们受到的关注持续增加。

**长难句拆解：**

- 主干：时间状语从句 + 主句：Now that Generation Z are graduating..., the attention has been rising（既然Z世代正在毕业，关注度持续上升）
- 逻辑：Now that 给出关注增加的现实背景；两个破折号之间插入Z世代的出生年份定义，主句落到近期关注趋势。
- 结构：
  - 原因/时间状语从句(Now that)：members of Generation Z are graduating college this spring
  -   └ 破折号插入：the most commonly accepted definition says
  -       └ 宾语从句(that)：this generation was born after 1995, give or take a year
  - 主句：the attention has been rising steadily in recent weeks
- 考点：
  - now that：既然、由于现在已经。
  - give or take a year：上下相差一年左右。
  - steadily：持续稳定地。

## 5. E2-2020-T4-P1-S5

If "entitled" is the most common adjective, fairly or not, applied to millennials (those born between 1981 and 1995), the catchwords for Generation Z are practical and cautious.

**译文：** 如果说“自以为应得一切”是最常用于千禧一代——无论是否公平，指1981至1995年出生者——的形容词，那么Z世代的关键词就是务实和谨慎。

**长难句拆解：**

- 主干：条件对比句：If 'entitled' is the adjective applied to millennials, the catchwords for Generation Z are practical and cautious（如果千禧一代的标签是……，Z世代的关键词则是……）
- 逻辑：if 在这里并非真实条件，而是建立代际标签对比；插入语承认对千禧一代评价可能不公。
- 结构：
  - 条件从句(if)：entitled is the most common adjective applied to millennials
  -   ├ 评注插入：fairly or not
  -   └ 同位解释：those born between 1981 and 1995
  - 主句：the catchwords for Generation Z are practical and cautious
- 考点：
  - entitled：自认为理应获得特殊待遇的。
  - applied to：用于描述。
  - catchword：关键词、流行标签。

## 16. E2-2020-T4-P1-S16

"Millennials wanted more flexibility in their lives," notes Tanya Michelsen, Associate Director of YouthSight, a UK-based brand manager that conducts regular 60-day surveys of British youth, in findings that might just as well apply to American youth.

**译文：** 英国品牌管理机构YouthSight的副主任坦尼娅·米克尔森指出：“千禧一代希望生活更灵活。”该机构每60天对英国青年开展一次调查，其研究结果很可能也适用于美国青年。

**长难句拆解：**

- 主干：引语 + notes Tanya Michelsen + 定语从句（米克尔森指出……，并补充其调查背景）
- 逻辑：核心是引语及其说话人；两个同位语和两个 that 从句交代机构性质、调查方式及结论的适用范围。
- 结构：
  - 直接引语：Millennials wanted more flexibility in their lives
  - 报道语：notes Tanya Michelsen
  -   ├ 同位语：Associate Director of YouthSight
  -   └ 同位语扩展：a UK-based brand manager
  -       └ 定语从句(that)：that conducts regular 60-day surveys of British youth
  - 句末介词结构：in findings
  -   └ 定语从句(that)：that might just as well apply to American youth
- 考点：
  - might just as well apply to：很可能同样适用于。
  - UK-based：总部或业务基础在英国的。
  - findings 在此指调查结果。
