# 2014 年考研英语二阅读 Text 2 · 逐句精读

## 1. E2-2014-T2-P1-S1

An article in Scientific American has pointed out that empirical research says that, actually, you think you're more beautiful than you are.

**译文：** 《科学美国人》的一篇文章指出，实证研究表明，实际上你认为自己比真实的自己更漂亮。

## 2. E2-2014-T2-P1-S2

We have a deep-seated need to feel good about ourselves and we naturally employ a number of self-enhancing strategies to achieve this.

**译文：** 我们内心深处需要对自己感觉良好，因此会自然而然地采用多种自我美化策略来达到这一目的。

## 3. E2-2014-T2-P1-S3

Social psychologists have amassed oceans of research into what they call the "above average effect", or "illusory superiority", and shown that, for example, 70% of us rate ourselves as above average in leadership, 93% in driving and 85% at getting on well with others – all obviously statistical impossibilities.

**译文：** 社会心理学家对所谓的“高于平均效应”或“虚幻优越感”积累了海量研究，并发现，例如，70%的人认为自己的领导力高于平均水平，93%的人认为自己的驾驶水平高于平均水平，85%的人认为自己比一般人更善于与人相处——这些在统计上显然都不可能。

**长难句拆解：**

- 主干：并列谓语主干：Social psychologists have amassed ... and shown that ...
- 逻辑：先说明研究积累，再用三组不可能同时成立的高比例，实证展示人们普遍高估自己的“虚幻优越感”。
- 结构：
  - 谓语①：have amassed oceans of research into what they call ...
  - what 宾语从句：what they call the 'above average effect' or 'illusory superiority'
  - 谓语②(and)：(have) shown that ...
  - that 从句列举三项自评比例：70% ... leadership, 93% ... driving, 85% ... getting on well with others
  - 破折号总结：all obviously statistical impossibilities
- 考点：
  - what = the phenomenon that，同时作 call 的宾语。
  - rate ourselves as above average 意为“把自己评为高于平均水平”。
  - oceans of 是夸张表达，意为“海量的”。

## 4. E2-2014-T2-P1-S4

We rose-tint our memories and put ourselves into self-affirming situations.

**译文：** 我们会给记忆蒙上一层玫瑰色，并把自己置于能够肯定自我的情境中。

## 5. E2-2014-T2-P1-S5

We become defensive when criticised, and apply negative stereotypes to others to boost our own esteem.

**译文：** 受到批评时，我们会采取防御姿态，还会把负面刻板印象加在别人身上，以提升自尊。

## 6. E2-2014-T2-P1-S6

We stalk around thinking we're hot stuff.

**译文：** 我们昂首阔步，觉得自己魅力非凡。

## 7. E2-2014-T2-P1-S7

Psychologist and behavioural scientist Nicholas Epley oversaw a key study into self-enhancement and attractiveness.

**译文：** 心理学家兼行为科学家尼古拉斯·埃普利主持了一项有关自我美化与外貌吸引力的重要研究。

## 8. E2-2014-T2-P1-S8

Rather than have people simply rate their beauty compared with others, he asked them to identify an original photograph of themselves from a lineup including versions that had been altered to appear more and less attractive.

**译文：** 他没有让人们简单地把自己的外貌与他人比较并评分，而是让他们从一组照片中找出自己的原始照片；这组照片还包括经过处理、显得更漂亮或更不漂亮的版本。

**长难句拆解：**

- 主干：主干：he asked them to identify an original photograph of themselves
- 逻辑：研究设计避开主观打分，改用看似更自动的照片识别任务，以检验受试者是否真的把美化后的形象当作自己。
- 结构：
  - 取舍状语：Rather than have people simply rate their beauty compared with others
  - 主句结构：ask + 宾语 them + 不定式 to identify
  - 来源范围：from a lineup
  - 现在分词短语修饰 lineup：including versions
  - 定语从句(that，修饰 versions)：that had been altered to appear more and less attractive
- 考点：
  - rather than 后与主句形成“不是……而是……”的选择。
  - have people rate 中 have 是使役动词。
  - had been altered 是过去完成时的被动，表示照片在识别前已被处理。

## 9. E2-2014-T2-P1-S9

Visual recognition, reads the study, is "an automatic psychological process, occurring rapidly and intuitively with little or no apparent conscious deliberation".

**译文：** 研究报告写道，视觉识别是“一种自动的心理过程，它迅速而直觉地发生，几乎不需要或完全不需要明显的有意识思考”。

## 10. E2-2014-T2-P1-S10

If the subjects quickly chose a falsely flattering image – which most did – they genuinely believed it was really how they looked.

**译文：** 如果受试者迅速选中一张经过虚假美化的照片——大多数人确实如此——那么他们是真的相信自己原本就长成那样。

## 11. E2-2014-T2-P1-S11

Epley found no significant gender difference in responses.

**译文：** 埃普利发现，不同性别的反应并无显著差异。

## 12. E2-2014-T2-P1-S12

Nor was there any evidence that those who self-enhanced the most (that is, the participants who thought the most positively doctored pictures were real) were doing so to make up for profound insecurities.

**译文：** 也没有证据表明，自我美化程度最高的人——也就是把美化程度最高的照片当成真实照片的参与者——这样做是为了弥补深层的不安全感。

## 13. E2-2014-T2-P1-S13

In fact, those who thought that the images higher up the attractiveness scale were real directly corresponded with those who showed other markers for having higher self-esteem.

**译文：** 事实上，把吸引力等级中更漂亮的照片当成真实照片的人，恰好也是在其他指标上表现出较高自尊的人。

**长难句拆解：**

- 主干：主干：those ... directly corresponded with those ...
- 逻辑：两组“those”其实指同一趋势：越把更美化照片认作真实自我的人，也越表现出其他高自尊指标。
- 结构：
  - 主语：those who thought that the images higher up the attractiveness scale were real
  - 定语从句①(who，修饰前一 those)：who thought that ...
  - thought 的宾语从句(that)：the images ... were real
  - 谓语：directly corresponded with
  - 宾语：those who showed other markers for having higher self-esteem；who 引导定语从句
- 考点：
  - higher up the attractiveness scale 是后置修饰 images。
  - correspond with 在此表示“与……相对应”。
  - markers for having higher self-esteem 指显示较高自尊的其他指标。

## 14. E2-2014-T2-P1-S14

"I don't think the findings that we have are any evidence of personal delusion," says Epley.

**译文：** 埃普利说：“我不认为我们的研究结果能证明个人存在自我欺骗。”

## 15. E2-2014-T2-P1-S15

"It's a reflection simply of people generally thinking well of themselves."

**译文：** “它只不过反映出，人们通常对自己评价良好。”

## 16. E2-2014-T2-P1-S16

If you are depressed, you won't be self-enhancing.

**译文：** 如果你处于抑郁状态，就不会进行自我美化。

## 17. E2-2014-T2-P1-S17

Knowing the results of Epley's study, it makes sense that many people hate photographs of themselves viscerally – on one level, they don't even recognise the person in the picture as themselves.

**译文：** 了解埃普利的研究结果后，就不难理解为什么许多人会本能地讨厌自己的照片：在某种层面上，他们甚至认不出照片里的人就是自己。

## 18. E2-2014-T2-P1-S18

Facebook, therefore, is a self-enhancer's paradise, where people can share only the most flattering photos, the cream of their wit, style, beauty, intellect and lifestyles.

**译文：** 因此，脸书是自我美化者的天堂；人们可以只分享最上镜的照片，以及自己才智、风格、容貌、智力和生活方式中最精华的部分。

## 19. E2-2014-T2-P1-S19

It's not that people's profiles are dishonest, says Catalina Toma of Wisconsin-Madison University, "but they portray an idealised version of themselves."

**译文：** 威斯康星大学麦迪逊分校的卡塔利娜·托马说，人们的个人资料并非不诚实，“只是呈现了一个理想化的自己”。
