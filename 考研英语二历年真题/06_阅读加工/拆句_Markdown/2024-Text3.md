## 3. E2-2024-T3-P1-S3

"The American Medical Association advises physicians that in situations where clear evidence of substantial driving impairment implies a strong threat to patient and public safety, and where the physician's advice to discontinue driving privileges is ignored, it is desirable and ethical to notify the Department of Motor Vehicles," Menke wrote.

**译文：** 门克写道："美国医学会建议医生，如果明确证据表明严重的驾驶能力受损对患者和公众安全构成重大威胁，而医生要求患者停止驾驶的建议又遭到无视，那么通知机动车管理部门是可取且合乎职业伦理的。"

**长难句拆解：**

- 主干：The Association advises physicians that it is desirable and ethical to notify the DMV（医学会建议医生：通知机动车部门是可取且合乎伦理的）
- 逻辑：that从句承载建议内容；两个where并列限定何种情境下应上报，真正主语是后置不定式to notify，it只是形式主语。
- 结构：
  - 引述主句：The American Medical Association advises physicians
  -   └ 宾语从句(that)：that ... it is desirable and ethical to notify the Department of Motor Vehicles
  -       ├ 条件/情境从句(where)：where clear evidence ... implies a strong threat ...
  -       ├ 并列情境从句(and where)：where the physician's advice ... is ignored
  -       └ 形式主语结构：it is desirable and ethical to notify ...
  - 引述来源：Menke wrote
- 考点：
  - driving impairment 指驾驶能力受损。
  - discontinue driving privileges 指停止驾驶资格或权利。
  - Department of Motor Vehicles 是美国州级机动车管理部门。

## 6. E2-2024-T3-P1-S6

Part of the problem in keeping older drivers safe is that the difficulties are addressed piecemeal by different professions with different focuses, including gerontologists, highway administration officials, automotive engineers and others, said gerontologist Elizabeth Dugan.

**译文：** 老年驾驶者安全问题之所以难以解决，部分原因在于，不同专业领域各有侧重、各自零散地处理这些困难；其中包括老年学专家、公路管理官员、汽车工程师等，老年学家伊丽莎白·杜根说。

**长难句拆解：**

- 主干：Part of the problem is that the difficulties are addressed piecemeal（部分问题在于这些困难被零散处理）
- 逻辑：表语从句解释problem的具体内容：不同专业各有重点，导致问题被割裂处理；including列出profession的例子。
- 结构：
  - 主句：Part of the problem in keeping older drivers safe is
  -   └ 表语从句(that)：that the difficulties are addressed piecemeal
  -       ├ 方式/施事：by different professions with different focuses
  -       └ 举例分词结构：including gerontologists, highway administration officials, automotive engineers and others
  - 引述来源：said gerontologist Elizabeth Dugan
- 考点：
  - piecemeal 意为“零散地、缺少统筹地”。
  - with different focuses 修饰 professions。
  - keeping older drivers safe 是problem的介词补足成分。

## 13. E2-2024-T3-P1-S13

Primary care providers have their hands full and may not be able to follow through with patients who have trouble driving because they can't turn their heads or remember where they are going - or have gotten shorter and haven't changed their seat settings sufficiently to reach car pedals easily.

**译文：** 基层医疗人员本就忙得不可开交，可能无力继续跟进那些驾驶有困难的患者；这些患者也许无法转头或记不住目的地，也许身高变矮后没有充分调整座椅，因而难以轻松踩到汽车踏板。

**长难句拆解：**

- 主干：Providers may not be able to follow through with patients（基层医疗人员可能无法继续跟进患者）
- 逻辑：主句先说明基层医疗人员忙碌并难以跟进；who锁定需要跟进的患者，because后以并列结构列出身体、认知和座椅调整方面的驾驶困难。
- 结构：
  - 并列分句一：Primary care providers have their hands full
  - 并列分句二：and may not be able to follow through with patients
  -   └ 定语从句(who)：who have trouble driving
  -       └ 原因状语从句(because)：because they can't turn their heads or remember ... or have gotten shorter ...
  -           ├ 并列原因一：can't turn their heads or remember where they are going
  -           └ 并列原因二：have gotten shorter and haven't changed their seat settings sufficiently to reach ...
- 考点：
  - have one's hands full 意为“忙得不可开交”。
  - follow through with patients 指继续跟进并落实照护。
  - where they are going 是remember的宾语从句。
