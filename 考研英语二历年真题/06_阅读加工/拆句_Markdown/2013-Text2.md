# 2013 年考研英语二阅读 Text 2 · 逐句精读

## 1. E2-2013-T2-P1-S1

A century ago, the immigrants from across the Atlantic included settlers and sojourners.

**译文：** 一个世纪以前，横渡大西洋而来的移民中，既有定居者，也有短暂停留者。

## 2. E2-2013-T2-P1-S2

Along with the many folks looking to make a permanent home in the United States came those who had no intention to stay, and who would make some money and then go home.

**译文：** 在众多打算在美国永久安家的人之外，也有一些人无意久留；他们赚上一些钱后便会回国。

**长难句拆解：**

- 主干：倒装主干：Along with A came those ...（与A一同到来的还有那些人）
- 逻辑：作者用 along with 引出的倒装，把永久定居者与赚够钱便返乡的短期移民并置，突出历史移民形态的多样性。
- 结构：
  - 句首介词短语：Along with the many folks looking to make a permanent home in the United States
  - 完全倒装：came those（正常语序为 those came）
  - 定语从句①(who，修饰 those)：who had no intention to stay
  - 并列定语从句②(and who)：who would make some money and then go home
- 考点：
  - 句首较长的 Along with 短语触发完全倒装，真正主语是 those。
  - looking to... 是现在分词短语，修饰 folks。
  - have no intention to do 表示“无意做某事”。

That framework has contributed mightily to our broken immigration system and the long political paralysis over how to fix it.

**译文：** 农作物采摘工、小提琴手、建筑工人、企业家、工程师、居家医疗护理员和物理学家，都属于今天的“候鸟”。

## 15. E2-2013-T2-P1-S15

They are energetic participants in a global economy driven by the flow of work, money and ideas.

**译文：** 他们积极参与着由工作、资金和思想流动所驱动的全球经济。

## 16. E2-2013-T2-P1-S16

## 17. E2-2013-T2-P1-S17

**译文：** 他们能够做到在一个地方工作、在另一个地方安家。

## 18. E2-2013-T2-P1-S18

With or without permission, they straddle laws, jurisdictions and identities with ease.

**译文：** 无论有无许可，他们都能轻松跨越法律、司法管辖区和身份认同的边界。

## 19. E2-2013-T2-P1-S19

We need them to imagine the United States as a place where they can be productive for a while without committing themselves to staying forever.

**译文：** 我们需要让他们把美国设想成这样一个地方：可以在这里高效工作一段时间，却不必承诺永久留下。

**长难句拆解：**

- 主干：主干：We need them to imagine the United States as a place（我们需要他们把美国想象成一个地方）
- 逻辑：句子提出一种新的移民预期：允许移民阶段性贡献，而不把永久定居作为获得接纳的前提。
- 结构：
  - need + 宾语 + 不定式：need them to imagine ...
  - imagine A as B：把 the United States 设想为 a place
  - 定语从句(where，修饰 place)：where they can be productive for a while
  - 伴随状语(without)：without committing themselves to staying forever
- 考点：
  - need them to do 是“需要他们做”，不是“需要他们来做某物”。
  - commit oneself to 中 to 是介词，后接 staying。
  - productive 在此指“从事生产性工作、作出贡献”。

## 20. E2-2013-T2-P1-S20

We need them to feel that home can be both here and there and that they can belong to two nations honorably.

**译文：** 我们需要让他们感到，家既可以在这里，也可以在那里，而且他们能够体面地归属于两个国家。

## 21. E2-2013-T2-P1-S21

Accommodating this new world of people in motion will require new attitudes on both sides of the immigration battle.

**译文：** 要适应这个人员不断流动的新世界，移民争论的双方都必须形成新的态度。

## 22. E2-2013-T2-P1-S22

Looking beyond the culture war logic of right or wrong means opening up the middle ground and understanding that managing immigration today requires multiple paths and multiple outcomes, including some that are not easy to accomplish legally in the existing system.

**译文：** 超越非对即错的文化战争逻辑，意味着要开辟中间地带，并认识到当今的移民管理需要多种路径和多种结果，其中有些结果在现行制度下很难通过合法途径实现。

**长难句拆解：**

- 主干：主干：Looking beyond ... means opening up ... and understanding ...
- 逻辑：本句把“超越二元对立”具体化为两步：开辟中间地带，并接受移民治理需要多路径、多结果。
- 结构：
  - 动名词短语作主语：Looking beyond the culture war logic of right or wrong
  - 并列宾语①：opening up the middle ground
  - 并列宾语②：understanding that managing immigration today requires ...
  - that 宾语从句内，动名词短语 managing immigration today 作主语
  - including 后置补充 outcomes；定语从句(that)：that are not easy to accomplish legally in the existing system
- 考点：
  - look beyond 意为“越过表面框架来看”。
  - means 后的 opening 与 understanding 平行。
  - including some 中 some 指代 outcomes；定语从句说明部分结果在现行制度下难以合法实现。
