# 2015 年考研英语二阅读 Text 4 · 逐句精读

## 1. E2-2015-T4-P1-S1

Many people talked of the 288,000 new jobs the Labor Department reported for June, along with the drop in the unemployment rate to 6.1 percent, as good news.

**译文：** 许多人把劳工部报告的6月份新增28.8万个工作岗位，以及失业率降至6.1%，视为好消息。

## 2. E2-2015-T4-P1-S2

And they were right.

**译文：** 他们的看法是对的。

## 3. E2-2015-T4-P1-S3

For now it appears the economy is creating jobs at a decent pace.

**译文：** 目前看来，经济正在以不错的速度创造就业岗位。

## 4. E2-2015-T4-P1-S4

We still have a long way to go to get back to full employment, but at least we are now finally moving forward at a faster pace.

**译文：** 我们距离恢复充分就业仍有很长的路要走，但至少现在终于在以更快的速度前进。

## 5. E2-2015-T4-P1-S5

However, there is another important part of the jobs picture that was largely overlooked.

**译文：** 然而，就业图景中还有另一个重要部分在很大程度上被忽视了。

## 6. E2-2015-T4-P1-S6

There was a big jump in the number of people who report voluntarily working part-time.

**译文：** 自愿从事兼职工作的人数大幅增加。

## 7. E2-2015-T4-P1-S7

This figure is now 830,000 (4.4 percent) above its year ago level.

**译文：** 这一数字目前比一年前高出83万人，即增长4.4%。

## 8. E2-2015-T4-P1-S8

Before explaining the connection to the Obamacare, it is worth making an important distinction.

**译文：** 在说明这与“奥巴马医改”的联系之前，有必要作出一个重要区分。

## 9. E2-2015-T4-P1-S9

Many people who work part-time jobs actually want full-time jobs.

**译文：** 许多从事兼职工作的人实际上想要全职工作。

## 10. E2-2015-T4-P1-S10

They take part-time work because this is all they can get.

**译文：** 他们接受兼职，只是因为只能找到这样的工作。

## 11. E2-2015-T4-P1-S11

An increase in involuntary part-time work is evidence of weakness in the labor market and it means that many people will be having a very hard time making ends meet.

**译文：** 非自愿兼职增加表明劳动力市场疲弱，也意味着许多人将很难维持收支平衡。

**长难句拆解：**

- 主干：并列主干：An increase ... is evidence ..., and it means that ...
- 逻辑：句子把非自愿兼职增加解释为双重警报：宏观上说明劳动力市场疲弱，微观上意味着个人收支困难。
- 结构：
  - 主语：An increase in involuntary part-time work
  - 系表结构①：is evidence of weakness in the labor market
  - 并列分句②(and)：it means that ...
  - 宾语从句(that)：many people will be having a very hard time making ends meet
- 考点：
  - it 回指 An increase in involuntary part-time work。
  - have a hard time doing 表示“做某事很困难”。
  - make ends meet 表示“维持收支平衡”。

## 12. E2-2015-T4-P1-S12

There was an increase in involuntary part-time in June, but the general direction has been down.

**译文：** 6月份非自愿兼职人数有所增加，但总体趋势一直向下。

## 13. E2-2015-T4-P1-S13

Involuntary part-time employment is still far higher than before the recession, but it is down by 640,000 (7.9 percent) from its year ago level.

**译文：** 非自愿兼职就业人数仍远高于经济衰退前的水平，但已经比一年前减少64万人，即下降7.9%。

## 14. E2-2015-T4-P1-S14

We know the difference between voluntary and involuntary part-time employment because people tell us.

**译文：** 我们之所以知道自愿兼职与非自愿兼职的区别，是因为人们会亲口告诉调查人员。

## 15. E2-2015-T4-P1-S15

The survey used by the Labor Department asks people if they worked less than 35 hours in the reference week.

**译文：** 劳工部采用的调查会询问受访者在调查所指的那一周是否工作不足35小时。

## 16. E2-2015-T4-P1-S16

If the answer is "yes," they are classified as working part-time.

**译文：** 如果回答“是”，他们就被归类为兼职工作者。

## 17. E2-2015-T4-P1-S17

The survey then asks whether they worked less than 35 hours in that week because they wanted to work less than full time or because they had no choice.

**译文：** 随后，调查会询问他们那一周工作不足35小时，是因为自己想少于全职工时，还是因为别无选择。

**长难句拆解：**

- 主干：主干：The survey then asks whether A or B
- 逻辑：调查以受访者陈述的原因划分兼职性质：主动减少工时属于自愿兼职，因无其他选择而减少则属于非自愿兼职。
- 结构：
  - whether 引导宾语从句：whether they worked less than 35 hours ... because ... or because ...
  - 时间限定：in that week
  - 原因分支①：because they wanted to work less than full time
  - 原因分支②：or because they had no choice
- 考点：
  - whether ... or ... 构成二选一。
  - less than full time 指少于全职工时。
  - have no choice 表示“别无选择”，后文据此完成分类。

## 18. E2-2015-T4-P1-S18

They are only classified as voluntary part-time workers if they tell the survey taker they chose to work less than 35 hours a week.

**译文：** 只有当受访者告诉调查员自己主动选择每周工作不足35小时，他们才会被归类为自愿兼职工作者。

## 19. E2-2015-T4-P1-S19

The issue of voluntary part-time relates to Obamacare because one of the main purposes was to allow people to get insurance outside of employment.

**译文：** 自愿兼职之所以与“奥巴马医改”有关，是因为这项改革的主要目的之一，就是让人们能够在雇佣关系之外获得医疗保险。

## 20. E2-2015-T4-P1-S20

For many people, especially those with serious health conditions or family members with serious health conditions, before Obamacare the only way to get insurance was through a job that provided health insurance.

**译文：** 对许多人而言，尤其是自己或家人患有严重疾病的人，医改之前获得保险的唯一办法，是找到一份提供医疗保险的工作。

**长难句拆解：**

- 主干：主干：the only way to get insurance was through a job
- 逻辑：句子说明医改前“就业绑定保险”的现实，尤其突出患重病者及其家庭为获得保险而不得不依赖全职岗位。
- 结构：
  - 范围状语：For many people
  - 插入具体化：especially those with serious health conditions or family members with serious health conditions
  - 时间状语：before Obamacare
  - 主语中心：the only way；不定式后置定语：to get insurance
  - 介词表途径：through a job
  - 定语从句(that，修饰 job)：that provided health insurance
- 考点：
  - those 指 people。
  - with serious health conditions 可修饰本人，也在后半并列中修饰 family members。
  - through a job 表示“通过一份工作这一渠道”。

## 21. E2-2015-T4-P1-S21

However, Obamacare has allowed more than 12 million people to either get insurance through Medicaid or the exchanges.

**译文：** 然而，“奥巴马医改”已经让超过1200万人能够通过医疗补助计划或保险交易平台获得保险。

## 22. E2-2015-T4-P1-S22

These are people who may previously have felt the need to get a full-time job that provided insurance in order to cover themselves and their families.

**译文：** 这些人此前可能觉得，必须找到一份提供保险的全职工作，才能保障自己和家人。

## 23. E2-2015-T4-P1-S23

With Obamacare there is no longer a link between employment and insurance.

**译文：** 有了“奥巴马医改”，就业与医疗保险之间不再存在必然联系。
