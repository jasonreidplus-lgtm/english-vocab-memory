# 2020 年考研英语二阅读 Text 2 · 逐句精读

## 1. E2-2020-T2-P1-S1

It is true that CEO pay has gone up - top ones may make 300 times the pay of typical workers on average, and since the mid-1970s, CEO pay for large publicly traded American corporations has, by varying estimates, gone up by about 500%.

**译文：** 首席执行官薪酬确实上涨了：顶尖首席执行官的收入平均可能达到普通员工的300倍；据不同估算，自20世纪70年代中期以来，美国大型上市公司首席执行官的薪酬已上涨约500%。

**长难句拆解：**

- 主干：S+系动词+表语从句：It is true that CEO pay has gone up（首席执行官薪酬上涨是事实）
- 逻辑：that 从句是句子的真正主语；后面用两个量化信息分别呈现薪酬倍数和历史涨幅，支撑“上涨”这一判断。
- 结构：
  - 形式主语结构：It is true
  -   └ 真正主语从句(that)：CEO pay has gone up
  -       ├ 破折号补充：top ones may make 300 times the pay of typical workers
  -       └ 并列分句(and)：since the mid-1970s, CEO pay ... has gone up by about 500%
  -           └ 插入语：by varying estimates
- 考点：
  - 300 times the pay of...：……薪酬的300倍。
  - by varying estimates：根据不同估算。
  - go up by 500% 指涨幅约为500%。

## 2. E2-2020-T2-P1-S2

The typical CEO of a top American corporation now makes about $18.9 million a year.

**译文：** 如今，美国一家顶级企业的普通首席执行官每年收入约为1890万美元。

## 3. E2-2020-T2-P1-S3

The best model for understanding the growth of CEO pay is that of limited CEO talent in a world where business opportunities for the top firms are growing rapidly.

**译文：** 理解首席执行官薪酬增长的最佳模型是：在顶尖企业商业机会迅速增加的世界里，优秀首席执行官人才数量有限。

## 4. E2-2020-T2-P1-S4

The efforts of America's highest-earning 1% have been one of the more dynamic elements of the global economy.

**译文：** 美国收入最高的1%人群所付出的努力，一直是全球经济中更具活力的因素之一。

## 5. E2-2020-T2-P1-S5

It's not popular to say, but one reason their pay has gone up so much is that CEOs really have upped their game relative to many other workers in the U.S. economy.

**译文：** 这种说法并不讨喜，但他们的薪酬之所以大幅上涨，一个原因是相较于美国经济中的许多其他劳动者，首席执行官确实提升了自己的能力水平。

**长难句拆解：**

- 主干：转折复句：It's not popular to say, but one reason... is that...（这种说法不讨喜，但一个原因是……）
- 逻辑：but 将舆论接受度与作者解释转折连接；one reason...is that 是核心因果框架，relative to 给出比较基准。
- 结构：
  - 分句①：It's not popular to say
  - 转折分句(but)：one reason ... is
  -   ├ 定语从句〔省略 why/that，修饰 reason〕：their pay has gone up so much
  -   └ 表语从句(that)：CEOs really have upped their game
  -       └ 比较状语：relative to many other workers in the U.S. economy
- 考点：
  - up one's game：提高能力或表现。
  - relative to：相对于。
  - reason 后省略的关系词在定语从句中作状语。

## 6. E2-2020-T2-P1-S6

Today's CEO, at least for major American firms, must have many mere skills than simply being able to "run the company."

**译文：** 如今的首席执行官，至少是美国大公司的首席执行官，除了能够“经营公司”之外，还必须具备更多技能。

## 7. E2-2020-T2-P1-S7

CEOs must have a good sense of financial markets and maybe even how the company should trade in them.

**译文：** 首席执行官必须对金融市场有良好判断，甚至可能还要懂得公司应如何在这些市场中交易。

## 8. E2-2020-T2-P1-S8

They also need better public relations skills than their predecessors, as the costs of even a minor slipup can be significant.

**译文：** 他们还需要比前任具备更强的公共关系能力，因为哪怕一次小小的失误也可能代价高昂。

## 9. E2-2020-T2-P1-S9

Then there's the fact that large American companies are much more globalized than ever before, with supply chains spread across a larger number of countries.

**译文：** 此外还有一个事实：美国大公司比以往任何时候都更加全球化，其供应链遍布更多国家。

## 10. E2-2020-T2-P1-S10

To lead in that system requires knowledge that is fairly mind-boggling.

**译文：** 领导这样一个体系，需要极为庞杂、令人难以想象的知识。

## 11. E2-2020-T2-P1-S11

Plus, virtually all major American companies are becoming tech companies, one way or another.

**译文：** 此外，几乎所有美国大公司都正以这样或那样的方式变成科技公司。

## 12. E2-2020-T2-P1-S12

Beyond this, major CEOs still have to do all the day-to-day work they have always done.

**译文：** 除此之外，大公司的首席执行官仍要完成他们一向承担的全部日常工作。

## 13. E2-2020-T2-P1-S13

The common idea that high CEO pay is mainly about ripping people off doesn't explain history very well.

**译文：** 那种认为高额首席执行官薪酬主要是在剥削他人的常见观点，并不能很好地解释历史。

## 14. E2-2020-T2-P1-S14

By most measures, corporate governance has become a lot tighter and more rigorous since the 1970s.

**译文：** 按照大多数衡量标准，自20世纪70年代以来，公司治理已经严格、严谨得多。

## 15. E2-2020-T2-P1-S15

Yet it is principally during this period of stronger governance that CEO pay has been high and rising.

**译文：** 然而，首席执行官薪酬居高不下且持续上涨，主要恰恰发生在治理更强的这一时期。

## 16. E2-2020-T2-P1-S16

That suggests it is in the broader corporate interest to recruit top candidates for increasingly tough jobs.

**译文：** 这表明，为日益艰难的职位招募顶尖人选，符合公司更广泛的利益。

## 17. E2-2020-T2-P1-S17

Furthermore, the highest CEO salaries are paid to outside candidates, not to the cozy insider picks, another sign that high CEO pay is not some kind of depredation at the expense of the rest of the company.

**译文：** 此外，最高的首席执行官薪酬给的是外部候选人，而不是关系亲密的内部人选；这进一步说明，高额首席执行官薪酬并非以公司其他成员为代价的某种掠夺。

**长难句拆解：**

- 主干：被动主句 + 同位解释：The highest CEO salaries are paid to outside candidates..., another sign that...（最高薪酬给外部人选，这是……的又一迹象）
- 逻辑：外部候选人和内部熟人形成对比，同位语 another sign 将这一事实转化为反驳“掠夺说”的证据。
- 结构：
  - 主句：the highest CEO salaries are paid to outside candidates
  -   └ 对比插入：not to the cozy insider picks
  - 同位补充：another sign
  -   └ 同位语从句(that)：high CEO pay is not some kind of depredation
  -       └ 介词短语：at the expense of the rest of the company
- 考点：
  - cozy insider picks：关系亲密的内部人选。
  - depredation：掠夺、侵占。
  - at the expense of：以……为代价。

## 18. E2-2020-T2-P1-S18

And the stock market reacts positively when companies tie CEO pay to, say, stock prices, a sign that those practices build up corporate value not just for the CEO.

**译文：** 而且，当公司把首席执行官薪酬与股价等指标挂钩时，股票市场会作出积极反应；这说明这些做法不仅为首席执行官本人，也为公司创造了价值。
