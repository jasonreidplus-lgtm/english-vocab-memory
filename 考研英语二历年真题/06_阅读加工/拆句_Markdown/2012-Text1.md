# 2012 年考研英语二阅读 Text 1 · 逐句精读

## 1. E2-2012-T1-P1-S1

Homework has never been terribly popular with students and even many parents, but in recent years it has been particularly scorned.

**译文：** 家庭作业从来都不太受学生、甚至许多家长欢迎，而近年来它尤其受到轻视。

## 2. E2-2012-T1-P1-S2

School districts across the country, most recently Los Angeles Unified, are revising their thinking on this educational ritual.

**译文：** 全国各地的学区——最近一个是洛杉矶联合学区——都在重新审视对这一教育惯例的看法。

## 3. E2-2012-T1-P1-S3

Unfortunately, L.A. Unified has produced an inflexible policy which mandates that with the exception of some advanced courses, homework may no longer count for more than 10% of a student's academic grade.

**译文：** 遗憾的是，洛杉矶联合学区制定了一项僵化政策，规定除少数高级课程外，家庭作业在学生学业成绩中所占比例不得超过10%。

**长难句拆解：**

- 主干：S+V+O：L.A. Unified has produced an inflexible policy（洛杉矶联合学区制定了一项僵化政策）
- 逻辑：which 从句说明政策内容；mandates 后 that 从句给出具体规定，with the exception of... 限定规定的适用范围。
- 结构：
  - 主句：Unfortunately, L.A. Unified has produced an inflexible policy
  -   └ 定语从句〔which，修饰 policy〕：which mandates
  -       └ 宾语从句(that)：homework may no longer count for more than 10% of a student's academic grade
  -           └ 例外状语：with the exception of some advanced courses
- 考点：
  - mandate 在此是动词，意为“强制规定”。
  - count for more than 10% 指在总成绩中占比超过10%。
  - with the exception of：除……之外。

## 4. E2-2012-T1-P1-S4

This rule is meant to address the difficulty that students from impoverished or chaotic homes might have in completing their homework.

**译文：** 这项规定旨在解决来自贫困或混乱家庭的学生完成作业时可能遇到的困难。

## 5. E2-2012-T1-P1-S5

But the policy is unclear and contradictory.

**译文：** 然而，这项政策含糊不清且自相矛盾。

## 6. E2-2012-T1-P1-S6

Certainly, no homework should be assigned that students cannot complete on their own or that they cannot do without expensive equipment.

**译文：** 当然，不应布置学生无法独立完成，或必须依靠昂贵设备才能完成的作业。

## 7. E2-2012-T1-P1-S7

But if the district is essentially giving a pass to students who do not do their homework because of complicated family lives, it is going riskily close to the implication that standards need to be lowered for poor children.

**译文：** 但如果学区实质上因为学生家庭生活复杂，就放过那些不做作业的学生，那便危险地接近于暗示：针对贫困儿童，标准需要降低。

**长难句拆解：**

- 主干：条件复句：If the district is giving a pass..., it is going close to the implication...（如果学区放过……，就接近于暗示……）
- 逻辑：if 建立政策做法与危险含义之间的条件推论；who 说明被放过的学生；that 从句把潜在暗示具体化。
- 结构：
  - 条件状语从句(if)：the district is essentially giving a pass to students
  -   ├ 定语从句〔who，修饰 students〕：who do not do their homework
  -   └ 原因状语：because of complicated family lives
  - 主句：it is going riskily close to the implication
  -   └ 同位语从句(that)〔说明 implication〕：that standards need to be lowered for poor children
- 考点：
  - give a pass to somebody 指对某人免予追究、放宽要求。
  - go close to the implication 可理解为“几乎等于暗示”。
  - need to be lowered 是被动结构，指标准被降低。

## 8. E2-2012-T1-P1-S8

District administrators say that homework will still be a part of schooling; teachers are allowed to assign as much of it as they want.

**译文：** 学区管理人员表示，家庭作业仍会是学校教育的一部分；教师想布置多少都可以。

## 9. E2-2012-T1-P1-S9

But with homework counting for no more than 10% of their grades, students can easily skip half their homework and see very little difference on their report cards.

**译文：** 但由于作业在总成绩中所占比例不超过10%，学生即便轻易少做一半作业，成绩单也几乎不会有什么变化。

## 10. E2-2012-T1-P1-S10

Some students might do well on state tests without completing their homework, but what about the students who performed well on the tests and did their homework?

**译文：** 有些学生不完成作业也可能在州统考中表现不错，但那些既考得好又完成了作业的学生又该怎么说呢？

## 11. E2-2012-T1-P1-S11

It is quite possible that the homework helped.

**译文：** 很可能正是家庭作业起了帮助作用。

## 12. E2-2012-T1-P1-S12

Yet rather than empowering teachers to find what works best for their students, the policy imposes a flat, across-the-board rule.

**译文：** 然而，这项政策没有赋予教师权力去寻找最适合自己学生的方法，反而强加了一条统一适用于所有人的僵化规定。

## 13. E2-2012-T1-P1-S13

At the same time, the policy addresses none of the truly thorny questions about homework.

**译文：** 与此同时，这项政策没有处理任何有关家庭作业的真正棘手问题。

## 14. E2-2012-T1-P1-S14

If the district finds homework to be unimportant to its students' academic achievement, it should move to reduce or eliminate the assignments, not make them count for almost nothing.

**译文：** 如果学区认定家庭作业对学生的学业成绩并不重要，就应着手减少或取消作业，而不是让它几乎不计入成绩。

## 15. E2-2012-T1-P1-S15

Conversely, if homework matters, it should account for a significant portion of the grade.

**译文：** 反过来说，如果家庭作业确实重要，它就应在成绩中占据相当大的比重。

## 16. E2-2012-T1-P1-S16

Meanwhile, this policy does nothing to ensure that the homework students receive is meaningful or appropriate to their age and the subject, or that teachers are not assigning more than they are willing to review and correct.

**译文：** 同时，这项政策完全无法确保学生收到的作业有意义、符合其年龄和学科特点，也无法确保教师不会布置超出自己愿意审阅和批改范围的作业。

**长难句拆解：**

- 主干：S+V+O：This policy does nothing to ensure A or B（这项政策无法确保A或B）
- 逻辑：ensure 后有两个并列 that 从句：一是作业本身合适，二是教师布置量不超过其批改能力；第一个从句内部 meaningful 与 appropriate 并列。
- 结构：
  - 主句：this policy does nothing
  -   └ 目的/结果不定式：to ensure
  -       ├ 宾语从句①(that)：that the homework students receive is meaningful or appropriate to their age and the subject
  -       │   └ 定语从句〔省略 that，修饰 homework〕：students receive
  -       └ 并列宾语从句②(or that)：that teachers are not assigning more than they are willing to review and correct
  -           └ 比较从句(than)：than they are willing to review and correct
- 考点：
  - does nothing to ensure 表示“完全没有保障”。
  - the homework students receive 中省略了关系代词 that。
  - more than they are willing to review and correct 指超过教师愿意审阅、批改的数量。

## 17. E2-2012-T1-P1-S17

The homework rules should be put on hold while the school board, which is responsible for setting educational policy, looks into the matter and conducts public hearings.

**译文：** 在负责制定教育政策的校董会调查此事并举行公开听证会期间，应暂缓实施这些作业规定。

## 18. E2-2012-T1-P1-S18

It is not too late for L.A. Unified to do homework right.

**译文：** 洛杉矶联合学区现在把“作业”做好，还为时不晚。
