# 2018 年考研英语二阅读 Text 2 · 逐句精读

## 1. E2-2018-T2-P1-S1

While fossil fuels - coal, oil, gas - still generate roughly 85 percent of the world's energy supply, it's clearer than ever that the future belongs to renewable sources such as wind and solar.

**译文：** 尽管化石燃料——煤、石油和天然气——仍供应着全球约85%的能源，但有一点比以往任何时候都更加清楚：未来属于风能、太阳能等可再生能源。

**长难句拆解：**

- 主干：主句：it's clearer than ever that the future belongs to renewable sources（比以往更清楚的是，未来属于可再生能源）
- 逻辑：while承认化石燃料当前仍占主导，主句用形式主语it和that真主语从句转向可再生能源代表未来的判断。
- 结构：
  - 让步状语从句(while)：While fossil fuels - coal, oil, gas - still generate roughly 85 percent of the world's energy supply
  -   └ 同位列举：coal, oil, gas〔解释 fossil fuels〕
  - 主句：it's clearer than ever
  -   └ 真正主语从句(that)：that the future belongs to renewable sources such as wind and solar
- 考点：
  - while在此表示让步“尽管”，不是单纯时间关系。
  - it是形式主语，that从句是真正主语。
  - belong to在这里表示“归属于、是……的天下”。

## 12. E2-2018-T2-P1-S12

But that message did not play well with many in Iowa, where wind turbines dot the fields and provide 36 percent of the state's electricity generation - and where tech giants like Microsoft are being attracted by the availability of clean energy to power their data centers.

**译文：** 但这一说法并未得到许多艾奥瓦人的认同；该州田野间遍布风力涡轮机，风电占全州发电量的36%，而微软等科技巨头也因当地有清洁能源可为数据中心供电而被吸引至此。

**长难句拆解：**

- 主干：主句：that message did not play well with many in Iowa（那一说法未获许多艾奥瓦人认同）
- 逻辑：主句指出政治表态在当地反响不佳；两个where从句分别用风电占比和科技企业选址解释原因。
- 结构：
  - 主句：But that message did not play well with many in Iowa
  -   ├ 非限制性定语从句①(where，修饰 Iowa)：where wind turbines dot the fields and provide 36 percent of the state's electricity generation
  -   └ 并列非限制性定语从句②(and where)：where tech giants like Microsoft are being attracted by the availability of clean energy
  -       └ 不定式作后置定语：to power their data centers〔修饰 clean energy〕
- 考点：
  - play well with sb表示“得到某人良好反响/认同”。
  - dot the fields意为“星罗棋布于田野”。
  - be attracted by the availability of…指因某种资源可获得而被吸引。

## 18. E2-2018-T2-P1-S18

The pace of change in energy sources appears to be speeding up - perhaps just in time to have a meaningful effect in slowing climate change.

**译文：** 能源来源的变革速度似乎正在加快——也许恰好来得及对减缓气候变化产生实质性影响。

**长难句拆解：**

- 主干：主句：The pace of change in energy sources appears to be speeding up（能源来源的变革速度似乎正在加快）
- 逻辑：主句判断转型提速；破折号后的perhaps降低断言强度，并说明这种提速可能恰好赶上减缓气候变化所需的时间窗口。
- 结构：
  - 主句：The pace of change in energy sources appears to be speeding up
  - 破折号后补充判断：perhaps just in time
  -   └ 不定式作结果/目的补充：to have a meaningful effect
  -       └ 介词短语：in slowing climate change
- 考点：
  - the pace of change意为“变化的速度”。
  - just in time to do表示“恰好来得及做”。
  - have an effect in doing表示“在做某事方面产生影响”。
