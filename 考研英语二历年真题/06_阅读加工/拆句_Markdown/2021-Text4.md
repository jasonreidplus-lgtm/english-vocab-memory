# 2021 年考研英语二阅读 Text 4 · 逐句精读

## 1. E2-2021-T4-P1-S1

We're fairly good at judging people based on first impressions, thin slices of experience ranging from a glimpse of a photo to five-minute interaction, and deliberation can be not only extraneous but intrusive.

**译文：** 我们相当擅长根据第一印象判断他人；这种判断只取自短暂的经验片段，从匆匆看一眼照片到五分钟的互动不等，而深思熟虑不仅可能多余，还可能造成干扰。

**长难句拆解：**

- 主干：并列复句：We're good at judging people..., and deliberation can be extraneous and intrusive（我们擅长凭短暂经验判断人，而深思可能多余且有干扰）
- 逻辑：前半句定义基于“薄片经验”的判断，后半句用 not only...but...提出核心观点：刻意思考不但没必要，还可能妨碍判断。
- 结构：
  - 分句①：We're fairly good at judging people based on first impressions
  -   └ 同位解释：thin slices of experience
  -       └ 现在分词短语：ranging from a glimpse of a photo to five-minute interaction
  - 并列分句(and)：deliberation can be not only extraneous but intrusive
- 考点：
  - be good at doing：擅长做某事。
  - thin slices of experience：极短的经验片段。
  - not only extraneous but intrusive：不仅多余，而且会造成干扰。

## 8. E2-2021-T4-P1-S8

Ambady suspected that deliberation focused them on vivid but misleading cues, such as certain gestures or utterances, rather than letting the complex interplay of subtle signals form a holistic impression.

**译文：** 安巴迪怀疑，深思使他们把注意力集中在生动却具有误导性的线索上，如某些手势或言语，而没有让各种细微信号之间复杂的相互作用形成整体印象。

**长难句拆解：**

- 主干：S+V+O从句：Ambady suspected that deliberation focused them on cues rather than letting signals form an impression（安巴迪怀疑深思让人关注线索，而非形成整体印象）
- 逻辑：that 从句说明准确率下降的原因；rather than 对比刻意抓取显眼线索和让细微信号整体作用两种处理方式。
- 结构：
  - 主句：Ambady suspected
  -   └ 宾语从句(that)：deliberation focused them on vivid but misleading cues
  -       ├ 举例：such as certain gestures or utterances
  -       └ 对比结构(rather than)：rather than letting the complex interplay of subtle signals form a holistic impression
- 考点：
  - focus somebody on：使某人把注意力集中于。
  - interplay：相互作用。
  - holistic impression：整体印象。

**长难句拆解：**

- 主干：S+V+O：people made decisions that were better and more satisfying（人们作出的决定更好且更令人满意）
- 逻辑：that 说明决定质量，when 交代实验要求；but only if 把直觉优势限制在复杂决策中，破折号再定义何为复杂。
- 结构：
  - 主句：people made car-buying decisions
  -   └ 定语从句(that)：that were both objectively better and more personally satisfying
  - 时间/条件省略结构：when asked to focus on their feelings rather than on details
  - 限制转折：but only if the decision was complex
  -   └ 破折号解释：when they had a lot of information to process
- 考点：
  - objectively better：在客观标准上更好。
  - rather than on details：而不是关注细节。
  - only if：只有在……情况下才。
