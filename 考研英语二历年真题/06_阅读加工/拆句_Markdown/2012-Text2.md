# 2012 年考研英语二阅读 Text 2 · 逐句精读

## 1. E2-2012-T2-P1-S1

Pretty in pink: adult women do not remember being so obsessed with the colour, yet it is pervasive in our young girls' lives.

**译文：** 粉红扮靓：成年女性并不记得自己小时候曾如此痴迷粉红色，但这种颜色如今充斥着小女孩的生活。

## 2. E2-2012-T2-P1-S2

It is not that pink is intrinsically bad, but it is such a tiny slice of the rainbow and, though it may celebrate girlhood in one way, it also repeatedly and firmly fuses girls' identity to appearance.

**译文：** 问题并不在于粉红色本身不好，而在于它只是彩虹中如此狭小的一部分；它或许以某种方式赞美女孩时代，却也反复而牢固地把女孩的身份认同与外表捆绑在一起。

**长难句拆解：**

- 主干：转折主干：It is not that pink is bad, but it is a tiny slice and it fuses identity to appearance（并非粉红色不好，而是它范围狭窄并把身份与外表捆绑）
- 逻辑：not that...but... 先排除对颜色本身的道德判断，再指出问题；though 插入承认其积极一面，主句用 also 转回更深层的负面影响。
- 结构：
  - 结构①：It is not that pink is intrinsically bad
  - 转折结构②(but)：it is such a tiny slice of the rainbow
  - 并列结构③(and)：it also repeatedly and firmly fuses girls' identity to appearance
  -   └ 让步状语从句(though)：though it may celebrate girlhood in one way
- 考点：
  - intrinsically 指“本质上、内在地”。
  - a tiny slice of the rainbow 比喻女孩可选择的色彩和想象空间被压缩。
  - fuse A to B：把A牢牢连接/捆绑到B上。

## 6. E2-2012-T2-P1-S6

Children were not colour-coded at all until the early 20th century: in the era before domestic washing machines all babies wore white as a practical matter, since the only way of getting clothes clean was to boil them.

**译文：** 直到20世纪初，儿童才开始按颜色区分性别；在家用洗衣机出现以前，所有婴儿出于实用考虑都穿白色，因为当时洗净衣服的唯一方法就是煮洗。

**长难句拆解：**

- 主干：S+V：Children were not colour-coded until the early 20th century（直到20世纪初才按颜色区分儿童性别）
- 逻辑：not...until... 表示“直到……才”；冒号后的历史事实解释此前为什么婴儿统一穿白色，since 再给出便于煮洗的原因。
- 结构：
  - 主句：Children were not colour-coded at all until the early 20th century
  - 冒号后解释：in the era before domestic washing machines all babies wore white as a practical matter
  -   └ 原因状语从句(since)：since the only way of getting clothes clean was to boil them
- 考点：
  - colour-code 在此指用不同颜色标记或区分性别。
  - as a practical matter 指出于实际、实用考虑。
  - getting clothes clean 是使衣服变干净。

## 10. E2-2012-T2-P1-S10

It was not until the mid-1980s, when amplifying age and sex differences became a dominant children's marketing strategy, that pink fully came into its own, when it began to seem inherently attractive to girls, part of what defined them as female, at least for the first few critical years.

**译文：** 直到20世纪80年代中期，放大年龄和性别差异成为儿童营销的主导策略，粉红色才真正大行其道；它开始显得天生就吸引女孩，成为界定女性身份的一部分，至少在最初几个关键成长年份是如此。

**长难句拆解：**

- 主干：强调句：It was not until the mid-1980s that pink fully came into its own（直到20世纪80年代中期，粉红色才真正流行起来）
- 逻辑：主轴是 not until...that...；第一个 when 界定80年代中期的营销变化，第二个 when 说明粉红色“大行其道”的具体表现。
- 结构：
  - not until 强调结构：It was not until the mid-1980s ... that pink fully came into its own
  -   └ 时间定语从句(when)：when amplifying age and sex differences became a dominant children's marketing strategy
  - 补充时间从句(when)：when it began to seem inherently attractive to girls
  -   ├ 同位补充：part of what defined them as female
  -   │   └ 宾语从句(what)：what defined them as female
  -   └ 范围状语：at least for the first few critical years
- 考点：
  - come into one's own 指开始充分展现影响力、真正走红。
  - amplifying differences 指人为放大差异。
  - what defined them as female 中 what 同时作 define 的主语并引导名词性从句。
