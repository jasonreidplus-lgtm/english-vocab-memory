# 2017 年考研英语二阅读 Text 3 · 逐句精读

## 1. E2-2017-T3-P1-S1

Today, widespread social pressure to immediately go to college in conjunction with increasingly high expectations in a fast-moving world often causes students to completely overlook the possibility of taking a gap year.

**译文：** 如今，在一个快速变化的世界里，立即上大学的广泛社会压力与日益升高的期望共同作用，常常使学生完全忽略休学间隔年这种可能性。

**长难句拆解：**

- 主干：主句：social pressure…often causes students to overlook the possibility（社会压力常使学生忽略这种可能性）
- 逻辑：长主语由“立即上大学的社会压力”构成，并用in conjunction with加入高期望这一共同因素；cause A to do B说明二者造成的结果。
- 结构：
  - 主语：widespread social pressure to immediately go to college
  -   ├ 不定式作后置定语：to immediately go to college
  -   └ 介词结构：in conjunction with increasingly high expectations in a fast-moving world
  - 谓语：often causes
  - 宾语+宾补：students to completely overlook the possibility of taking a gap year
- 考点：
  - in conjunction with意为“与……共同、结合”。
  - cause sb to do sth表示“导致某人做某事”。
  - the possibility of doing中的of taking a gap year具体说明possibility。

## 2. E2-2017-T3-P1-S2

After all, if everyone you know is going to college in the fall, it seems silly to stay back a year, doesn't it?

**译文：** 毕竟，如果你认识的所有人都将在秋季上大学，自己却推迟一年，看起来很傻，不是吗？

## 3. E2-2017-T3-P1-S3

And after going to school for 12 years, it doesn't feel natural to spend a year doing something that isn't academic.

**译文：** 而且，上了12年学以后，花一年时间做与学业无关的事，也会让人觉得不自然。

## 4. E2-2017-T3-P1-S4

But while this may be true, it's not a good enough reason to condemn gap years.

**译文：** 然而，即便这些情况属实，也不足以成为否定间隔年的理由。

## 5. E2-2017-T3-P1-S5

There's always a constant fear of falling behind everyone else on the socially perpetuated "race to the finish line," whether that be toward graduate school, medical school or a lucrative career.

**译文：** 人们总是担心自己在社会持续推动的“冲向终点的竞赛”中落后于所有人，无论那个终点是研究生院、医学院，还是高收入职业。

## 6. E2-2017-T3-P1-S6

But despite common misconceptions, a gap year does not hinder the success of academic pursuits - in fact, it probably enhances it.

**译文：** 但与常见误解相反，间隔年并不会妨碍学业成功；事实上，它很可能会促进学业成功。

## 7. E2-2017-T3-P1-S7

Studies from the United States and Australia show that students who take a gap year are generally better prepared for and perform better in college than those who do not.

**译文：** 美国和澳大利亚的研究表明，与不休间隔年的学生相比，选择间隔年的学生通常为大学生活准备得更充分，学业表现也更好。

## 8. E2-2017-T3-P1-S8

Rather than pulling students back, a gap year pushes them ahead by preparing them for independence, new responsibilities and environmental changes - all things that first-year students often struggle with the most.

**译文：** 间隔年不会拖学生后腿，反而会让他们提前做好准备，去面对独立生活、新的责任和环境变化——这些恰恰是大学一年级学生往往最难应对的事情。

**长难句拆解：**

- 主干：主句：a gap year pushes them ahead by preparing them for…（间隔年通过让学生做好准备来推动他们前进）
- 逻辑：rather than先否定“拖后腿”的误解，主句转而说明间隔年的推进作用；破折号后的all things概括前三项并补充其现实难度。
- 结构：
  - 对比状语：Rather than pulling students back
  - 主句：a gap year pushes them ahead
  -   └ 方式状语(by)：by preparing them for independence, new responsibilities and environmental changes
  - 同位概括：all things
  -   └ 定语从句(that)：that first-year students often struggle with the most
- 考点：
  - rather than doing表示“不是……而是……”，句中与pushes形成语义对照。
  - prepare sb for sth意为“使某人为……做好准备”。
  - struggle with的介词with被保留在定语从句末尾。

## 9. E2-2017-T3-P1-S9

Gap year experiences can lessen the blow when it comes to adjusting to college and being thrown into a brand new environment, making it easier to focus on academics and activities rather than acclimation blunders.

**译文：** 在适应大学、突然进入全新环境时，间隔年的经历能减轻冲击，使学生更容易专注于学业和活动，而不是犯下适应不良造成的错误。

## 10. E2-2017-T3-P1-S10

If you're not convinced of the inherent value in taking a year off to explore interests, then consider its financial impact on future academic choices.

**译文：** 如果你仍不相信花一年时间探索兴趣本身具有价值，那就考虑一下它对未来学业选择所产生的经济影响。

## 11. E2-2017-T3-P1-S11

According to the National Center for Education Statistics, nearly 80 percent of college students end up changing their majors at least once.

**译文：** 美国国家教育统计中心的数据显示，近80%的大学生最终至少会更换一次专业。

## 12. E2-2017-T3-P1-S12

This isn't surprising, considering the basic mandatory high school curriculum leaves students with a poor understanding of the vast academic possibilities that await them in college.

**译文：** 这并不奇怪，因为高中基础必修课程使学生难以充分了解大学里等待着他们的众多学术可能性。

## 13. E2-2017-T3-P1-S13

Many students find themselves listing one major on their college applications, but switching to another after taking college classes.

**译文：** 许多学生在大学申请中填写一个专业，上过大学课程后却转向另一个专业。

## 14. E2-2017-T3-P1-S14

It's not necessarily a bad thing, but depending on the school, it can be costly to make up credits after switching too late in the game.

**译文：** 这未必是坏事，但视学校而定，如果转专业太晚，为补足学分可能要付出高昂代价。

## 15. E2-2017-T3-P1-S15

At Boston College, for example, you would have to complete an extra year were you to switch to the nursing school from another department.

**译文：** 例如，在波士顿学院，如果你从其他院系转入护理学院，就必须额外读一年。

**长难句拆解：**

- 主干：主句：you would have to complete an extra year（你将不得不多读一年）
- 逻辑：句子先给出转专业的额外成本，随后用省略if的虚拟条件倒装限定这一结果发生的条件。
- 结构：
  - 举例状语：At Boston College, for example
  - 主句：you would have to complete an extra year
  - 倒装条件句：were you to switch to the nursing school from another department
  -   └ 还原：if you were to switch to the nursing school from another department
- 考点：
  - were you to do是正式语体的虚拟条件句，等于if you were to do。
  - have to complete an extra year表示“必须额外修读一年”。
  - switch to A from B表示“从B转入A”。

## 16. E2-2017-T3-P1-S16

Taking a gap year to figure things out initially can help prevent stress and save money later on.

**译文：** 先用一个间隔年把事情想清楚，有助于日后减轻压力并节省金钱。
