# 2018 年考研英语二阅读 Text 3 · 逐句精读

## 1. E2-2018-T3-P1-S1

The power and ambition of the giants of the digital economy is astonishing - Amazon has just announced the purchase of the upmarket grocery chain Whole Foods for $13.5bn, but two years ago Facebook paid even more than that to acquire the WhatsApp messaging service, which doesn't have any physical product at all.

**译文：** 数字经济巨头的力量和野心令人震惊——亚马逊刚刚宣布以135亿美元收购高端食品连锁店全食超市，而两年前，脸书收购完全没有实体产品的即时通信服务WhatsApp时，支付的金额甚至更高。

**长难句拆解：**

- 主干：并列主干：The power and ambition…is astonishing - Amazon has announced…, but Facebook paid even more…（数字巨头的力量令人震惊：亚马逊宣布收购，而脸书此前支付得更多）
- 逻辑：首句先作总体评价，破折号后用两笔巨额收购作证；but通过金额比较突出没有实体产品的数字服务也能获得更高估值。
- 结构：
  - 总起句：The power and ambition of the giants of the digital economy is astonishing
  - 破折号后例证分句①：Amazon has just announced the purchase of the upmarket grocery chain Whole Foods for $13.5bn
  - 并列转折分句②(but)：two years ago Facebook paid even more than that
  -   └ 目的状语：to acquire the WhatsApp messaging service
  -       └ 非限制性定语从句(which)：which doesn't have any physical product at all
- 考点：
  - 主语power and ambition在此被视作整体概念，因此原文使用is。
  - upmarket表示“面向高端市场的”。
  - even more than that中的that指亚马逊收购全食超市所付的135亿美元。

## 12. E2-2018-T3-P1-S12

Competition law as presently interpreted deals with financial disadvantage to consumers and this is not obvious when the users of these services don't pay for them.

**译文：** 按照目前的解释，竞争法处理的是消费者在经济上遭受的不利；但这些服务的用户并不付费，因此这种不利并不明显。

**长难句拆解：**

- 主干：并列句：Competition law deals with financial disadvantage, and this is not obvious when users don't pay（竞争法处理经济损害，但免费服务中的损害并不明显）
- 逻辑：第一分句界定现行竞争法关注的损害类型；第二分句指出用户不付费时，传统的经济损害标准难以显现。
- 结构：
  - 分句①：Competition law as presently interpreted deals with financial disadvantage to consumers
  -   └ 过去分词省略结构：as presently interpreted〔修饰/限定 Competition law〕
  - 并列分句②(and)：this is not obvious
  -   └ 时间/条件状语从句(when)：when the users of these services don't pay for them
- 考点：
  - as presently interpreted可补全为as it is presently interpreted。
  - this指前面的financial disadvantage to consumers。
  - pay for them中的them指these services。

## 16. E2-2018-T3-P1-S16

Just as some ants farm the bugs called aphids for the honeydew they produce when they feed, so Google farms us for the data that our digital lives yield.

**译文：** 正如某些蚂蚁饲养名为蚜虫的小虫，以获取它们进食时分泌的蜜露一样，谷歌也“饲养”我们，以获取我们的数字生活所产生的数据。

**长难句拆解：**

- 主干：类比结构：Just as ants farm aphids…, so Google farms us…（正如蚂蚁饲养蚜虫，谷歌也“饲养”我们）
- 逻辑：just as…so…建立严格类比：蚂蚁保护并利用蚜虫获取蜜露，谷歌则维护服务并利用用户获取数据。
- 结构：
  - 类比分句(just as)：Just as some ants farm the bugs called aphids for the honeydew
  -   ├ 过去分词作后置定语：called aphids〔修饰 bugs〕
  -   └ 定语从句(that，修饰 honeydew)：they produce when they feed
  -       └ 时间状语从句(when)：when they feed
  - 对应主句(so)：so Google farms us for the data
  -   └ 定语从句(that，修饰 data)：that our digital lives yield
- 考点：
  - just as A, so B表示“正如A，B也同样”。
  - farm在此是带批判意味的比喻用法，意为“饲养并从中获利”。
  - yield作动词表示“产生、产出”。
