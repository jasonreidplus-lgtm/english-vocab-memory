# 2013 年考研英语二阅读 Text 1 · 逐句精读

## 1. E2-2013-T1-P1-S1

In an essay entitled "Making It in America," the author Adam Davidson relates a joke from cotton country about just how much a modern textile mill has been automated: The average mill has only two employees today, "a man and a dog.

**译文：** 在题为《在美国获得成功》的一篇文章中，作者亚当·戴维森讲了一个来自棉花产区的笑话，用来说明现代纺织厂的自动化程度有多高：如今，一家普通工厂只有两名“员工”——“一个人和一条狗”。

## 2. E2-2013-T1-P1-S2

The man is there to feed the dog, and the dog is there to keep the man away from the machines."

**译文：** 这个人负责喂狗，而这条狗负责让这个人远离机器。

## 3. E2-2013-T1-P1-S3

Davidson's article is one of a number of pieces that have recently appeared making the point that the reason we have such stubbornly high unemployment and declining middle-class incomes today is largely because of the big drop in demand because of the Great Recession, but it is also because of the advances in both globalization and the information technology revolution, which are more rapidly than ever replacing labor with machines or foreign workers.

**译文：** 戴维森的文章是近期出现的众多同类文章之一；这些文章指出，如今失业率居高不下、中产阶级收入下降，很大程度上是因为“大衰退”造成需求骤降，但也因为全球化和信息技术革命不断推进，它们正以前所未有的速度用机器或外国劳工取代本国劳动力。

**长难句拆解：**

- 主干：主干：Davidson's article is one of a number of pieces（戴维森的文章是众多文章之一）
- 逻辑：句子先界定文章类别，再用 point 的同位语从句提出“双重原因”：大衰退导致需求下降，同时全球化和信息技术进步加速替代劳动。
- 结构：
  - 定语从句(that，修饰 pieces)：that have recently appeared
  - 现在分词短语作伴随/结果说明：making the point
  - 同位语从句(that，解释 point)：the reason ... is largely because of A, but it is also because of B
  - 定语从句(which，指代 advances)：which are ... replacing labor with machines or foreign workers
- 考点：
  - the reason ... is because ... 在原文口语化论述中用于解释原因。
  - which 的先行内容是 advances，而不是 revolution 单独一词。
  - stubbornly high 表示“居高不下”；replace A with B 表示“用B取代A”。

## 4. E2-2013-T1-P1-S4

In the past, workers with average skills, doing an average job, could earn an average lifestyle.

**译文：** 过去，技能一般的工人做着普通工作，也能过上普通的生活。

## 5. E2-2013-T1-P1-S5

But, today, average is officially over.

**译文：** 但如今，“普通”这个时代已经正式结束。

## 6. E2-2013-T1-P1-S6

Being average just won't earn you what it used to.

**译文：** 仅仅达到普通水平，已无法再给你带来过去那样的收入。

## 7. E2-2013-T1-P1-S7

It can't when so many more employers have so much more access to so much more above average cheap foreign labor, cheap robotics, cheap software, cheap automation and cheap genius.

**译文：** 当越来越多的雇主能够获得大量高于平均水平且价格低廉的外国劳动力、机器人、软件、自动化技术乃至廉价天才时，普通水平当然不再管用。

**长难句拆解：**

- 主干：主干：It can't（普通水平不可能再带来过去的回报）
- 逻辑：本句用 when 给出“普通不再值钱”的现实条件：雇主能在全球范围内低成本取得更多、更优秀的人力与技术。
- 结构：
  - It 承接上一句 Being average，can't 后省略 earn you what it used to
  - 时间/原因状语从句(when)：when so many more employers have ... access to ...
  - 宾语中心：cheap foreign labor, robotics, software, automation and genius
  - above average 同时限定后列的高水平资源，多个 so much more 强化数量和可获得性
- 考点：
  - can't 是省略表达，理解时要从上一句补出完整谓语。
  - have access to 意为“能够获得/使用”。
  - cheap genius 并非贬低天才，而是强调高端才智的获取成本下降。

## 8. E2-2013-T1-P1-S8

Therefore, everyone needs to find their extra – their unique value contribution that makes them stand out in whatever is their field of employment.

**译文：** 因此，每个人都需要找到自己的“额外价值”——一种独特的价值贡献，使自己无论身处哪个就业领域都能脱颖而出。

## 9. E2-2013-T1-P1-S9

Yes, new technology has been eating jobs forever, and always will.

**译文：** 没错，新技术一直都在吞噬就业岗位，而且今后也会如此。

## 10. E2-2013-T1-P1-S10

But there's been an acceleration.

**译文：** 但这一过程已经加速。

## 11. E2-2013-T1-P1-S11

As Davidson notes, "In the 10 years ending in 2009, [U.S.] factories shed workers so fast that they erased almost all the gains of the previous 70 years; roughly one out of every three manufacturing jobs – about 6 million in total – disappeared."

**译文：** 正如戴维森所指出的：“截至2009年的十年间，美国工厂裁员速度之快，几乎抹去了此前70年取得的全部就业增长；大约每三个制造业岗位中就有一个消失，总计约600万个。”

## 12. E2-2013-T1-P1-S12

There will always be change – new jobs, new products, new services.

**译文：** 变化总会存在——会有新工作、新产品和新服务。

## 13. E2-2013-T1-P1-S13

But the one thing we know for sure is that with each advance in globalization and the I.T. revolution, the best jobs will require workers to have more and better education to make themselves above average.

**译文：** 但我们唯一能够确定的是：全球化和信息技术革命每推进一步，最好的工作岗位就越要求劳动者接受更多、更好的教育，从而使自己达到平均水平之上。

## 14. E2-2013-T1-P1-S14

In a world where average is officially over, there are many things we need to do to support employment, but nothing would be more important than passing some kind of G.I. Bill for the 21st century that ensures that every American has access to post-high school education.

**译文：** 在一个“普通时代已经结束”的世界里，我们需要采取许多措施来支持就业，但最重要的莫过于为21世纪通过某种类似《退伍军人权利法案》的教育法案，确保每个美国人都有机会接受高中后的教育。

**长难句拆解：**

- 主干：并列主干：there are many things ..., but nothing would be more important than passing ...
- 逻辑：but 把“许多可做之事”收束到最优先的一项；nothing ... more important than 是最高级含义，强调教育法案最重要。
- 结构：
  - 地点/背景状语：In a world where average is officially over
  - 定语从句(where，修饰 world)：where average is officially over
  - 定语从句〔省略 that，修饰 things〕：we need to do
  - 比较结构：nothing would be more important than passing some kind of G.I. Bill
  - 定语从句(that，修饰 Bill)：that ensures that every American has access to post-high school education
- 考点：
  - G.I. Bill 原指美国为退伍军人提供教育等福利的法案，此处借指全民继续教育支持。
  - where 引导定语从句，不是单纯地点状语从句。
  - ensure 后的 that 从句是宾语从句；have access to 表示“有机会获得”。
