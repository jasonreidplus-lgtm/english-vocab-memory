# 2011 年考研英语二阅读 Text 1 · 逐句精读

## 1. E2-2011-T1-P1-S1

Ruth Simmons joined Goldman Sachs's board as an outside director in January 2000; a year later she became president of Brown University.

**译文：** 2000年1月，露丝·西蒙斯以外部董事身份加入高盛董事会；一年后，她出任布朗大学校长。

## 2. E2-2011-T1-P1-S2

For the rest of the decade she apparently managed both roles without attracting much criticism.

**译文：** 在此后十年剩余的时间里，她显然兼顾了这两个职位，并未招致太多批评。

## 3. E2-2011-T1-P1-S3

But by the end of 2009 Ms. Simmons was under fire for having sat on Goldman's compensation committee; how could she have let those enormous bonus payouts pass unremarked?

**译文：** 但到2009年底，西蒙斯女士因曾任高盛薪酬委员会委员而饱受抨击：她怎么会任由那些巨额奖金发放而不置一词？

**长难句拆解：**

- 主干：分号连接两部分：Ms. Simmons was under fire; how could she have let payouts pass unremarked?（西蒙斯受抨击；她怎能对奖金发放不置一词？）
- 逻辑：前半句交代遭批评的事实和原因，分号后的反问把批评内容直接说出；could have done 表示对过去行为的质疑。
- 结构：
  - 陈述句：Ms. Simmons was under fire
  -   ├ 时间状语：by the end of 2009
  -   └ 原因状语(for)：for having sat on Goldman's compensation committee
  - 分号后反问句：how could she have let those enormous bonus payouts pass unremarked?
  -   └ 使役结构：let + payouts + pass + unremarked
- 考点：
  - be under fire 意为“受到猛烈批评”。
  - having sat on... 是完成式动名词，说明因曾担任委员会成员而受批评。
  - let...pass unremarked 指任由某事发生而不评论、不质疑。

## 4. E2-2011-T1-P1-S4

By February the next year Ms. Simmons had left the board.

**译文：** 到次年2月，西蒙斯女士已经离开董事会。

## 5. E2-2011-T1-P1-S5

The position was just taking up too much time, she said.

**译文：** 她说，这个职位实在占用了太多时间。

## 6. E2-2011-T1-P1-S6

Outside directors are supposed to serve as helpful, yet less biased, advisers on a firm's board.

**译文：** 外部董事理应在公司董事会中担任有助益但偏见较少的顾问。

## 7. E2-2011-T1-P1-S7

Having made their wealth and their reputations elsewhere, they presumably have enough independence to disagree with the chief executive's proposals.

**译文：** 由于他们在别处积累了财富和声誉，想必拥有足够的独立性，可以反对首席执行官的提议。

## 8. E2-2011-T1-P1-S8

If the sky, and the share price is falling, outside directors should be able to give advice based on having weathered their own crises.

**译文：** 如果形势以及股价都在下跌，外部董事应该能够凭借自己曾渡过危机的经验提出建议。

## 9. E2-2011-T1-P1-S9

The researchers from Ohio University used a database that covered more than 10,000 firms and more than 64,000 different directors between 1989 and 2004.

**译文：** 俄亥俄大学的研究人员使用了一个数据库，其中涵盖1989年至2004年间一万多家公司和六万四千多名不同董事。

## 10. E2-2011-T1-P1-S10

Then they simply checked which directors stayed from one proxy statement to the next.

**译文：** 随后，他们只需核对从一份股东委托书到下一份之间，哪些董事仍然留任。

## 11. E2-2011-T1-P1-S11

The most likely reason for departing a board was age, so the researchers concentrated on those "surprise" disappearances by directors under the age of 70.

**译文：** 离开董事会最可能的原因是年龄，因此研究人员把注意力集中在70岁以下董事那些“出人意料”的离任上。

## 12. E2-2011-T1-P1-S12

They found that after a surprise departure, the probability that the company will subsequently have to restate earnings increases by nearly 20%.

**译文：** 他们发现，一名董事意外离任之后，该公司随后不得不重报收益的概率会增加近20%。

**长难句拆解：**

- 主干：S+V+O：They found that the probability increases（他们发现这种概率会上升）
- 逻辑：外层 that 引导 found 的宾语；内层 that 紧跟 probability，说明“哪件事发生的概率”，真正谓语 increases 位于较后位置。
- 结构：
  - 主句：They found
  -   └ 宾语从句(that)：the probability ... increases by nearly 20%
  -       ├ 时间状语：after a surprise departure
  -       └ 同位语从句〔that，说明 probability 的内容〕：that the company will subsequently have to restate earnings
- 考点：
  - 不要把 company 误当作 increases 的主语，主语是 probability。
  - restate earnings 指更正并重新公布收益数据。
  - subsequently 表示董事离任之后的后续时间。

## 13. E2-2011-T1-P1-S13

The likelihood of being named in a federal class-action lawsuit also increases, and the stock is likely to perform worse.

**译文：** 公司在联邦集体诉讼中被列为被告的可能性也会增加，其股票表现也可能更差。

## 14. E2-2011-T1-P1-S14

The effect tended to be larger for larger firms.

**译文：** 这种影响往往在规模较大的公司中更为明显。

## 15. E2-2011-T1-P1-S15

Although a correlation between them leaving and subsequent bad performance at the firm is suggestive, it does not mean that such directors are always jumping off a sinking ship.

**译文：** 尽管董事离任与公司随后表现不佳之间的相关性颇具暗示性，但这并不意味着这些董事总是在弃沉船而逃。

## 16. E2-2011-T1-P1-S16

Often they "trade up," leaving riskier, smaller firms for larger and more stable firms.

**译文：** 他们常常是在“向上跳槽”，即离开风险更高的小公司，转投规模更大、更稳定的公司。

## 17. E2-2011-T1-P1-S17

But the researchers believe that outside directors have an easier time of avoiding a blow to their reputations if they leave a firm before bad news breaks, even if a review of history shows they were on the board at the time any wrongdoing occurred.

**译文：** 但研究人员认为，如果外部董事在坏消息传出前离开公司，就更容易避免声誉受损，即使回顾历史会发现，在不当行为发生时他们其实仍在董事会任职。

**长难句拆解：**

- 主干：S+V+O：The researchers believe that outside directors have an easier time（研究人员认为外部董事会更容易……）
- 逻辑：核心判断是“提前离开可更容易保住声誉”；if 给出实现条件，even if 再补充让步：历史记录仍可能证明其当时在任。
- 结构：
  - 主句：the researchers believe
  -   └ 宾语从句(that)：outside directors have an easier time of avoiding a blow to their reputations
  -       ├ 条件状语从句(if)：if they leave a firm before bad news breaks
  -       └ 让步状语从句(even if)：even if a review of history shows
  -           └ 宾语从句〔省略 that〕：they were on the board at the time
  -               └ 定语从句〔省略 when，修饰 time〕：any wrongdoing occurred
- 考点：
  - have an easier time of doing：做某事更容易。
  - bad news breaks 指坏消息突然传出。
  - a blow to one's reputation 指声誉受到打击。

## 18. E2-2011-T1-P1-S18

Firms who want to keep their outside directors through tough times may have to create incentives.

**译文：** 希望外部董事在艰难时期仍然留任的公司，可能不得不设置激励措施。

## 19. E2-2011-T1-P1-S19

Otherwise outside directors will follow the example of Ms. Simmons, once again very popular on campus.

**译文：** 否则，外部董事就会效仿西蒙斯女士，离开董事会，再次成为校园里广受欢迎的人物。
