# 2026 年考研英语二阅读 Text 4 · 逐句精读

## 1. E2-2026-T4-P1-S1

In 2023, Chicago lost one of its most beloved street festivals.

**译文：** 2023年，芝加哥失去了最受喜爱的街头节庆活动之一。

## 2. E2-2026-T4-P1-S2

The Silver Room Block Party, staged by Hyde Park community leader Eric Williams, announced it would not return in 2024.

**译文：** 由海德公园社区领袖埃里克·威廉斯主办的"银屋街区派对"宣布，活动不会在2024年回归。

## 3. E2-2026-T4-P1-S3

What began as a small neighborhood gathering blossomed into a massive cultural event, welcoming tens of thousands of people each year over nearly two decades before abruptly shutting down.

**译文：** 这项活动最初只是一次小型社区聚会，随后发展成大型文化盛会；近二十年来每年吸引数万人参加，却最终突然停办。

## 4. E2-2026-T4-P1-S4

Williams pointed to rising production costs and declining attendee donations as primary reasons the Silver Room Block Party could not continue, highlighting a reality that all street festival organizers face right now.

**译文：** 威廉斯指出，制作成本上升和参加者捐款减少，是银屋街区派对无法继续举办的主要原因；这也揭示了当下所有街头节庆组织者都面对的现实。

**长难句拆解：**

- 主干：Williams pointed to costs and donations as reasons（威廉斯指出成本和捐款是主要原因）
- 逻辑：主句列出停办原因，省略why的从句说明这些原因导致何事；highlighting把个案上升为所有街头节庆共同面对的现实。
- 结构：
  - 主句：Williams pointed to rising production costs and declining attendee donations as primary reasons
  -   └ 定语从句〔省略why，修饰 reasons〕：the Silver Room Block Party could not continue
  - 现在分词短语〔结果/补充〕：highlighting a reality
  -   └ 定语从句(that)：that all street festival organizers face right now
- 考点：
  - point to A as B 意为“指出A是B”。
  - attendee donations 指参加者在活动现场作出的捐款。
  - highlighting 的逻辑主语是前面整件事。

## 5. E2-2026-T4-P1-S5

The cost of producing a street festival in Chicago has skyrocketed.

**译文：** 在芝加哥，举办街头节庆的成本已经飙升。

## 6. E2-2026-T4-P1-S6

Security, entertainment, portable restrooms, insurance and even basics such as fencing and staffing have all become significantly more expensive.

**译文：** 安保、娱乐演出、移动厕所、保险，甚至围栏和工作人员等基本项目，都变得贵得多。

## 7. E2-2026-T4-P1-S7

At the same time, donations at festival gates have dropped dramatically.

**译文：** 与此同时，节庆入口处收到的捐款大幅下降。

## 8. E2-2026-T4-P1-S8

Chicago's summer festivals are about more than just entertainment; they are economic engines that directly benefit the neighborhoods they're in and the city of Chicago as whole.

**译文：** 芝加哥的夏季节庆不只是娱乐活动，它们还是经济引擎，直接造福活动所在社区和整个芝加哥市。

## 9. E2-2026-T4-P1-S9

Street festivals drive foot traffic to local businesses and foster the kind of cultural vibrancy that makes our city special.

**译文：** 街头节庆为当地商家带来客流，并培育使这座城市独具魅力的文化活力。

## 10. E2-2026-T4-P1-S10

We often hear people ask why we solicit donations at our entry points, especially when the city's largest festivals like the Chicago Jazz Festival do not request donations.

**译文：** 我们经常听到有人问，为什么要在入口处募捐，尤其是芝加哥爵士音乐节等全市最大型节庆并不要求捐款。

## 11. E2-2026-T4-P1-S11

The fact is, unlike those large, city-produced music festivals, your neighborhood street festivals receive no city funding and rely on a combination of sponsorships, vendor fees and gate donations to cover their costs.

**译文：** 事实是，与那些由市政府举办的大型音乐节不同，社区街头节庆得不到市政府拨款，只能依靠赞助费、摊位费和入场捐款共同覆盖成本。

**长难句拆解：**

- 主干：Festivals receive no city funding and rely on three sources（社区节庆没有市政府拨款，只能依赖三类资金）
- 逻辑：unlike先建立城市主办大型节庆与社区节庆的对比，两个并列谓语说明后者无拨款、靠自筹，末尾to cover交代资金用途。
- 结构：
  - 主句：The fact is
  -   └ 表语内容：your neighborhood street festivals receive no city funding and rely on ...
  -       ├ 比较介词短语：unlike those large, city-produced music festivals
  -       ├ 并列谓语一：receive no city funding
  -       └ 并列谓语二：rely on a combination of sponsorships, vendor fees and gate donations
  -           └ 目的状语：to cover their costs
- 考点：
  - city-produced 指由市政府组织举办。
  - vendor fees 指摊商或参展商缴纳的费用。
  - gate donations 指活动入口处募集的捐款。

## 12. E2-2026-T4-P1-S12

Wicker Park Fest has long been one of Chicago's most anticipated summer festivals, drawing upward of 70,000 attendees for a full weekend of live indie music, local art, small business vendors and, most importantly, community connection.

**译文：** 柳条公园节长期以来一直是芝加哥最令人期待的夏季节庆之一，整个周末的独立音乐现场演出、本地艺术、小企业摊位，以及最重要的社区联结，吸引了七万多人参加。

## 13. E2-2026-T4-P1-S13

In 2024, Wicker Park Fest saw record-breaking attendance.

**译文：** 2024年，柳条公园节的到场人数创下纪录。

## 14. E2-2026-T4-P1-S14

Despite the turnout, gate donations reached their lowest point in our history.

**译文：** 尽管参加者众多，入口捐款却降至该活动有史以来的最低点。

## 15. E2-2026-T4-P1-S15

This year, we've been forced to scale back the footprint of the fest.

**译文：** 今年，我们被迫缩小节庆活动的规模。

## 16. E2-2026-T4-P1-S16

We are eliminating a stage, booking fewer performers and making additional cuts to reduce our costs, all while striving to keep the festival as vibrant as ever, as supportive of local artists and businesses, and as true to Wicker Park's unique spirit and reputation as festgoers have come to expect.

**译文：** 我们将取消一个舞台、减少演出者并作出更多削减以降低成本；与此同时，我们仍力求让节庆一如既往地充满活力、支持本地艺术家和企业，并忠实延续柳条公园独特的精神与声誉，达到节庆参与者已经形成的期待。

**长难句拆解：**

- 主干：We are eliminating, booking fewer and making cuts, while striving to keep the festival vibrant（我们在取消、缩减和削支，同时努力保持节庆活力）
- 逻辑：主句列出三项削减措施，while强调削减与维持品质同时进行；三个as...结构并列说明希望保留的活动特征。
- 结构：
  - 主句三个并列谓语：We are eliminating a stage, booking fewer performers and making additional cuts
  -   └ 目的状语：to reduce our costs
  - 让步/伴随结构：all while striving to keep the festival
  -   ├ 宾补一：as vibrant as ever
  -   ├ 宾补二：as supportive of local artists and businesses
  -   └ 宾补三：as true to Wicker Park's unique spirit and reputation
  -       └ 方式/比较从句(as)：as festgoers have come to expect
- 考点：
  - scale back 指缩小规模。
  - all while 表示“与此同时仍然……”。
  - true to 意为“忠实于、不偏离”。

## 17. E2-2026-T4-P1-S17

This summer, as you enjoy your favorite neighborhood street festival, I hope you'll remember that they exist because of community support.

**译文：** 今年夏天，当你享受自己喜爱的社区街头节庆时，我希望你记得，它们之所以存在，是因为有社区支持。

## 18. E2-2026-T4-P1-S18

A thriving summer festival season doesn't happen by accident; it happens when we all chip in.

**译文：** 繁荣的夏季节庆季不会凭空出现；只有大家共同出一份力，它才能实现。
