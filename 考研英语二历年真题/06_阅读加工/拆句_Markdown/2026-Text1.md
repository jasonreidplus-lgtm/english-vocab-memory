# 2026 年考研英语二阅读 Text 1 · 逐句精读

## 1. E2-2026-T1-P1-S1

Ask people about public libraries and a certain image springs to mind: dusty, old-fashioned, the sort of place you enjoyed as a child but, rather like a British seaside town, would you go there now?

**译文：** 向人们提起公共图书馆，脑海中往往会浮现某种形象：尘土飞扬、陈旧过时，是小时候喜欢去的那种地方；但它又有点像英国海滨小镇——你现在还会去吗？

**长难句拆解：**

- 主干：Ask people and a certain image springs to mind（向人们提起图书馆，某种形象便浮现出来）
- 逻辑：句子先用祈使结构模拟提问场景，再列出刻板印象；最后以插入类比和反问，把“童年喜欢”与“现在还去不去”形成对照。
- 结构：
  - 祈使条件/引入：Ask people about public libraries
  - 主句：and a certain image springs to mind
  -   └ 冒号后同位说明(image)：dusty, old-fashioned, the sort of place
  -       ├ 定语从句〔省略that，修饰 place〕：you enjoyed as a child
  -       └ 转折插入与疑问：but, rather like a British seaside town, would you go there now?
- 考点：
  - spring to mind 意为“立刻浮现在脑海中”。
  - the sort of place 后省略关系代词that。
  - rather like 表示“颇有点像”。

## 3. E2-2026-T1-P1-S3

The reality is startlingly different, as I discovered when the culture department commissioned me to conduct an independent review of English public libraries, published yesterday.

**译文：** 然而事实截然不同，甚至令人吃惊；文化部门委托我对英格兰公共图书馆开展独立评审时，我发现了这一点，评审报告已于昨日发布。

**长难句拆解：**

- 主干：The reality is startlingly different（现实截然不同）
- 逻辑：主干先否定刻板印象，as说明作者如何得知，when再交代受委托评审的具体情境；published yesterday修饰评审报告。
- 结构：
  - 主句：The reality is startlingly different
  -   └ 方式状语从句(as)：as I discovered
  -       └ 时间状语从句(when)：when the culture department commissioned me
  -           └ 宾补不定式：to conduct an independent review of English public libraries
  -               └ 过去分词短语〔修饰 review〕：published yesterday
- 考点：
  - startlingly different 意为“不同得令人吃惊”。
  - commission sb to do 指“委托某人做某事”。
  - published yesterday 可理解为评审报告于昨日发布。

## 19. E2-2026-T1-P1-S19

The review recommends a national branding campaign to give libraries a stronger physical presence, the reintroduction of a scheme to enable members to use their card in any library in the country, and automatic memberships for children.

**译文：** 评审报告建议开展全国统一品牌宣传，让图书馆在现实空间中更加醒目；恢复一项允许会员凭同一张卡使用全国任何图书馆的计划；并为儿童自动办理会员资格。

**长难句拆解：**

- 主干：The review recommends a campaign, the reintroduction of a scheme, and memberships（评审建议开展宣传、恢复计划并提供会员资格）
- 逻辑：recommend后用三个名词短语列出政策包；两个to do分别说明宣传活动和会员互通计划的具体目的或内容。
- 结构：
  - 主句：The review recommends 三个并列宾语
  -   ├ 宾语一：a national branding campaign
  -   │   └ 目的状语：to give libraries a stronger physical presence
  -   ├ 宾语二：the reintroduction of a scheme
  -   │   └ 不定式〔说明 scheme〕：to enable members to use their card in any library in the country
  -   └ 宾语三：automatic memberships for children
- 考点：
  - branding campaign 指统一品牌识别与宣传活动。
  - physical presence 在此指现实环境中的可见度和存在感。
  - automatic memberships 指无需另行申请即可获得会员资格。
