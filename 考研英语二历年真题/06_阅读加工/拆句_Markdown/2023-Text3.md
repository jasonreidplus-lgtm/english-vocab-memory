# 2023 年考研英语二阅读 Text 3 · 逐句精读

## 1. E2-2023-T3-P1-S1

The Internet may be changing merely what we remember, not our capacity to do so, suggests Columbia University psychology professor Betsy Sparrow.

**译文：** 哥伦比亚大学心理学教授贝齐·斯帕罗认为，互联网改变的也许只是我们记住什么，而不是我们的记忆能力。

## 2. E2-2023-T3-P1-S2

In 2011, Sparrow led a study in which participants were asked to record 40 factoids in a computer ("an ostrich's eye is bigger than its brain," for example).

**译文：** 2011年，斯帕罗主持了一项研究，要求参与者把40条零碎事实记录到电脑里（例如“鸵鸟的眼睛比它的大脑还大”）。

**长难句拆解：**

- 主干：S+V+O：Sparrow led a study（斯帕罗主持了一项研究）
- 逻辑：主句只交代研究；in which 从句展开研究任务，被动结构 were asked 后接不定式；括号内容给出 factoid 的具体例子。
- 结构：
  - 时间状语：In 2011
  - 主句：Sparrow led a study
  -   └ 定语从句(in which)：in which participants were asked
  -       └ 不定式宾补：to record 40 factoids in a computer
  -       └ 括号举例："an ostrich's eye is bigger than its brain," for example
- 考点：
  - factoid 在此指一条简短、零碎的事实。
  - in which 可替换为 where，但正式程度更高。
  - be asked to do 表示被要求做某事。

## 3. E2-2023-T3-P1-S3

Half of the participants were told the information would be erased, while the other half were told it would be saved.

**译文：** 一半参与者被告知这些信息会被删除，另一半则被告知信息会被保存。

## 4. E2-2023-T3-P1-S4

Guess what?

**译文：** 猜猜结果如何？

## 5. E2-2023-T3-P1-S5

The latter group made no effort to recall the information when quizzed on it later, because they knew they could find it on their computers.

**译文：** 后一组在随后接受测试时没有努力回想这些信息，因为他们知道可以在电脑上找到它们。

## 6. E2-2023-T3-P1-S6

In the same study, a group was asked to remember both the information and the folders it was stored in.

**译文：** 在同一项研究中，一组参与者被要求同时记住信息本身和存放信息的文件夹。

## 7. E2-2023-T3-P1-S7

They didn't remember the information, but they remembered how to find the folders.

**译文：** 他们没有记住信息，却记住了怎样找到那些文件夹。

## 8. E2-2023-T3-P1-S8

In other words, human memory is not deteriorating but "adapting to new communications technology," Sparrow says.

**译文：** 斯帕罗说，换言之，人类记忆并非正在退化，而是在“适应新的通信技术”。

## 9. E2-2023-T3-P1-S9

In a very practical way, the Internet is becoming an external hard drive for our memories, a process known as "cognitive offloading."

**译文：** 从非常实际的意义上说，互联网正成为我们记忆的外置硬盘，这一过程被称为“认知卸载”。

## 10. E2-2023-T3-P1-S10

Traditionally, this role was fulfilled by data banks, libraries, and other humans.

**译文：** 传统上，数据库、图书馆和其他人承担着这一角色。

## 11. E2-2023-T3-P1-S11

Your father may never remember birthdays because your mother does, for instance.

**译文：** 例如，你的父亲可能从不记生日，因为你的母亲会记。

## 12. E2-2023-T3-P1-S12

Some worry that this is having a destructive effect on society, but Sparrow sees an upside.

**译文：** 有些人担心这会对社会产生破坏性影响，但斯帕罗看到了积极的一面。

## 13. E2-2023-T3-P1-S13

Perhaps, she suggests, the trend will change our approach to learning from a focus on individual facts and memorization to an emphasis on more conceptual thinking—something that is not available on the Internet.

**译文：** 她认为，这一趋势或许会改变我们的学习方式：从关注个别事实和死记硬背，转向更重视概念性思考——而这种思考无法从互联网上直接获得。

**长难句拆解：**

- 主干：S+V+O：The trend will change our approach to learning（这一趋势会改变我们的学习方式）
- 逻辑：from...to...明确学习重心的迁移；破折号后的 something 回指 conceptual thinking，并指出这种能力不能直接从互联网取得。
- 结构：
  - 主句：Perhaps the trend will change our approach to learning
  - 插入语：she suggests
  - 变化范围：from a focus on individual facts and memorization
  -   └ 对应终点(to)：to an emphasis on more conceptual thinking
  - 破折号同位补充：something
  -   └ 定语从句(that)：that is not available on the Internet
- 考点：
  - approach to 中 to 是介词，后接 learning。
  - a focus on...to an emphasis on... 是平行结构。
  - available on the Internet 在此不是“联网可用”，而是“网上现成可得”。

## 14. E2-2023-T3-P1-S14

"I personally have never seen all that much intellectual value in memorizing things," Sparrow says, adding that we haven't lost our ability to do it.

**译文：** “就我个人而言，我从未觉得记住各种东西有多大智力价值，”斯帕罗说，并补充道，我们并没有失去这种能力。

## 15. E2-2023-T3-P1-S15

Still other experts say it's too soon to understand how the Internet affects our brains.

**译文：** 另一些专家则说，现在要弄清互联网如何影响大脑还为时过早。

## 16. E2-2023-T3-P1-S16

There is no experimental evidence showing that it interferes with our ability to focus, for instance, wrote psychologists Christopher Chabris and Daniel J. Simons.

**译文：** 心理学家克里斯托弗·查布里斯和丹尼尔·J. 西蒙斯写道，例如，并没有实验证据表明互联网会干扰我们的专注能力。

## 17. E2-2023-T3-P1-S17

And surfing the web exercised the brain more than reading did among computer-savvy older adults in a 2008 study involving 24 participants at the Semel Institute for Neuroscience and Human Behavior at the University of California, Los Angeles.

**译文：** 此外，2008年加州大学洛杉矶分校塞梅尔神经科学与人类行为研究所对24名参与者开展的一项研究发现，在熟悉电脑的老年人中，上网比阅读更能调动大脑。

**长难句拆解：**

- 主干：倒装比较：Surfing the web exercised the brain more than reading did（上网比阅读更能调动大脑）
- 逻辑：句首 And 承接前文证据；核心是 more than 比较，上网与阅读是两个并列比较项；后面连续介词短语只补充人群、研究和机构。
- 结构：
  - 主句：surfing the web exercised the brain more than reading did
  - 适用人群：among computer-savvy older adults
  - 研究背景：in a 2008 study
  -   └ 现在分词短语：involving 24 participants
  -       └ 地点机构：at the Semel Institute... at the University of California, Los Angeles
- 考点：
  - reading did 中 did 代替 exercised the brain，避免重复。
  - computer-savvy 表示熟悉、善于使用电脑。
  - involving 24 participants 修饰 study。

## 18. E2-2023-T3-P1-S18

"There may be costs associated with our increased reliance on the Internet, but I'd have to imagine that overall the benefits are going to outweigh those costs," observes psychology professor Benjamin Storm.

**译文：** “更加依赖互联网或许会带来代价，但我认为，总体而言，其益处将超过这些代价，”心理学教授本杰明·斯托姆评论道。

## 19. E2-2023-T3-P1-S19

"It seems pretty clear that memory is changing, but is it changing for the better?

**译文：** “记忆正在改变似乎相当明确，但它是在变得更好吗？

At this point, we don't know."

**译文：** 就目前而言，我们还不知道。”
