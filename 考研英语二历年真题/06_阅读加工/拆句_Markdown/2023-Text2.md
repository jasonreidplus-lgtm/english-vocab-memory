# 2023 年考研英语二阅读 Text 2 · 逐句精读

## 1. E2-2023-T2-P1-S1

It's easy to dismiss as absurd the federal government's ideas for plugging the chronic funding gap of our national parks.

**译文：** 联邦政府为填补国家公园长期资金缺口而提出的那些设想，很容易被斥为荒谬。

## 2. E2-2023-T2-P1-S2

Can anyone really think it's a good idea to allow Amazon deliveries to your tent in Yosemite or food trucks to line up under the redwood trees at Sequoia National Park?

**译文：** 真有人会认为，让亚马逊把包裹送到你在优胜美地的帐篷旁，或让餐车在红杉国家公园的巨杉树下排成一列，是个好主意吗？

## 3. E2-2023-T2-P1-S3

But the government is right about one thing: U.S. national parks are in crisis.

**译文：** 但政府有一点说对了：美国国家公园正陷入危机。

## 4. E2-2023-T2-P1-S4

Collectively, they have a maintenance backlog of more than $12 billion.

**译文：** 这些公园合计积压了超过120亿美元的维修项目。

## 5. E2-2023-T2-P1-S5

Roads, trails, restrooms, visitor centers and other infrastructure are crumbling.

**译文：** 道路、小径、洗手间、游客中心及其他基础设施正在破败。

## 6. E2-2023-T2-P1-S6

But privatizing and commercializing the campgrounds would not be a cure-all.

**译文：** 然而，将露营地私有化、商业化并不是包治百病的办法。

## 7. E2-2023-T2-P1-S7

Campgrounds are a tiny portion of the overall infrastructure backlog, and businesses in the parks hand over, on average, only about 5% of their revenues to the National Park Service.

**译文：** 露营地只占整体基础设施欠账的一小部分，而且公园内的企业平均只把约5%的收入上交给国家公园管理局。

## 8. E2-2023-T2-P1-S8

Moreover, increased privatization would certainly undercut one of the major reasons why 300 million visitors come to the parks each year: to enjoy nature and get a break from the commercial drumbeat that overwhelms daily life.

**译文：** 此外，进一步私有化无疑会削弱每年3亿游客来到公园的一项主要理由：亲近自然，并从充斥日常生活的商业喧嚣中暂时解脱。

**长难句拆解：**

- 主干：S+V+O：Increased privatization would undercut one reason（进一步私有化会削弱一项主要理由）
- 逻辑：why 从句说明被削弱的是哪项到访理由；冒号把理由具体化为两个并列目的：亲近自然和摆脱商业喧嚣。
- 结构：
  - 主句：increased privatization would certainly undercut one of the major reasons
  -   └ 定语从句(why)：why 300 million visitors come to the parks each year
  - 冒号后同位解释：to enjoy nature and get a break
  -   └ 介词短语：from the commercial drumbeat
  -       └ 定语从句(that)：that overwhelms daily life
- 考点：
  - undercut 在此意为削弱，而非字面“从下方切割”。
  - get a break from 表示暂时摆脱。
  - commercial drumbeat 比喻持续不断的商业宣传和刺激。

## 9. E2-2023-T2-P1-S9

The real problem is that the parks have been chronically starved of funding.

**译文：** 真正的问题是，这些公园长期得不到足够资金。

## 10. E2-2023-T2-P1-S10

We conducted a comprehensive survey examining how U.S. residents view their national parks, and we found that Americans place a very high value on them—whether or not they actually visit them.

**译文：** 我们开展了一项综合调查，考察美国居民如何看待本国的国家公园；结果发现，无论实际上是否去过，人们都赋予这些公园极高价值。

**长难句拆解：**

- 主干：S+V+O；S+V+O：We conducted a survey, and we found that...（我们开展调查，并发现……）
- 逻辑：and 连接研究方法和研究发现；examining...说明调查内容；whether or not 排除实际到访这一条件，突出人们对公园的非使用价值。
- 结构：
  - 分句1：We conducted a comprehensive survey
  -   └ 现在分词短语：examining how U.S. residents view their national parks
  -       └ 宾语从句(how)：how U.S. residents view their national parks
  - 并列分句2(and)：we found
  -   └ 宾语从句(that)：that Americans place a very high value on them
  -       └ 让步插入：whether or not they actually visit them
- 考点：
  - place a high value on 表示高度重视、赋予高价值。
  - them 两次均指 national parks。
  - whether or not 意为“无论是否”。

## 11. E2-2023-T2-P1-S11

The peer-reviewed economic survey of 700 U.S. taxpayers, conducted by mail and internet, also found that people would be willing to pay a significant amount of money to make sure the parks and their programs are kept intact.

**译文：** 这项经过同行评议的经济调查通过邮寄和网络方式访问了700名美国纳税人，还发现人们愿意支付一笔可观费用，以确保这些公园及其项目保持完整。

## 12. E2-2023-T2-P1-S12

Some 81% of respondents said they would be willing to pay additional taxes for the next 10 years to avoid any cuts to the national parks.

**译文：** 约81%的受访者表示，他们愿意在未来十年缴纳额外税款，以避免国家公园遭受任何经费削减。

## 13. E2-2023-T2-P1-S13

The national parks provide great value to U.S. residents both as places to escape and as symbols of nature.

**译文：** 国家公园对美国居民价值巨大：它们既是逃离日常生活的去处，也是自然的象征。

## 14. E2-2023-T2-P1-S14

On top of this, they produce value from their extensive educational programs, their positive impact on the climate through carbon sequestration, their contribution to our cultural and artistic life, and of course through tourism.

**译文：** 除此之外，国家公园还凭借广泛的教育项目、通过碳封存对气候产生的积极影响、对文化艺术生活的贡献，当然还有旅游业，创造价值。

## 15. E2-2023-T2-P1-S15

The parks also help keep America's past alive, working with thousands of local jurisdictions around the country to protect historical sites—including Ellis Island and Gettysburg—and to bring the stories of these places to life.

**译文：** 国家公园还与全国数千个地方辖区合作，保护包括埃利斯岛和葛底斯堡在内的历史遗址，并让这些地方的故事鲜活起来，从而帮助保存美国的历史。

## 16. E2-2023-T2-P1-S16

The parks do all this on a shoestring.

**译文：** 公园仅靠微薄经费就完成了这一切。

## 17. E2-2023-T2-P1-S17

Congress allocates only $3 billion a year to the national park system—an amount that has been flat since 2001 (in inflation-adjusted dollars) with the exception of a onetime boost in 2009 as part of the Obama stimulus package.

**译文：** 国会每年只给国家公园系统拨款30亿美元；按通胀调整后，这一金额自2001年以来一直没有增长，唯一例外是2009年作为奥巴马经济刺激计划一部分的一次性增加。

**长难句拆解：**

- 主干：S+V+O：Congress allocates only $3 billion（国会每年只拨款30亿美元）
- 逻辑：主句给出年度拨款；破折号后评价其长期不变，并用 with the exception of 标出唯一例外，构成“长期停滞—短暂增加”的对照。
- 结构：
  - 主句：Congress allocates only $3 billion a year to the national park system
  - 破折号同位说明：an amount
  -   └ 定语从句(that)：that has been flat since 2001
  -       ├ 插入限定：in inflation-adjusted dollars
  -       └ 例外状语：with the exception of a onetime boost in 2009
  -           └ 介词短语：as part of the Obama stimulus package
- 考点：
  - flat 在数值语境中表示没有增长。
  - inflation-adjusted dollars 指按通货膨胀调整后的金额。
  - onetime boost 指一次性的增加。

Meanwhile, the number of annual visitors has increased by more than 50% since 1980, and now stands at 330 million visitors per year.

**译文：** 与此同时，年度游客数量自1980年以来增长了50%以上，如今达到每年3.3亿人次。
