# 2025 年考研英语二阅读 Text 4 · 逐句精读

## 1. E2-2025-T4-P1-S1

Navigating beyond the organised pavements and parks of our urban spaces, desire paths are the unofficial footprints of a community, revealing the unspoken preferences, shared shortcuts and collective choices of humans.

**译文：** 在城市空间规划整齐的人行道和公园之外，意愿小径是一个群体留下的非正式足迹，显露出人们没有说出口的偏好、共同选择的捷径和集体抉择。

## 2. E2-2025-T4-P1-S2

Often appearing as trodden dirt tracks through otherwise neat green spaces, these routes of collective disobedience cut corners, bisect lawns and cross hills, representing the natural capability of people (and animals) to go from point A to point B most effectively.

**译文：** 这些集体"不服从"的路线常以踩出来的土路出现在原本整洁的绿地中；它们抄近路、穿过草坪和山坡，体现了人和动物以最高效率从A点到B点的天然能力。

**长难句拆解：**

- 主干：These routes cut corners, bisect lawns and cross hills（这些路线抄近路、穿草坪、越山坡）
- 逻辑：句首描述小径如何出现，主干列出三种穿行方式，末尾representing将这些行为概括为人和动物寻找最高效路线的能力。
- 结构：
  - 现在分词短语〔描述外观〕：Often appearing as trodden dirt tracks through otherwise neat green spaces
  - 主句：these routes of collective disobedience cut corners, bisect lawns and cross hills
  -   └ 现在分词短语〔结果/说明〕：representing the natural capability of people (and animals)
  -       └ 不定式〔修饰 capability〕：to go from point A to point B most effectively
- 考点：
  - trodden dirt tracks 指被反复踩出的土路。
  - cut corners 既可字面指切过拐角，也有“走捷径”之意。
  - otherwise neat 表示若没有这些小径，绿地本来很整齐。

## 3. E2-2025-T4-P1-S3

Urban planners interpret desire paths as more than just convenient shortcuts; they offer valuable insights into the dynamics between planning and behaviour.

**译文：** 城市规划师认为，意愿小径不只是方便的捷径，它们还能为理解规划与行为之间的互动提供宝贵线索。

## 4. E2-2025-T4-P1-S4

Ohio State University allowed its students to navigate the Oval, a lawn in the centre of campus, freely, then proceeded to pave the desire paths, creating a web of effective routes students had established.

**译文：** 俄亥俄州立大学先让学生自由穿行校园中央一片名为"椭圆草坪"的草地，随后把学生走出来的意愿小径铺成路，形成一张高效路线网。

## 5. E2-2025-T4-P1-S5

Yet, reluctance persists among other planners to integrate desire paths into formal plans, citing concerns about safety, environmental impact, or primarily, aesthetics.

**译文：** 然而，其他规划者仍不愿将意愿小径纳入正式规划，理由是担心安全、环境影响，尤其是美观问题。

## 6. E2-2025-T4-P1-S6

A Reddit webpage devoted to the phenomenon, boasting nearly 50,000 members, showcases images of local desire paths adorned with signs instructing pedestrians to adhere to designated walkways, underscoring the rebellious nature inherent in these human-made tracks.

**译文：** 一个专门讨论这种现象、拥有近五万名成员的Reddit页面，展示了各地意愿小径的图片；有些小径旁竖着要求行人走指定道路的标牌，反而凸显了这些人为踩出的小路内在的反叛性质。

**长难句拆解：**

- 主干：A Reddit webpage showcases images（一个Reddit页面展示图片）
- 逻辑：主干很短，其余多个分词短语逐层修饰页面、路径和标牌，最后underscoring点出“越禁止越体现反叛”的含义。
- 结构：
  - 主句：A Reddit webpage ... showcases images of local desire paths
  -   ├ 过去分词短语〔修饰 webpage〕：devoted to the phenomenon
  -   ├ 现在分词短语〔补充 webpage〕：boasting nearly 50,000 members
  -   ├ 过去分词短语〔修饰 paths〕：adorned with signs
  -   │   └ 现在分词短语〔修饰 signs〕：instructing pedestrians to adhere to designated walkways
  -   └ 结果状语：underscoring the rebellious nature inherent in these human-made tracks
- 考点：
  - boast 在此意为“拥有”，不一定含贬义。
  - adhere to designated walkways 指遵守要求、走指定人行道。
  - inherent in 意为“内在于、固有于”。

## 7. E2-2025-T4-P1-S7

This clash highlights an ongoing struggle between the organic, user-driven evolution of public spaces and the desire for a visually curated and controlled urban environment.

**译文：** 这种冲突凸显出一场持续的较量：一边是公共空间由使用者推动的自然演化，另一边则是对视觉上经过设计、受到控制的城市环境的追求。

## 8. E2-2025-T4-P1-S8

The Wickquasgeck Trail is an example of a historical desire path, created by Native Americans to cross the forests of Manhattan and move between settlements quickly.

**译文：** 威克夸斯盖克小径是一条历史悠久的意愿小径；美洲原住民开辟它，是为了穿越曼哈顿的森林，在定居点之间快速往来。

## 9. E2-2025-T4-P1-S9

This trail, when Dutch colonists arrived, was widened and made into one of the main trade roads across the island, known at the time as de Heere Straat, or Gentlemen's Street.

**译文：** 荷兰殖民者到来后，这条小径被拓宽，变成横贯曼哈顿岛的主要贸易道路之一，当时称作de Heere Straat，也就是"绅士街"。

## 10. E2-2025-T4-P1-S10

Following the British assumption of control in New York, the street was renamed Broadway.

**译文：** 英国控制纽约后，这条街被改名为百老汇。

## 11. E2-2025-T4-P1-S11

Notably, Broadway stands out as one of the few areas in NYC that defies the grid-based system applied to the rest of the city, cutting a diagonal across parts of the city.

**译文：** 值得注意的是，百老汇是纽约市少数不遵循其他地区网格化布局的地带之一，以对角线斜穿城市部分区域。

## 12. E2-2025-T4-P1-S12

In online spaces, desire paths have sparked a fascination that can approach obsession, with the Reddit page serving as a hub.

**译文：** 在网络空间里，意愿小径激起了一种近乎痴迷的兴趣，而那个Reddit页面正是爱好者的聚集地。

## 13. E2-2025-T4-P1-S13

Contributors offer a wide array of stories, from little-known new shortcuts to long-established alternate routes.

**译文：** 投稿者分享了各种各样的故事，从鲜为人知的新捷径到存在已久的替代路线，不一而足。

## 14. E2-2025-T4-P1-S14

Animal desire paths, such as ducks forging trails through frozen ponds or dogs carving direct routes in gardens, highlight the adaptability of these trails in both human and animal experiences.

**译文：** 鸭子在结冰池塘上踩出路线、狗在花园中开辟直达小路等动物意愿小径，说明这种小径在人类和动物的活动中都具有适应性。

## 15. E2-2025-T4-P1-S15

As desire paths criss-cross through both physical and virtual landscapes, they stand as a proof of the collective insistence on forging unconventional routes and embracing the spirit of communal choice.

**译文：** 当意愿小径纵横于现实和虚拟空间时，它们证明了群体坚持开辟非常规路线、拥抱共同选择精神的力量。

**长难句拆解：**

- 主干：They stand as a proof of the collective insistence（它们成为群体坚持的证明）
- 逻辑：As从句把意愿小径的存在范围扩展到现实与网络，主句用两个并列动名词概括其象征意义：开辟非常规路线和坚持共同选择。
- 结构：
  - 时间/伴随状语从句(As)：As desire paths criss-cross through both physical and virtual landscapes
  - 主句：they stand as a proof of the collective insistence
  -   └ 介词短语(on)：on forging unconventional routes and embracing the spirit of communal choice
  -       └ 并列动名词：forging ... and embracing ...
- 考点：
  - criss-cross 意为“纵横交错地穿过”。
  - insistence on 指“对……的坚持”。
  - communal choice 强调群体共同形成的选择。
