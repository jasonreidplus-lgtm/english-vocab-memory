# 2025 年考研英语二阅读 Text 2 · 逐句精读

## 1. E2-2025-T2-P1-S1

When it was established, the National Health Service (NHS) was visionary: offering high-quality, timely care to meet the dominant needs of the population it served.

**译文：** 英国国民医疗服务体系创立时极具远见：它提供优质、及时的医疗服务，以满足当时服务人群的主要需求。

## 2. E2-2025-T2-P1-S2

Nearly 75 years on, with the UK facing very different health challenges, it is clear that the model is out of date.

**译文：** 近75年过去，英国面对的健康挑战已经大不相同，显然这一模式已经过时。

## 3. E2-2025-T2-P1-S3

From life expectancy to cancer and infant mortality rates, we are lagging behind many of our peers.

**译文：** 从预期寿命到癌症和婴儿死亡率，我们都落后于许多同类国家。

## 4. E2-2025-T2-P1-S4

With more than 6.8 million on waitlists, healthcare is becoming increasingly inaccessible for those who cannot opt to pay for private treatment; and the cost of providing healthcare is increasingly squeezing our investment in other public services.

**译文：** 候诊名单上已有超过680万人，无法选择自费接受私立医疗的人越来越难获得医疗服务；与此同时，医疗成本也日益挤压我们对其他公共服务的投入。

**长难句拆解：**

- 主干：Healthcare is becoming inaccessible; the cost is squeezing investment（医疗服务愈发难以获得，成本正挤压其他投入）
- 逻辑：With先给出候诊规模，分号两侧再并列说明可及性下降和公共资金被挤压两种后果；who限定无法购买私立医疗的人群。
- 结构：
  - with复合背景：With more than 6.8 million on waitlists
  - 并列分句一：healthcare is becoming increasingly inaccessible for those
  -   └ 定语从句(who)：who cannot opt to pay for private treatment
  - 并列分句二：and the cost of providing healthcare is increasingly squeezing our investment in other public services
- 考点：
  - waitlist 指等待接受医疗服务的名单。
  - opt to do 意为“选择做某事”。
  - squeeze investment 指成本压力压缩可用于其他领域的投资。

## 5. E2-2025-T2-P1-S5

As demand for healthcare continues to grow, pressures on the workforce - which is already near breaking point - will only become more acute.

**译文：** 随着医疗需求持续增长，本已接近崩溃临界点的医护队伍将面临更加严峻的压力。

## 6. E2-2025-T2-P1-S6

Many of the answers to the crisis in health and care are well rehearsed.

**译文：** 应对医疗和照护危机的许多办法，人们早已反复讨论。

## 7. E2-2025-T2-P1-S7

We need to be much better at reducing and diverting demand on health services, rather than simply managing it.

**译文：** 我们必须更有效地减少医疗服务需求并将其分流，而不能只是管理这些需求。

## 8. E2-2025-T2-P1-S8

Much more needs to be invested in communities and primary care to reduce our reliance on hospitals.

**译文：** 社区和基层医疗需要获得更多投入，以降低我们对医院的依赖。

## 9. E2-2025-T2-P1-S9

And capacity in social care needs to be greater, to support the growing number of people living with long-term conditions.

**译文：** 社会照护的服务能力也需要增强，才能支持日益增多的长期病患者。

## 10. E2-2025-T2-P1-S10

Yet despite two decades of strategies and a number of major health reforms, we have failed to make meaningful progress on any of these aims.

**译文：** 然而，尽管过去二十年出台了多项战略并实施了数次重大医疗改革，我们在这些目标上都未能取得实质性进展。

## 11. E2-2025-T2-P1-S11

That is why the Reform think tank is launching a new programme of work entitled "Reimagining health", supported by ten former health ministers.

**译文：** 正因如此，Reform智库在十位前卫生大臣的支持下，启动了一项名为"重新构想健康"的新研究计划。

## 12. E2-2025-T2-P1-S12

Together, we are calling for a much more open and honest conversation about the future of health in the UK, and an "urgent rethink" of the hospital-centric model we retain.

**译文：** 我们共同呼吁，就英国医疗的未来展开一场更加公开、坦诚的讨论，并"紧急重新思考"我们仍在沿用的以医院为中心的模式。

**长难句拆解：**

- 主干：We are calling for a conversation and an urgent rethink（我们呼吁开展讨论并紧急反思）
- 逻辑：call for后并列两个名词中心：conversation和rethink；末尾省略关系代词的从句说明需要反思的是英国仍在保留的模式。
- 结构：
  - 主句：Together, we are calling for a much more open and honest conversation ... and an urgent rethink ...
  -   ├ 并列宾语一：a ... conversation about the future of health in the UK
  -   └ 并列宾语二：an urgent rethink of the hospital-centric model
  -       └ 定语从句〔省略that，修饰 model〕：we retain
- 考点：
  - call for 意为“呼吁、要求”。
  - hospital-centric 指“以医院为中心的”。
  - retain 在此意为“继续保留、沿用”。

## 13. E2-2025-T2-P1-S13

This must begin with the question of how we maximise the health of the nation, rather than "fix" the NHS.

**译文：** 这种反思必须从一个问题开始：我们如何最大限度提升全民健康，而不是如何"修好"国民医疗服务体系。

## 14. E2-2025-T2-P1-S14

It is estimated, for example, that healthcare accounts for only about 20% of health outcomes.

**译文：** 例如，据估计医疗服务本身只决定约20%的健康结果。

## 15. E2-2025-T2-P1-S15

Much more important are the places we live, work and socialise - yet there is no clear cross-government strategy for improving these social determinants of health.

**译文：** 更重要的是我们生活、工作和社交的环境，但政府各部门之间并没有一项明确战略来改善这些健康的社会决定因素。

## 16. E2-2025-T2-P1-S16

Worse, when policies like the national obesity strategy are scrapped, taxpayers are left with the hefty price tag of treating the illnesses, like diabetes, that result.

**译文：** 更糟的是，国家肥胖战略之类的政策一旦被取消，纳税人便不得不承担治疗由此产生的糖尿病等疾病的高昂费用。

## 17. E2-2025-T2-P1-S17

Reform wants to ask how power and resources should be distributed in our health system.

**译文：** Reform希望探讨，权力和资源应如何在我们的医疗体系中分配。

## 18. E2-2025-T2-P1-S18

What health functions should remain at the centre, and what should be given to local leaders, often responsible for services that create health, and with a much better understanding of the needs of their populations?

**译文：** 哪些医疗职能应保留在中央，哪些应交给地方领导者？这些领导者往往负责那些能够促进健康的服务，也更加了解当地居民的需求。

**长难句拆解：**

- 主干：What functions should remain, and what should be given to local leaders?（哪些职能应保留，哪些应交给地方领导者？）
- 逻辑：两个what疑问分句对照中央和地方的职能分配；后面的responsible和with结构补充说明地方领导者更适合承接部分职能的理由。
- 结构：
  - 并列疑问分句一：What health functions should remain at the centre
  - 并列疑问分句二：and what should be given to local leaders
  -   ├ 分词短语〔补充 local leaders〕：often responsible for services
  -   │   └ 定语从句(that)：that create health
  -   └ 并列补充：and with a much better understanding of the needs of their populations
- 考点：
  - at the centre 在本文语境中指中央层级。
  - services that create health 指能改善健康决定因素的公共服务。
  - their populations 指各地方领导者所服务的当地居民。
