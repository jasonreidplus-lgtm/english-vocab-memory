# 2018 年考研英语二阅读 Text 4 · 逐句精读

## 1. E2-2018-T4-P1-S1

To combat the trap of putting a premium on being busy, Cal Newport, author of Deep Work: Rules for Focused Success in a Distracted World, recommends building a habit of "deep work" - the ability to focus without distraction.

**译文：** 为了摆脱过分看重忙碌的陷阱，《深度工作：在分心世界中专注成功的法则》作者卡尔·纽波特建议养成“深度工作”的习惯，也就是不受干扰、专心致志的能力。

## 2. E2-2018-T4-P1-S2

There are a number of approaches to mastering the art of deep work - be it lengthy retreats dedicated to a specific task; developing a daily ritual; or taking a "journalistic" approach to seizing moments of deep work when you can throughout the day.

**译文：** 掌握深度工作艺术的方法有很多：可以长期闭关专攻某项任务，可以建立每日固定仪式，也可以采取“新闻记者式”的方法，随时抓住一天中能够进行深度工作的片刻。

**长难句拆解：**

- 主干：主句：There are a number of approaches to mastering the art of deep work（掌握深度工作有多种方法）
- 逻辑：主句提出方法多样，be it之后列举长期闭关、每日仪式和随时抓住机会三种路线。
- 结构：
  - 存在句：There are a number of approaches
  -   └ 介词短语作后置定语：to mastering the art of deep work
  - 破折号后列举(be it)：
  -   ├ lengthy retreats dedicated to a specific task
  -   ├ developing a daily ritual
  -   └ taking a 'journalistic' approach to seizing moments of deep work when you can throughout the day
- 考点：
  - approach to中的to是介词，所以后接mastering。
  - be it A, B, or C是让步列举结构，意为“无论是A、B还是C”。
  - when you can后省略seize moments of deep work。

## 3. E2-2018-T4-P1-S3

Whichever approach, the key is to determine your length of focus time and stick to it.

**译文：** 无论采用哪种方法，关键都是确定自己的专注时长并坚持下去。

## 4. E2-2018-T4-P1-S4

Newport also recommends "deep scheduling" to combat constant interruptions and get more done in less time.

**译文：** 纽波特还建议采用“深度日程安排”，以对抗不断出现的干扰，在更短时间内完成更多工作。

## 5. E2-2018-T4-P1-S5

"At any given point, I should have deep work scheduled for roughly the next month.

**译文：** 他写道：“在任何一个时间点，我都应该已为未来大约一个月安排好深度工作。

## 6. E2-2018-T4-P1-S6

Once on the calendar, I protect this time like I would a doctor's appointment or important meeting," he writes.

**译文：** 一旦写进日程，我就会像对待就诊预约或重要会议那样保护这段时间。”

## 7. E2-2018-T4-P1-S7

Another approach to getting more done in less time is to rethink how you prioritise your day - in particular how we craft our to-do lists.

**译文：** 要在更少时间内完成更多工作，另一种方法是重新考虑如何确定一天事务的优先级，尤其是如何编排待办事项清单。

## 8. E2-2018-T4-P1-S8

Tim Harford, author of Messy: The Power of Disorder to Transform Our Lives, points to a study in the early 1980s that divided undergraduates into two groups: some were advised to set out monthly goals and study activities; others were told to plan activities and goals in much more detail, day by day.

**译文：** 《混乱：无序如何改变我们的生活》一书作者蒂姆·哈福德提到20世纪80年代初的一项研究：研究把大学生分成两组，一组被建议列出每月目标和学习活动，另一组则被要求按天制定详细得多的活动与目标计划。

**长难句拆解：**

- 主干：主句：Tim Harford points to a study（蒂姆·哈福德提到一项研究）
- 逻辑：主句引入研究，that从句说明分组方式，冒号后的两个被动句具体交代两组收到的不同规划建议。
- 结构：
  - 主语：Tim Harford
  -   └ 同位语：author of Messy: The Power of Disorder to Transform Our Lives
  - 谓语宾语：points to a study in the early 1980s
  -   └ 定语从句(that)：that divided undergraduates into two groups
  - 冒号后解释two groups：
  -   ├ 分句①：some were advised to set out monthly goals and study activities
  -   └ 分句②：others were told to plan activities and goals in much more detail, day by day
- 考点：
  - point to在此意为“援引、提到”。
  - advise sb to do与tell sb to do在句中都用了被动语态。
  - set out goals意为“列出、制定目标”。

## 9. E2-2018-T4-P1-S9

While the researchers assumed that the well-structured daily plans would be most effective when it came to the execution of tasks, they were wrong: the detailed daily plans demotivated students.

**译文：** 研究人员原以为，结构严密的每日计划在执行任务时最为有效，但事实证明他们错了：过于详细的每日计划反而让学生失去动力。

## 10. E2-2018-T4-P1-S10

Harford argues that inevitable distractions often render the daily to-do list ineffective, while leaving room for improvisation in such a list can reap the best results.

**译文：** 哈福德认为，不可避免的干扰往往会让每日待办清单失效，而在清单中给临场调整留出空间，反而能取得最佳结果。

## 11. E2-2018-T4-P1-S11

In order to make the most of our focus and energy, we also need to embrace downtime, or as Newport suggests, "be lazy".

**译文：** 为了充分利用我们的专注力和精力，我们还需要接受休息时间，或者用纽波特的话说，要“懒惰”。

## 12. E2-2018-T4-P1-S12

"Idleness is not just a vacation, an indulgence or a vice; it is as indispensable to the brain as vitamin D is to the body…[idleness] is, paradoxically, necessary to getting any work done," he argues.

**译文：** 他主张：“闲散不仅是假期、放纵或恶习；它对大脑而言，就像维生素D对身体一样不可或缺……矛盾的是，闲散是完成任何工作所必需的。”

**长难句拆解：**

- 主干：引语主干：Idleness is not just A; it is as indispensable to the brain as vitamin D is to the body; idleness is necessary to getting work done（闲散不只是A；它对大脑不可或缺；而且是完成工作的必要条件）
- 逻辑：三层递进先否定对闲散的贬义理解，再用维生素D类比其必要性，最后得出看似矛盾的结论：休息是工作的前提。
- 结构：
  - 分句①：Idleness is not just a vacation, an indulgence or a vice
  - 分号后分句②：it is as indispensable to the brain as vitamin D is to the body
  - 省略号后总结：idleness is, paradoxically, necessary to getting any work done
  - 插入性副词：paradoxically
  - 引述说明：he argues
- 考点：
  - not just A承接后文更强的判断，不宜只译为简单并列。
  - as indispensable to A as B is to C表示“对A而言，就像B对C一样不可或缺”。
  - be necessary to中的to是介词，故后接getting。

## 13. E2-2018-T4-P1-S13

Srini Pillay, an assistant professor of psychiatry at Harvard Medical School, believes this counterintuitive link between downtime and productivity may be due to the way our brains operate.

**译文：** 哈佛医学院精神病学助理教授斯里尼·皮莱认为，休息时间与生产效率之间这种反直觉的联系，可能源于我们大脑的运作方式。

## 14. E2-2018-T4-P1-S14

When our brains switch between being focused and unfocused on a task, they tend to be more efficient.

**译文：** 当我们的大脑在专注于任务和不专注于任务的状态之间切换时，往往会更加高效。

## 15. E2-2018-T4-P1-S15

"What people don't realise is that in order to complete these tasks they need to use both the focus and unfocus circuits in their brain," says Pillay.

**译文：** 皮莱说：“人们没有意识到，要完成这些任务，就需要同时使用大脑中的专注回路和非专注回路。”
