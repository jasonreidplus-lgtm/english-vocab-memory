# 2010 年考研英语二阅读 Text 3 · 逐句精读

## 1. E2-2010-T3-P1-S1

Over the past decade, many companies had perfected the art of creating automatic behaviors - habits - among consumers.

**译文：** 在过去十年中，许多公司已经精通了让消费者形成自动化行为——也就是习惯——的技巧。

## 2. E2-2010-T3-P1-S2

These habits have helped companies earn billions of dollars when customers eat snacks or wipe counters almost without thinking, often in response to a carefully designed set of daily cues.

**译文：** 当顾客几乎不假思索地吃零食或擦拭台面时，这些习惯已经帮助企业赚取数十亿美元，而这些行为往往是对一套精心设计的日常提示所作的反应。

## 3. E2-2010-T3-P1-S3

"There are fundamental public health problems, like dirty hands instead of a soap habit, that remain killers only because we can't figure out how to change people's habits," said Dr. Curtis, the director of the Hygiene Center at the London School of Hygiene & Tropical Medicine.

**译文：** 伦敦卫生与热带医学学院卫生中心主任柯蒂斯博士说：“有些根本性的公共卫生问题，例如人们手很脏却没有养成用肥皂洗手的习惯，之所以至今仍能致命，仅仅是因为我们还不知道如何改变人们的习惯。”

**长难句拆解：**

- 主干：引语主干：There are public health problems that remain killers（有些公共卫生问题仍会致命）
- 逻辑：核心因果是“问题仍致命，只因不会改变习惯”；that 限定 problems，only because 强调原因单一。
- 结构：
  - 直接引语主句：There are fundamental public health problems
  -   ├ 举例插入语：like dirty hands instead of a soap habit
  -   └ 定语从句〔that，修饰 problems〕：that remain killers
  -       └ 原因状语从句(only because)：only because we can't figure out
  -           └ 疑问词+不定式作宾语：how to change people's habits
  - 引语后的报告语：said Dr. Curtis
  -   └ 同位语：the director of the Hygiene Center at the London School of Hygiene & Tropical Medicine
- 考点：
  - remain killers 表示“仍然是致命因素”，不是“杀手仍存在”。
  - figure out how to... 意为“弄明白如何……”。
  - instead of a soap habit 表示缺少使用肥皂的习惯。

## 4. E2-2010-T3-P1-S4

"We wanted to learn from private industry how to create new behaviors that happen automatically."

**译文：** “我们希望向私营企业学习，了解如何创造能够自动发生的新行为。”

## 5. E2-2010-T3-P1-S5

The companies that Dr. Curtis turned to - Procter & Gamble, Colgate-Palmolive and Unilever - had invested hundreds of millions of dollars finding the subtle cues in consumers' lives that corporations could use to introduce new routines.

**译文：** 柯蒂斯博士求助的公司——宝洁、高露洁-棕榄和联合利华——已经投入数亿美元，寻找消费者生活中那些可供企业用来引入新惯例的细微信号。

**长难句拆解：**

- 主干：S+V+O：The companies had invested hundreds of millions of dollars（这些公司已投入数亿美元）
- 逻辑：第一个 that 说明她求助于哪些公司；第二个 that 说明公司要寻找什么样的 cues；finding 表明巨额投入用于寻找线索。
- 结构：
  - 主句：The companies ... had invested hundreds of millions of dollars
  -   ├ 定语从句〔that，修饰 companies〕：that Dr. Curtis turned to
  -   ├ 破折号同位列举：Procter & Gamble, Colgate-Palmolive and Unilever
  -   └ 方式/内容状语：finding the subtle cues in consumers' lives
  -       └ 定语从句〔that，修饰 cues〕：that corporations could use to introduce new routines
  -           └ 目的状语(to)：to introduce new routines
- 考点：
  - turn to somebody 意为“向某人求助”。
  - invest money doing 在此表示“投入资金做某事”。
  - cue 指触发习惯行为的“提示、信号”。

## 6. E2-2010-T3-P1-S6

If you look hard enough, you'll find that many of the products we use every day - chewing gums, skin moisturizers, disinfecting wipes, air fresheners, water purifiers, health snacks, teeth whiteners, fabric softeners, vitamins - are results of manufactured habits.

**译文：** 只要仔细观察，你就会发现，我们每天使用的许多产品——口香糖、润肤霜、消毒湿巾、空气清新剂、净水器、健康零食、牙齿美白剂、衣物柔顺剂和维生素——都是人为塑造习惯的产物。

## 7. E2-2010-T3-P1-S7

A century ago, few people regularly brushed their teeth multiple times a day.

**译文：** 一个世纪前，很少有人每天多次定期刷牙。

## 8. E2-2010-T3-P1-S8

Today, because of shrewd advertising and public health campaigns, many Americans habitually give their pearly whites a cavity-preventing scrub twice a day, often with Colgate, Crest or one of the other brands.

**译文：** 如今，由于精明的广告和公共卫生宣传，许多美国人已经习惯每天两次擦洗自己洁白的牙齿以预防蛀牙，常用的是高露洁、佳洁士或其他品牌。

## 9. E2-2010-T3-P1-S9

A few decades ago, many people didn't drink water outside of a meal.

**译文：** 几十年前，许多人在用餐之外并不喝水。

## 10. E2-2010-T3-P1-S10

Then beverage companies started bottling the production of far-off springs, and now office workers unthinkingly sip bottled water all day long.

**译文：** 后来，饮料公司开始把远方泉水生产并装瓶，如今办公室职员会整天不假思索地啜饮瓶装水。

## 11. E2-2010-T3-P1-S11

Chewing gum, once bought primarily by adolescent boys, is now featured in commercials as a breath freshener and teeth cleanser for use after a meal.

**译文：** 口香糖过去主要由青春期男孩购买，如今在广告中则被塑造成饭后使用的清新口气和洁牙产品。

## 12. E2-2010-T3-P1-S12

Skin moisturizers are advertised as part of morning beauty rituals, slipped in between hair brushing and putting on makeup.

**译文：** 润肤霜被宣传成晨间美容仪式的一部分，安插在梳头和化妆之间。

## 13. E2-2010-T3-P1-S13

"Our products succeed when they become part of daily or weekly patterns," said Carol Berning, a consumer psychologist who recently retired from Procter & Gamble, the company that sold $76 billion of Tide, Crest and other products last year.

**译文：** 宝洁公司最近退休的消费者心理学家卡罗尔·伯宁说：“当我们的产品成为日常或每周惯例的一部分时，它们就成功了。”该公司去年销售了价值760亿美元的汰渍、佳洁士及其他产品。

**长难句拆解：**

- 主干：引语主干：Our products succeed when they become part of patterns（产品成为惯例的一部分时就成功了）
- 逻辑：引语给出产品成功的条件；said 后人物身份通过同位语和两层定语关系连续补充，阅读时应先抓住引语和报告语。
- 结构：
  - 直接引语：Our products succeed
  -   └ 时间状语从句(when)：when they become part of daily or weekly patterns
  - 报告语：said Carol Berning
  -   └ 同位语：a consumer psychologist
  -       └ 定语从句〔who，修饰 psychologist〕：who recently retired from Procter & Gamble
  -           └ Procter & Gamble 的同位说明：the company
  -               └ 定语从句〔that，修饰 company〕：that sold $76 billion of Tide, Crest and other products last year
- 考点：
  - patterns 在语境中指反复发生的日常/每周惯例。
  - $76 billion of... 表示价值760亿美元的产品销售额。
  - who 修饰 Carol Berning 的职业身份，that 则修饰 Procter & Gamble。

## 14. E2-2010-T3-P1-S14

"Creating positive habits is a huge part of improving our consumers' lives, and it's essential to making new products commercially viable."

**译文：** “培养积极习惯是改善消费者生活的一个极其重要的部分，也是让新产品具备商业可行性的关键。”

## 15. E2-2010-T3-P1-S15

Through experiments and observation, social scientists like Dr. Berning have learned that there is power in tying certain behaviors to habitual cues through ruthless advertising.

**译文：** 通过实验和观察，伯宁博士等社会科学家已经认识到，借助强势广告把某些行为与习惯性提示联系起来，能够产生强大的力量。

## 16. E2-2010-T3-P1-S16

As this new science of habit has emerged, controversies have erupted when the tactics have been used to sell questionable beauty creams or unhealthy foods.

**译文：** 随着这门新兴的习惯科学逐渐形成，当这些手段被用来推销功效可疑的美容霜或不健康食品时，争议也随之爆发。
