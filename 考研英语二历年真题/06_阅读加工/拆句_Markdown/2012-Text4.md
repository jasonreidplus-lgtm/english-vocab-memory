# 2012 年考研英语二阅读 Text 4 · 逐句精读

## 1. E2-2012-T4-P1-S1

The great recession may be over, but this era of high joblessness is probably beginning.

**译文：** 大衰退或许已经结束，但这个高失业率时代可能才刚刚开始。

## 2. E2-2012-T4-P1-S2

Before it ends, it will likely change the life course and character of a generation of young adults.

**译文：** 在它结束之前，很可能会改变一代年轻人的人生轨迹和性格。

## 3. E2-2012-T4-P1-S3

And ultimately, it is likely to reshape our politics, our culture, and the character of our society for years.

**译文：** 最终，它还可能在未来多年重塑我们的政治、文化以及整个社会的特征。

## 4. E2-2012-T4-P1-S4

No one tries harder than the jobless to find silver linings in this national economic disaster.

**译文：** 没有谁比失业者更努力地从这场全国性经济灾难中寻找一线希望。

## 5. E2-2012-T4-P1-S5

Many said that unemployment, while extremely painful, had improved them in some ways: they had become less materialistic and more financially prudent; they were more aware of the struggles of others.

**译文：** 许多人说，失业虽然极其痛苦，却在某些方面使自己变得更好：他们不再那么崇尚物质，在财务上更加谨慎，也更能体会他人的困境。

## 6. E2-2012-T4-P1-S6

In limited respects, perhaps the recession will leave society better off.

**译文：** 或许在有限的几个方面，这场衰退会让社会状况有所改善。

## 7. E2-2012-T4-P1-S7

At the very least, it has awoken us from our national fever dream of easy riches and bigger houses, and put a necessary end to an era of reckless personal spending.

**译文：** 至少，它已把我们从轻松致富、追求更大房屋的全民狂热美梦中唤醒，并为一个个人消费毫无节制的时代画上了必要的句号。

## 8. E2-2012-T4-P1-S8

But for the most part, these benefits seem thin, uncertain, and far off.

**译文：** 但总体而言，这些益处似乎很微弱、很不确定，而且遥不可及。

## 9. E2-2012-T4-P1-S9

In The Moral Consequences of Economic Growth, the economic historian Benjamin Friedman argues that both inside and outside the U.S., lengthy periods of economic stagnation or decline have almost always left society more mean-spirited and less inclusive, and have usually stopped or reversed the advance of rights and freedoms.

**译文：** 经济史学家本杰明·弗里德曼在《经济增长的道德后果》中指出，无论在美国国内还是国外，经济长期停滞或衰退几乎总会使社会变得更刻薄、更缺乏包容，并且通常会阻止甚至逆转权利与自由的进步。

**长难句拆解：**

- 主干：S+V+O：Benjamin Friedman argues that lengthy periods have left society..., and have stopped or reversed...（弗里德曼指出，长期停滞会使社会……并阻碍或逆转……）
- 逻辑：that 从句是整句论点；主语 lengthy periods 带两个并列谓语，分别描述社会性格恶化和权利自由进程受阻。
- 结构：
  - 出处状语：In The Moral Consequences of Economic Growth
  - 主句：the economic historian Benjamin Friedman argues
  -   └ 宾语从句(that)：both inside and outside the U.S., lengthy periods of economic stagnation or decline have ...
  -       ├ 并列谓语①：have almost always left society more mean-spirited and less inclusive
  -       └ 并列谓语②(and)：have usually stopped or reversed the advance of rights and freedoms
- 考点：
  - leave + 宾语 + 形容词：使社会处于某种状态。
  - mean-spirited 指刻薄、缺乏善意。
  - the advance of rights and freedoms 指权利与自由的发展进程。

## 10. E2-2012-T4-P1-S10

Anti-immigrant sentiment typically increases, as does conflict between races and classes.

**译文：** 反移民情绪通常会上升，种族与阶级之间的冲突也是如此。

## 11. E2-2012-T4-P1-S11

Income inequality usually falls during a recession, but it has not shrunk in this one.

**译文：** 收入不平等通常会在经济衰退期间缩小，但在这场衰退中却没有。

## 12. E2-2012-T4-P1-S12

Indeed, this period of economic weakness may reinforce class divides, and decrease opportunities to cross them - especially for young people.

**译文：** 事实上，这段经济疲软期可能会加深阶级鸿沟，减少跨越鸿沟的机会，尤其会影响年轻人。

## 13. E2-2012-T4-P1-S13

The research of Till Von Wachter, the economist at Columbia University, suggests that not all people graduating into a recession see their life chances dimmed: those with degrees from elite universities catch up fairly quickly to where they otherwise would have been if they had graduated in better times; it is the masses beneath them that are left behind.

**译文：** 哥伦比亚大学经济学家蒂尔·冯·瓦赫特的研究表明，并非所有在经济衰退期毕业的人都会看到人生机会黯淡：名校毕业生会相当快地追赶上假如自己在景气时期毕业本可达到的水平；真正被甩在后面的是他们之下的广大人群。

**长难句拆解：**

- 主干：S+V+O：The research suggests that not all graduates see their chances dimmed（研究表明并非所有毕业生的机会都会变差）
- 逻辑：not all 是部分否定；冒号后解释哪些人能追上、哪些人会掉队；末尾用 it is...that... 强调真正受损的是普通大众。
- 结构：
  - 主句：The research of Till Von Wachter ... suggests
  -   ├ 同位语插入：the economist at Columbia University
  -   └ 宾语从句(that)：not all people graduating into a recession see their life chances dimmed
  - 冒号后分项解释①：those with degrees from elite universities catch up fairly quickly
  -   └ 地点/程度从句(where)：to where they otherwise would have been
  -       └ 条件状语从句(if)：if they had graduated in better times
  - 分号后强调解释②：it is the masses beneath them that are left behind
- 考点：
  - graduating into a recession 指毕业时恰逢经济衰退。
  - would have been if... 是对过去相反情况的虚拟假设。
  - see their life chances dimmed 可理解为“发现人生机会变得黯淡”。

## 14. E2-2012-T4-P1-S14

In the Internet age, it is particularly easy to see the resentment that has always been hidden within American society.

**译文：** 在互联网时代，人们尤其容易看到美国社会中一直潜藏的怨恨。

## 15. E2-2012-T4-P1-S15

More difficult, in the moment, is discerning precisely how these lean times are affecting society's character.

**译文：** 眼下更难做到的，是准确判断这些艰难岁月正在如何影响社会的性格。

## 16. E2-2012-T4-P1-S16

In many respects, the U.S. was more socially tolerant entering this recession than at any time in its history, and a variety of national polls on social conflict since then have shown mixed results.

**译文：** 在许多方面，美国进入这次衰退时的社会宽容程度超过历史上任何时期；但此后围绕社会冲突开展的多项全国性民意调查却给出了喜忧参半的结果。

**长难句拆解：**

- 主干：并列主干：The U.S. was more tolerant..., and polls have shown mixed results（美国当时更宽容，但多项调查结果不一）
- 逻辑：前半句给出进入衰退时较高的社会宽容基线，后半句说明衰退后的调查并未呈现单一方向，因此影响仍难判断。
- 结构：
  - 范围状语：In many respects
  - 分句①：the U.S. was more socially tolerant ... than at any time in its history
  -   └ 时间状语：entering this recession
  - 并列分句②(and)：a variety of national polls on social conflict since then have shown mixed results
- 考点：
  - entering this recession 是时间压缩表达，相当于 when it entered this recession。
  - more...than at any time in its history 表示高于历史上任何时候。
  - mixed results 指结果有好有坏、没有一致结论。

## 17. E2-2012-T4-P1-S17

We will have to wait and see exactly how these hard times will reshape our social fabric.

**译文：** 我们必须等待并观察，这些艰难岁月究竟会怎样重塑我们的社会结构。

## 18. E2-2012-T4-P1-S18

But they certainly will reshape it, and all the more so the longer they extend.

**译文：** 但它们肯定会重塑社会结构，而且持续时间越长，重塑的程度就越深。
