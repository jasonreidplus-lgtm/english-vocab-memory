# 2022 年考研英语二阅读 Text 3 · 逐句手译

## 1

We have all encountered them, in both our personal and professional lives.

**译文：** 无论在个人生活还是职业生活中，我们都遇到过它们。

## 2

Think about the times you felt tricked or frustrated by a membership or subscription that had a seamless sign-up process but was later difficult to cancel.

**译文：** 想想那些时刻：某项会员服务或订阅的注册过程顺畅无比，后来却难以取消，让你觉得受骗或感到沮丧。

## 3

Something that should be simple and transparent can be complicated, intentionally or unintentionally, in ways that impair consumer choice.

**译文：** 本应简单透明的事情，可能被有意或无意地复杂化，从而损害消费者的选择权。

## 4

These are examples of dark patterns.

**译文：** 这些就是“暗黑模式”的例子。

## 5

First coined in 2010 by user experience expert Harry Brignull, "dark patterns" is a catch-all term for practices that manipulate user interfaces to influence the decision-making ability of users.

**译文：** “暗黑模式”一词最早由用户体验专家哈里·布里格努尔于2010年提出，是对一类做法的统称：它们操纵用户界面，从而影响用户作出决定的能力。

## 6

Brignull identifies 12 types of common dark patterns, ranging from misdirection and hidden costs to "roach motel," where a user experience seems easy and intuitive at the start, but turns difficult when the user tries to get out.

**译文：** 布里格努尔归纳出12种常见暗黑模式，从误导和隐藏费用，到“蟑螂旅馆”模式；在后者中，用户体验一开始看似简单直观，但用户试图退出时却变得困难。

## 7

In a 2019 study of 53,000 product pages and 11,000 websites, researchers found that about one in 10 employs these design practices.

**译文：** 2019年一项针对5.3万个商品页面和1.1万个网站的研究发现，约每十个网站中就有一个采用这些设计手法。

## 8

Though widely prevalent, the concept of dark patterns is still not well understood.

**译文：** 尽管暗黑模式广泛存在，人们对这一概念仍缺乏充分理解。

## 9

Business and nonprofit leaders should be aware of dark patterns and try to avoid the gray areas they engender.

**译文：** 企业和非营利组织的负责人应当了解暗黑模式，并尽量避开其造成的灰色地带。

## 10

Where is the line between ethical, persuasive design and dark patterns?

**译文：** 合乎伦理的说服性设计与暗黑模式之间，界线究竟在哪里？

## 11

Businesses should engage in conversations with IT, compliance, risk, and legal teams to review their privacy policy, and include in the discussion the customer/user experience designers and coders responsible for the company's user interface, as well as the marketers and advertisers responsible for sign-ups, checkout baskets, pricing, and promotions.

**译文：** 企业应让信息技术、合规、风险和法务团队展开讨论，以审查其隐私政策；参与讨论的还应包括负责公司用户界面的客户/用户体验设计师和程序员，以及负责注册、结算购物篮、定价和促销的营销人员与广告人员。

## 12

Any or all these teams can play a role in creating or avoiding "digital deception."

**译文：** 这些团队中的任何一个或全部，都可能在制造或避免“数字欺骗”方面发挥作用。

## 13

Lawmakers and regulators are slowly starting to address the ambiguity around dark patterns, most recently at the state level.

**译文：** 立法者和监管机构正慢慢开始处理暗黑模式周围的模糊地带，最近的行动主要发生在州一级。

## 14

In March, the California Attorney General announced the approval of additional regulations under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) that "ensure that consumers will not be confused or misled when seeking to exercise their data privacy rights."

**译文：** 今年3月，加利福尼亚州总检察长宣布批准《加利福尼亚消费者隐私法》下的补充法规，以“确保消费者在寻求行使其数据隐私权时不会受到迷惑或误导”。

## 15

The regulations aim to ban dark patterns—this means prohibiting companies from using "confusing language or unnecessary steps such as forcing them to click through multiple screens or listen to reasons why they shouldn't opt out."

**译文：** 这些法规旨在禁止暗黑模式，也就是禁止公司使用“令人困惑的语言或不必要的步骤，例如迫使消费者点击多个页面，或听取他们不应选择退出的理由”。

## 16

As more states consider promulgating additional regulations, there is a need for greater accountability from within the business community.

**译文：** 随着更多州考虑颁布补充法规，企业界内部需要承担更大的责任。

## 17

Dark patterns also can be addressed on a self-regulatory basis, but only if organizations hold themselves accountable, not just to legal requirements but also to industry best practices and standards.

**译文：** 暗黑模式也可以通过行业自律加以治理，但前提是组织不仅对法律要求负责，也对行业最佳实践和标准负责。

> 译文为个人学习整理，不冒充考试机构标准答案。
