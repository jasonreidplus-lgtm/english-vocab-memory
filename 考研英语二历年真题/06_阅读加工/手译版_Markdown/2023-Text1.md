# 2023 年考研英语二阅读 Text 1 · 逐句手译

## 1

In the quest for the perfect lawn, homeowners across the country are taking a shortcut—and it is the environment that is paying the price.

**译文：** 为了拥有一片完美草坪，全国各地的房主都在走捷径，而为此付出代价的却是环境。

## 2

About eight million square metres of plastic grass is sold each year but opposition has now spread to the highest gardening circles.

**译文：** 每年售出的塑料草皮约有800万平方米，但反对意见如今已蔓延到园艺界最高层。

## 3

The Chelsea Flower Show has banned fake grass from this year's event, declaring it to be not part of its ethos.

**译文：** 切尔西花展已禁止在今年的活动中使用假草，并宣布假草不符合花展的理念。

## 4

The Royal Horticultural Society (RHS), which runs the annual show in west London, says it has introduced the ban because of the damage plastic grass does to the environment and biodiversity.

**译文：** 在伦敦西部主办这一年度花展的英国皇家园艺学会表示，之所以实施这项禁令，是因为塑料草皮会损害环境和生物多样性。

## 5

Ed Horne, of the RHS, said: "We launched our sustainability strategy last year and fake grass is just not in line with our ethos and views on plastic.

**译文：** 英国皇家园艺学会的埃德·霍恩说：“我们去年推出了可持续发展战略，而假草与我们在塑料问题上的理念和立场完全不符。

## 6

We recommend using real grass because of its environmental benefits, which include supporting wildlife, alleviating flooding and cooling the environment."

**译文：** 我们建议使用真草，因为它具有多种环境效益，包括为野生动物提供支持、缓解洪水以及给环境降温。”

## 7

The RHS's decision comes as campaigners try to raise awareness of the problems fake grass causes.

**译文：** 英国皇家园艺学会作出这一决定之际，活动人士正努力提高公众对假草所造成问题的认识。

## 8

A Twitter account, which claims to "cut through the greenwash" of artificial grass, already has more than 20,000 followers.

**译文：** 一个自称要“揭穿”人造草皮环保粉饰的推特账号，已经拥有两万多名关注者。

## 9

It is trying to encourage people to sign two petitions, one calling for a ban on the sale of plastic grass and another calling for an "ecological damage" tax on such lawns.

**译文：** 该账号正鼓励人们签署两份请愿书：一份呼吁禁止销售塑料草皮，另一份呼吁对这类草坪征收“生态损害”税。

## 10

They have gathered 7,276 and 11,282 signatures.

**译文：** 两份请愿书分别收集到7276个和11282个签名。

## 11

However, supporters of fake grass point out that there is also an environmental impact with natural lawns, which need mowing and therefore usually consume electricity or petrol.

**译文：** 然而，假草的支持者指出，天然草坪同样会影响环境，因为它需要修剪，通常会因此消耗电力或汽油。

## 12

The industry also points out that real grass requires considerable amounts of water, weed killer or other treatments and that people who lay fake grass tend to use their garden more.

**译文：** 该行业还指出，真草需要大量用水、除草剂或其他养护处理，而且铺设假草的人往往会更频繁地使用自家花园。

## 13

The industry also claims that people who lay fake grass spend an average of £500 on trees or shrubs for their garden, which provides habitat for insects.

**译文：** 该行业还声称，铺设假草的人平均会在花园中的树木或灌木上花费500英镑，而这些植物能为昆虫提供栖息地。

## 14

In response to another petition last year about banning fake lawns, which gathered 30,000 signatures, the government responded that it has "no plans to ban the use of artificial grass."

**译文：** 去年另一份要求禁止假草坪的请愿书收集了3万个签名，政府在回应时表示，它“没有禁止使用人造草的计划”。

## 15

It added: "We prefer to help people and organizations make the right choice rather than legislating on such matters.

**译文：** 政府补充说：“我们更愿意帮助人们和组织作出正确选择，而不是就此类事项立法。

## 16

However, the use of artificial grass must comply with the legal and policy safeguards in place to protect biodiversity and ensure sustainable drainage, while measures such as the strengthened biodiversity duty should serve to encourage public authorities to consider sustainable alternatives."

**译文：** 不过，人造草的使用必须遵守现行的法律和政策保障措施，以保护生物多样性并确保可持续排水；同时，强化生物多样性职责等措施应能促使公共机构考虑可持续的替代方案。”
