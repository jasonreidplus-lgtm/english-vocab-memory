# 2024 年考研英语二阅读 Text 2 · 逐句手译

## 1

The UK is facing a future construction crisis because of a failure to plant trees to produce wood, Confor has warned.

**译文：** 英国林业与木材行业组织Confor警告称，由于未能种植用于生产木材的树木，英国未来将面临建筑危机。

## 2

The forestry and wood trade body has called for urgent action to reduce the country's reliance on timber imports and provide a stable supply of wood for future generations.

**译文：** 该组织呼吁立即采取行动，降低英国对进口木材的依赖，并为后代提供稳定的木材供应。

## 3

Currently only 20 percent of the UK's wood requirement is home-grown while it remains the second-largest net importer of timber in the world.

**译文：** 目前，英国所需木材中只有20%产自本国，而英国仍是世界第二大木材净进口国。

## 4

Coming at a time of fresh incentives from the UK government for landowners to grow more trees, the trade body says these don't go far enough and fail to promote the benefits of planting them to boost timber supplies.

**译文：** 英国政府最近推出新激励措施，鼓励土地所有者种更多树，但该行业组织表示，这些措施力度不够，也没有宣传植树对于增加木材供应的益处。

## 5

"Not only are we facing a carbon crisis now, but we will also be facing a future construction crisis because of a failure to plant trees to produce wood," said Stuart Goodall, chief executive of Confor.

**译文：** Confor首席执行官斯图尔特·古德尔说："我们现在不仅面临碳危机，未来还会因为没有种植用于生产木材的树木而面临建筑危机。"

## 6

"For decades we have not taken responsibility for investing in our domestic wood supply, leaving us exposed to fluctuating prices and fighting for future supplies of wood as global demand rises and our own supplies fall."

**译文：** "几十年来，我们一直没有承担投资国内木材供应的责任，使自己暴露在价格波动之下；随着全球需求上升而本国供应下降，我们还得争抢未来的木材供应。"

## 7

The UK has ideal conditions for growing wood to build low-carbon homes and is a global leader in certifying that its forests are sustainably managed, Confor says.

**译文：** Confor表示，英国拥有种植木材、建造低碳住宅的理想条件，并且在认证森林得到可持续管理方面处于全球领先地位。

## 8

While around three quarters of Scottish homes are built from Scottish timber, the use of home-grown wood in England is only around 25 percent.

**译文：** 苏格兰约四分之三的住宅使用苏格兰木材建造，而英格兰使用本土木材的比例只有约25%。

## 9

The causes of the UK's current position are complex and range from outdated perceptions of productive forestry to the decimation of trees by grey squirrels.

**译文：** 英国形成当前局面的原因很复杂，既包括对生产性林业的陈旧观念，也包括灰松鼠对树木的大量破坏。

## 10

It also encompasses significant hesitation on behalf of farmers and other landowners to invest in long-term planting projects.

**译文：** 其中还包括农民和其他土地所有者对投资长期植树项目的严重犹豫。

## 11

While productive tree planting can deliver real financial benefits to rural economies and contribute to the UK's net-zero strategy, the focus of government support continues to be on food production and the rewilding and planting of native woodland solely for biodiversity.

**译文：** 尽管生产性植树能给农村经济带来切实的经济收益，并助力英国的净零战略，政府支持的重点却仍然放在粮食生产、土地再野化以及仅为保护生物多样性而种植本土林地上。

## 12

Goodall added: "While food production and biodiversity health are clearly of critical importance, we need our land to also provide secure supplies of wood for construction, manufacturing and contribute to net-zero."

**译文：** 古德尔补充说："虽然粮食生产和生物多样性健康显然至关重要，但我们的土地还应当为建筑和制造提供有保障的木材供应，并为实现净零目标作出贡献。"

## 13

"While the UK government has stated its ambition for more tree planting, there has been little action on the ground.

**译文：** "英国政府虽然已表明增加植树的雄心，但实际行动寥寥。

## 14

Confor is now calling for much greater impetus behind those aspirations to ensure we have enough wood to meet increasing demand."

**译文：** Confor目前正呼吁为这些目标注入更强劲的推动力，确保我们拥有足够木材来满足不断增长的需求。"
