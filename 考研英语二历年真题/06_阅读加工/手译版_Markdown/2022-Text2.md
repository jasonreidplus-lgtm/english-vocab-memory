# 2022 年考研英语二阅读 Text 2 · 逐句手译

## 1

More Americans are opting to work well into retirement, a growing trend that threatens to upend the old workforce model.

**译文：** 越来越多的美国人选择一直工作到退休之后很久；这一日益增长的趋势可能会颠覆旧有的劳动力模式。

## 2

One in three Americans who are at least 40 have or plan to have a job in retirement to prepare for a longer life, according to a survey conducted by Harris Poll for TD Ameritrade.

**译文：** 哈里斯民调公司为德美利证券开展的一项调查显示，为了应对更长的寿命，每三名40岁及以上的美国人中就有一人已经在退休后工作，或计划这样做。

## 3

Even more surprising is that more than half of "unretirees"—those who plan to work in retirement or went back to work after retiring—said they would be employed in their later years even if they had enough money to settle down, the survey showed.

**译文：** 更令人惊讶的是，调查显示，超过半数的“退而不休者”——即计划退休后继续工作，或退休后又重返职场的人——表示，即使他们有足够的钱安顿下来，晚年仍会继续受雇工作。

## 4

Financial needs aren't the only culprit for the "unretirement" trend.

**译文：** 经济需要并不是造成“退而不休”趋势的唯一原因。

## 5

Other reasons, according to the study, include personal fulfillment such as staying mentally fit, preventing boredom or avoiding depression.

**译文：** 研究称，其他原因还包括获得个人满足，例如保持思维敏锐、防止无聊或避免抑郁。

## 6

About 72% of "unretiree" respondents said that they would return to work once retired to keep mentally fit while 59% said it would be tied to making ends meet.

**译文：** 约72%的“退而不休”受访者表示，他们退休后会重返工作岗位以保持思维敏锐；另有59%表示，这样做与维持生计有关。

## 7

"The concept of retirement is evolving," said Christine Russell, senior manager of retirement at TD Ameritrade.

**译文：** “退休这一概念正在演变，”德美利证券退休业务高级经理克里斯蒂娜·拉塞尔说。

## 8

"It's not just about finances. The value of work is also driving folks to continue working past retirement."

**译文：** “它不只是一个财务问题。工作的价值也在促使人们过了退休年龄仍继续工作。”

## 9

One reason for the change in retirement patterns: Americans are living longer.

**译文：** 退休模式发生变化的一个原因是：美国人活得更久了。

## 10

The share of the population 65 and older was 16% in 2018, up 3.2% from the prior year, according to the U.S. Census Bureau.

**译文：** 美国人口普查局数据显示，2018年65岁及以上人口占比为16%，比前一年增加3.2%。

## 11

That's also up 30.2% since 2010.

**译文：** 这一数字与2010年相比也增长了30.2%。

## 12

Older Americans are also the fastest-growing segment of the U.S. workforce, and boomers are expected to live longer than previous generations.

**译文：** 老年美国人也是美国劳动力中增长最快的群体，而婴儿潮一代预计会比前几代人寿命更长。

## 13

The percentage of retirement-age people in the labor force has doubled over the past three decades.

**译文：** 过去三十年中，达到退休年龄却仍在劳动力队伍中的人口比例翻了一番。

## 14

About 20% of people 65 and older were in the workforce in February, up from an all-time low of 10% in January 1985, according to money manager United Income.

**译文：** 资金管理公司联合收入的数据显示，今年2月，65岁及以上人群中约20%仍在工作，高于1985年1月创下的10%的历史最低点。

## 15

Because of longer life spans, Americans are also boosting their savings to preserve their nest eggs, the TD Ameritrade study showed, which surveyed 2,000 adults between 40 to 79.

**译文：** 德美利证券的研究还表明，由于寿命延长，美国人正在增加储蓄以保住自己的养老积蓄；该研究调查了2000名40至79岁的成年人。

## 16

Six in 10 "unretirees" are increasing their savings in anticipation of a longer life, according to the survey.

**译文：** 调查显示，每十名“退而不休者”中有六人正增加储蓄，为更长的寿命做准备。

## 17

Among the most popular ways they are doing this, the company said, is by reducing their overall expenses, securing life insurance or maximizing their contributions to retirement accounts.

**译文：** 该公司表示，他们最常采用的做法包括减少总体开支、购买人寿保险，或尽可能提高退休账户的缴款额。

## 18

Unfortunately, many people who are opting to work in retirement are preparing to do so because they are worried about making ends meet in their later years, said Brent Weiss, a co-founder at Baltimore-based financial-planning firm Facet Wealth.

**译文：** 位于巴尔的摩的理财规划公司Facet Wealth联合创始人布伦特·韦斯说，遗憾的是，许多选择退休后继续工作的人之所以作此准备，是因为担心晚年入不敷出。

## 19

He suggested that preretirees should speak with a financial adviser to set long-term financial goals.

**译文：** 他建议，退休前的人应当与理财顾问沟通，设定长期财务目标。

## 20

"The most challenging moments in life are getting married, starting a family and ultimately retiring," Weiss said.

**译文：** “人生中最具挑战性的时刻，是结婚、组建家庭以及最终退休，”韦斯说。

## 21

"It's not just a financial decision, but an emotional one.

**译文：** “这不仅是一个财务决定，也是一个情感决定。

## 22

Many people believe they can't retire."

**译文：** 许多人认为自己根本退不了休。”

> 译文为个人学习整理，不冒充考试机构标准答案。
