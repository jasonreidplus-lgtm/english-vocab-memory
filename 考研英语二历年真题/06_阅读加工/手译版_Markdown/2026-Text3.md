# 2026 年考研英语二阅读 Text 3 · 逐句手译

## 1

Since the 2008 launch of the high-speed rail network between Rome and Milan, trains have become the preferred means of travel across Italy for locals and tourists alike.

**译文：** 自2008年罗马至米兰高速铁路网开通以来，火车已经成为当地居民和游客在意大利各地出行的首选方式。

## 2

Fast trains can cover the 500km between the two cities in three hours.

**译文：** 高速列车可在三小时内跑完两座城市之间的500公里。

## 3

The network also connects Naples, Bologna, Florence and Turin.

**译文：** 该铁路网还连接那不勒斯、博洛尼亚、佛罗伦萨和都灵。

## 4

In many cases, it is the best option - in terms of travel time and cost - for both leisure and business travellers.

**译文：** 在许多情况下，无论休闲还是商务出行，从时间和费用来看，高铁都是最佳选择。

## 5

This is why the recent severe delays caused by numerous maintenance works have thrown the rail industry into chaos at a time when most people head on holiday, which matters for business in a country where tourism accounts for 10 percent of GDP.

**译文：** 因此，近期大量维修工程造成的严重延误，在大多数人外出度假之际让铁路行业陷入混乱；而在一个旅游业占国内生产总值10%的国家，这会影响商业。

## 6

Disruptions have become increasingly frequent with train delays now a fixture in Italian media coverage.

**译文：** 运行中断越来越频繁，列车延误如今已成为意大利媒体报道的固定内容。

## 7

Ferrovie dello Stato Italiane, the publicly controlled group that owns train operator Trenitalia and the national train network RFI, said 23 percent of all high-speed trains it operated were late in 2023.

**译文：** 意大利国家铁路公司是一家受公共控制的集团，旗下拥有列车运营商Trenitalia和国家铁路网运营商RFI；该集团表示，2023年其运营的所有高速列车中有23%晚点。

## 8

The huge improvements in Italy's obsolete infrastructure partly explained the disruption, said Ferrovie.

**译文：** 意大利国家铁路公司称，对意大利老旧基础设施进行的大规模改进，在一定程度上造成了这些运行中断。

## 9

RFI, the network operator, is the single largest European recovery fund beneficiary with planned investments of €24bn by 2026.

**译文：** 铁路网运营商RFI是欧洲复苏基金最大的单一受益者，计划到2026年投入240亿欧元。

## 10

Ferrovie will invest a total of €124bn in infrastructure over the next 10 years.

**译文：** 意大利国家铁路公司将在未来十年向基础设施投入总计1,240亿欧元。

## 11

But lack of capacity is another problem.

**译文：** 但运力不足是另一个问题。

## 12

Transport economist Andrea Giuricin said the planned investments would bring improvements but disruptions in the meantime were inevitable.

**译文：** 交通经济学家安德烈亚·朱里钦说，计划中的投资将带来改善，但在此期间出现运行中断不可避免。

## 13

"We have a mixed system, there is no spare capacity and as soon as there's a hiccup on the line, the situation becomes highly complex," he said.

**译文：** 他说："我们采用混合系统，没有备用运力；线路上一有小故障，情况就会变得极其复杂。"

## 14

A mixed system means that high-speed trains must travel on the regular tracks on certain routes or when passing through large cities.

**译文：** 所谓混合系统，是指高速列车在某些线路或穿越大城市时，必须在普通铁路轨道上行驶。

## 15

If there's congestion or, for example, a local train breaks down, the entire high-speed network is affected.

**译文：** 一旦发生拥堵，或者比如一列普通列车出现故障，整个高速铁路网都会受到影响。

## 16

One of the most important changes the upgrades will bring is the segregation of the high-speed line from the regular one in certain urban centres through the construction of underground rail links.

**译文：** 升级将带来的最重要变化之一，是通过修建地下铁路连接线，在某些城市中心将高铁线路与普通线路分离。

## 17

Improvements will also come from the high-density technology and satellite signalling that RFI has been investing in for years, say experts.

**译文：** 专家表示，RFI多年来投资的高密度技术和卫星信号系统也将带来改善。

## 18

It will allow for a reduction of the distance between high-speed trains travelling on the same line at any given time, which should mean a significant increase in capacity and traffic fluidity, according to Giuricin.

**译文：** 朱里钦说，这将缩短同一时刻在同一线路上运行的高速列车之间所需保持的间隔，从而应能显著提高运力和运行流畅度。
