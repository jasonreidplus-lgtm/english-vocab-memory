# 2026 年考研英语二阅读 Text 2 · 逐句手译

## 1

According to our research, around one in five workers in the UK talk to AI like a friend, looking for guidance on personal and professional problems.

**译文：** 我们的研究显示，英国约五分之一的劳动者会像和朋友交谈一样同人工智能对话，向它寻求个人和职业问题的指导。

## 2

Our data shows that engaging with AI like this can leave us feeling heard and less isolated.

**译文：** 我们的数据表明，以这种方式与人工智能互动，能让我们觉得自己的声音被听见，也不再那么孤独。

## 3

But, with this newfound connection, many of us share sensitive, sometimes highly confidential information, even though over a third of people don't realise that AI platforms may not be very good at keeping our secrets...secret.

**译文：** 然而，伴随这种新建立的联系，我们中许多人会分享敏感、有时甚至高度机密的信息，尽管超过三分之一的人没有意识到，人工智能平台也许并不很擅长把我们的秘密……守成秘密。

## 4

For businesses, the implications are worrying.

**译文：** 对企业来说，这些影响令人担忧。

## 5

Consider Microsoft Copilot, for example.

**译文：** 以微软Copilot为例。

## 6

It gains Microsoft broad rights to the data inputted or outputted by any user - rights to use this data in any way it sees fit; it can even share it with third parties.

**译文：** 它让微软对任何用户输入或输出的数据拥有广泛权利——微软可以按自己认为合适的任何方式使用这些数据，甚至可以与第三方分享。

## 7

This means that any sensitive business information could potentially be exposed to the world.

**译文：** 这意味着任何敏感的商业信息都有可能暴露给全世界。

## 8

Employers are taking note and taking action.

**译文：** 雇主已经注意到这一点，并开始采取行动。

## 9

According to our research, 25% have decided to either outright ban AI or regulate its use within their organisations.

**译文：** 我们的研究显示，25%的雇主已经决定要么彻底禁止人工智能，要么对其在组织内部的使用加以规范。

## 10

But even with these policies in place, some employees choose to break the rules.

**译文：** 但即使制定了这些政策，一些员工仍会选择违规。

## 11

They have their reasons - around 63% of them report that using AI increases their productivity, and some even feel AI offers more help than their human colleagues.

**译文：** 他们自有理由：约63%的人表示，使用人工智能提高了自己的生产率；有些人甚至觉得，人工智能比人类同事提供的帮助更多。

## 12

The situation presents a delicate balance between leveraging AI for its productivity gains and risking confidential data exposure.

**译文：** 这形成了一种微妙的平衡：一边要利用人工智能带来的生产率提升，一边又要承担机密数据泄露的风险。

## 13

Employers need to manage AI tools with the same level of care as any other form of data sharing or storage.

**译文：** 雇主需要像管理其他任何形式的数据分享或存储一样谨慎地管理人工智能工具。

## 14

There's also a knowledge gap that needs to be addressed - 40% of individuals surveyed are unsure about who retains ownership over the content produced by AI.

**译文：** 此外，还有一个亟待弥合的知识缺口：40%的受访者不确定人工智能生成内容的所有权究竟归谁。

## 15

By instilling best practices in AI engagement and creating policies that evolve with the technology, businesses can positively shift the AI landscape.

**译文：** 通过确立与人工智能互动的最佳做法，并制定随技术演进而更新的政策，企业可以推动人工智能生态朝积极方向转变。

## 16

With AI's potential to simplify our professional lives, do the admin and enhance the work experience, it's up to us to navigate its usage cautiously so it supports us without compromising our privacy.

**译文：** 人工智能有潜力简化我们的职业生活、处理行政事务并改善工作体验，因此我们有责任谨慎把握其使用方式，让它在不损害隐私的前提下为我们提供支持。

## 17

There's no need to back away from progress, as long as we're equipped with the knowledge and tools to make sure AI remains a friend.

**译文：** 只要我们具备相应知识和工具，能够确保人工智能始终是朋友，就没有必要拒绝进步。

## 18

And this is where the challenge lies.

**译文：** 而挑战恰恰就在这里。

## 19

There are so many products out there, being promoted with huge advertising and marketing budgets, that it's easy to fall victim.

**译文：** 市面上的产品如此之多，又有巨额广告和营销预算为其推广，人们很容易上当受骗。

## 20

But by building a culture of digital responsibility within our businesses, we can create a future where AI can help us without spilling the beans.

**译文：** 但只要在企业内部营造数字责任文化，我们就能创造这样一个未来：人工智能可以帮助我们，同时不泄露秘密。
