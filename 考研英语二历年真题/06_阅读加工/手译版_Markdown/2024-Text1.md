# 2024 年考研英语二阅读 Text 1 · 逐句手译

## 1

In her new book Cogs and Monsters: What Economics Is, and What It Should Be, Diane Coyle, an economist at Cambridge University, argues that the digital economy requires new ways of thinking about progress.

**译文：** 剑桥大学经济学家黛安·科伊尔在新书《齿轮与怪物：经济学是什么，又应当是什么》中主张，数字经济要求我们以新的方式思考进步。

## 2

"Whatever we mean by the economy growing, by things getting better, the gains will have to be more evenly shared than in the recent past," she writes.

**译文：** 她写道："无论我们所说的经济增长、事物变好究竟意味着什么，成果都必须比不久前更加公平地由人们共享。"

## 3

"An economy of tech millionaires or billionaires and gig workers, with middle-income jobs undercut by automation, will not be politically sustainable."

**译文：** "一种由科技界百万富翁或亿万富翁和零工劳动者构成、而中等收入岗位又被自动化削弱的经济，在政治上是不可持续的。"

## 4

Improving living standards and increasing prosperity for more people will require greater use of digital technologies to boost productivity in various sectors, including health care and construction, says Coyle.

**译文：** 科伊尔表示，要提高生活水平并让更多人日益富足，就需要更广泛地运用数字技术，提高包括医疗保健和建筑业在内的各行业生产率。

## 5

But people can't be expected to embrace the changes if they're not seeing the benefits - if they're just seeing good jobs being destroyed.

**译文：** 但如果人们看不到变化带来的好处，只看到好工作被摧毁，就不能指望他们接受这些变化。

## 6

In a recent interview, Coyle said she fears that tech's inequality problem could be a roadblock to deploying AI.

**译文：** 科伊尔在最近一次采访中说，她担心科技行业的不平等问题可能成为部署人工智能的障碍。

## 7

"We're talking about disruption," she says.

**译文：** 她说："我们谈的是颠覆。"

## 8

"These are transformative technologies that change the ways we spend our time every day, that change business models that succeed."

**译文：** "这些变革性技术会改变我们每天支配时间的方式，也会改变哪些商业模式能够成功。"

## 9

To make such "tremendous changes," she adds, you need social buy-in.

**译文：** 她补充说，要实现这种"巨大变革"，就需要获得社会认同。

## 10

Instead, says Coyle, resentment is simmering among many as the benefits are perceived to go to elites in a handful of prosperous cities.

**译文：** 科伊尔说，现实却是，许多人认为收益都流向了少数繁荣城市中的精英，怨愤正在他们心中积聚。

## 11

According to the Brookings Institution, a short list of eight American cities that included San Francisco, San Jose, Boston, and Seattle had roughly 38% of all tech jobs by 2019.

**译文：** 据布鲁金斯学会统计，截至2019年，包括旧金山、圣何塞、波士顿和西雅图在内的八座美国城市大约占据了全美38%的科技岗位。

## 12

New AI technologies are particularly concentrated: Brookings's Mark Muro and Sifan Liu estimate that just 15 cities account for two-thirds of the AI assets and capabilities in the United States.

**译文：** 新的人工智能技术分布得尤其集中：布鲁金斯学会的马克·穆罗和刘思凡估计，仅15座城市就占有美国三分之二的人工智能资产与能力。

## 13

The dominance of a few cities in the invention and commercialization of AI means that geographical disparities in wealth will continue to soar.

**译文：** 少数城市主导人工智能的发明和商业化，意味着地域之间的财富差距将继续急剧扩大。

## 14

Not only will this foster political and social unrest, but it could, as Coyle suggests, hold back the sorts of AI technologies needed for regional economies to grow.

**译文：** 这不仅会滋生政治和社会动荡，而且正如科伊尔所说，还可能阻碍那些为地区经济增长所需的人工智能技术。

## 15

Part of the solution could lie in somehow loosening the stranglehold that Big Tech has on defining the AI agenda.

**译文：** 部分解决办法或许在于，以某种方式削弱大型科技公司对人工智能议程定义权的牢牢控制。

## 16

That will likely take increased federal funding for research independent of the tech giants.

**译文：** 这很可能需要增加联邦资金，支持独立于科技巨头的研究。

## 17

A more immediate response is to broaden our digital imaginations to conceive of AI technologies that don't simply replace jobs but expand opportunities in the sectors that different parts of the country care most about, like health care, education, and manufacturing.

**译文：** 更直接的应对办法，是拓展我们对数字技术的想象，构想出不只是取代岗位、而是能在各地最重视的医疗、教育和制造等行业中扩大机会的人工智能技术。
