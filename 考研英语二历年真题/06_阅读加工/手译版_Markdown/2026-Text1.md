# 2026 年考研英语二阅读 Text 1 · 逐句手译

## 1

Ask people about public libraries and a certain image springs to mind: dusty, old-fashioned, the sort of place you enjoyed as a child but, rather like a British seaside town, would you go there now?

**译文：** 向人们提起公共图书馆，脑海中往往会浮现某种形象：尘土飞扬、陈旧过时，是小时候喜欢去的那种地方；但它又有点像英国海滨小镇——你现在还会去吗？

## 2

And anyway - aren't they all closing?

**译文：** 况且，它们不是都在关闭吗？

## 3

The reality is startlingly different, as I discovered when the culture department commissioned me to conduct an independent review of English public libraries, published yesterday.

**译文：** 然而事实截然不同，甚至令人吃惊；文化部门委托我对英格兰公共图书馆开展独立评审时，我发现了这一点，评审报告已于昨日发布。

## 4

As I visited libraries up and down the country, I was surprised to learn there are more than twice the number of libraries (2,892) as there are branches of McDonald's.

**译文：** 我走访全国各地的图书馆后惊讶地得知，图书馆共有2,892家，数量是麦当劳门店的两倍多。

## 5

Enter any one of them and you will find a hive of activity.

**译文：** 走进其中任何一家，你都会看到一派繁忙景象。

## 6

While books are, and should always be, at the heart of any library, a multitude of other services are offered: employment advice, language classes or digital access and support.

**译文：** 书籍现在是、也永远应该是每家图书馆的核心，但图书馆还提供大量其他服务，包括就业建议、语言课程以及数字接入与支持。

## 7

There are libraries with business and intellectual property centres, which can help business owners and entrepreneurs.

**译文：** 有些图书馆设有商业与知识产权中心，可以帮助企业主和创业者。

## 8

Many have nurses on site to carry out basic health checks, with a link to the GP's surgery.

**译文：** 许多图书馆有护士驻点开展基础健康检查，并与全科医生诊所相连。

## 9

There are libraries where young people can borrow a Fifa-standard football free.

**译文：** 有些图书馆还允许年轻人免费借用达到国际足联标准的足球。

## 10

In return for all of this, you'll be asked for precisely nothing.

**译文：** 作为这些服务的回报，你所需支付的恰恰是什么也没有。

## 11

There will be no charge and you will never be asked to justify or explain yourself; you will simply be welcomed in, offered help if you need it, and left alone if you do not.

**译文：** 这里不收取任何费用，也绝不会要求你说明或解释自己；你只会受到欢迎，需要时有人帮助，不需要时则可以不受打扰。

## 12

There is no other institution, public or private, that can say the same.

**译文：** 无论公共还是私人机构，没有其他任何机构能作出同样的承诺。

## 13

Yet still our libraries are often overlooked and underappreciated.

**译文：** 可即便如此，我们的图书馆仍常常被忽视和低估。

## 14

There is an overall decline in visits and many are struggling as local authorities come under continued financial pressure.

**译文：** 到访图书馆的总人次正在下降，而地方政府持续承受财政压力，许多图书馆也举步维艰。

## 15

The number of libraries that have closed since 2010 is disputed.

**译文：** 自2010年以来究竟关闭了多少家图书馆，各方说法不一。

## 16

An annual survey by the Chartered Institute of Public Finance and Accountancy puts it at nearly 800 across the UK; official statistics held by the Arts Council record 230 in England.

**译文：** 英国特许公共财政与会计协会的一项年度调查认为，全英关闭数量接近800家；英格兰艺术委员会掌握的官方统计数据则记录了英格兰关闭230家。

## 17

It is, of course, 230 libraries too many.

**译文：** 当然，哪怕230家也多得令人无法接受。

## 18

So if we are to protect our libraries for future generations, we must raise awareness of them and the work they do.

**译文：** 因此，如果要为后代保护图书馆，我们就必须提高人们对图书馆及其工作的认识。

## 19

The review recommends a national branding campaign to give libraries a stronger physical presence, the reintroduction of a scheme to enable members to use their card in any library in the country, and automatic memberships for children.

**译文：** 评审报告建议开展全国统一品牌宣传，让图书馆在现实空间中更加醒目；恢复一项允许会员凭同一张卡使用全国任何图书馆的计划；并为儿童自动办理会员资格。

## 20

If we don't use them, we will end up losing them.

**译文：** 如果我们不使用图书馆，最终就会失去它们。

## 21

And for those who haven't been to a library for some time, they may be surprised by what they find.

**译文：** 而那些已经有一段时间没去过图书馆的人，也许会对自己看到的一切感到惊喜。
