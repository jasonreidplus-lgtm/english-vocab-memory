# 2020 年考研英语二阅读 Text 1 · 逐句手译

## 1

Rats and other animals need to be highly attuned to social signals from others so they can identify friends to cooperate with and enemies to avoid.

**译文：** 老鼠和其他动物需要高度敏锐地感知同伴发出的社会信号，以便识别可以合作的朋友和应当躲避的敌人。

## 2

To find out if this extends to non-living beings, Laleh Quinn at the University of California, San Diego, and her colleagues tested whether rats can detect social signals from robotic rats.

**译文：** 为弄清这种能力是否延伸到非生命体，加利福尼亚大学圣迭戈分校的拉莱·奎因及其同事测试了老鼠能否识别机器鼠发出的社会信号。

## 3

They housed eight adult rats with two types of robotic rat - one social and one asocial - for four days.

**译文：** 他们让八只成年鼠与两类机器鼠共同生活四天，一类具有社会性，另一类不具有社会性。

## 4

The robot rats were quite minimalist, resembling a chunkier version of a computer mouse with wheels to move around and colorful markings.

**译文：** 这些机器鼠的设计非常简约，像是更粗壮的电脑鼠标，装有用于移动的轮子和彩色标记。

## 5

During the experiment, the social robot rat followed the living rats around, played with the same toys, and opened cage doors to let trapped rats escape.

**译文：** 实验期间，社会型机器鼠会跟随活鼠四处活动、玩同样的玩具，还会打开笼门，让被困的老鼠逃脱。

## 6

Meanwhile, the asocial robot simply moved forwards and backwards and side to side.

**译文：** 与此同时，非社会型机器鼠只是前后左右地移动。

## 7

Next, the researchers trapped the robots in cages and gave the rats the opportunity to release them by pressing a lever.

**译文：** 接着，研究人员把机器鼠困在笼中，让活鼠有机会通过按压杠杆将其放出。

## 8

Across 18 trials each, the living rats were 52 per cent more likely on average to set the social robot free than the asocial one.

**译文：** 在两种情形各18次试验中，活鼠释放社会型机器鼠的平均可能性比释放非社会型机器鼠高52%。

## 9

This suggests that the rats perceived the social robot as a genuine social being, says Quinn.

**译文：** 奎因说，这表明老鼠把社会型机器鼠当成了真正的社会性生命。

## 10

The rats may have bonded more with the social robot because it displayed behaviors like communal exploring and playing.

**译文：** 活鼠可能与社会型机器鼠建立了更深的联系，因为后者会表现出共同探索和玩耍等行为。

## 11

This could lead to the rats better remembering having freed it earlier, and wanting the robot to return the favour when they get trapped, she says.

**译文：** 她说，这可能使老鼠更清楚地记得自己之前曾释放过它，并希望自己受困时，机器鼠也能回报这一帮助。

## 12

"Rats have been shown to engage in multiple forms of reciprocal help and cooperation, including what is referred to as direct reciprocity where a rat will help another rat that has previously helped them," says Quinn.

**译文：** 奎因说：“研究已经证明，老鼠会采取多种形式的互助与合作，其中包括所谓的直接互惠，即一只老鼠会帮助此前帮助过自己的另一只老鼠。”

## 13

The readiness of the rats to befriend the social robot was surprising given its minimal design.

**译文：** 考虑到这种机器鼠设计极其简单，活鼠愿意与社会型机器鼠交朋友这一点令人惊讶。

## 14

The robot was the same size as a regular rat but resembled a simple plastic box on wheels.

**译文：** 机器鼠与普通老鼠大小相同，但外形像一个装有轮子的简单塑料盒。

## 15

"We'd assumed we'd have to give it a moving head and tail, facial features, and put a scent on it to make it smell like a real rat, but that wasn't necessary," says Janet Wiles at the University of Queensland in Australia, who helped with the research.

**译文：** 参与这项研究的澳大利亚昆士兰大学学者珍妮特·怀尔斯说：“我们原以为必须给它装上会动的头和尾巴、面部特征，再加上气味，让它闻起来像真老鼠，但事实证明没有必要。”

## 16

The finding shows how sensitive rats are to social cues, even when they come from basic robots, says Wiles.

**译文：** 怀尔斯说，这一发现显示了老鼠对社会线索有多么敏感，即便这些线索来自结构简单的机器人。

## 17

Similarly, children tend to treat robots as if they are fellow beings, even when they display only simple social signals.

**译文：** 同样，儿童往往会把机器人当作同类，即便机器人只表现出简单的社会信号。

## 18

"We humans seem to be fascinated by robots, and it turns out other animals are too," says Wiles.

**译文：** 怀尔斯说：“我们人类似乎会被机器人吸引，事实证明其他动物也是如此。”
